## Thematic structures

**The recursive trap:** Addiction caused brain injury (hypoxia from OD). Injury made escape harder. That loop is the structure of the book.

**The diagnostic instinct:** Reading rooms at O'Toole's → reading processes in QC → reading systems in engineering. Survived cognitive disruption. Not a metaphor — a literal cognitive pattern that transferred.

**Construction over Recovery:** The thesis. Not stated in the book — demonstrated by what the narrator builds. The reader should arrive at it without being told.

**Memory as material:** "I don't remember if it was 19 or 21" is a sentence, not a gap to fill. The AMEN eval's Processing Speed vs. Recall Memory asymmetry is literally the brain that wrote this book.

**External scaffolding:** Tools, systems, documentation — prosthetic cognition. The book's central irony: the systems multiplied until they became their own burden.

## What the book is not (keep this visible)
- A recovery memoir
- A sobriety arc
- "Brave battle with addiction and TBI"
- A cautionary tale

## What the book is
A letter to his daughter. An account of building a self from non-standard materials when the standard materials are unavailable. Literary nonfiction. Specific. Declarative. Honest about what was built and what wasn't.
