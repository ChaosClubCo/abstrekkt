IGNITABLE CANDOR 
## Genre / Lane
Cinematic hip-hop · dark boom bap / industrial edge · aggressive, mythic

## Structure
Intro → Verse 1 → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Verse 2 → Bridge (beat switch) → Final Chorus → Outro

---

### [INTRO]
Welcome to my humble an’ almighty imperial palace,  
Old fashioned, ascribed status—ghosts in the ballast.

---

### [VERSE 1]
Flyin’ into the nebula, phantom menace cadence,  
My Alcatraz foundation—keeps you festerin’, waitin’.  
Busta bites, Twista tight, tech-laced licks,  
Musically inject my life in your head—quick fix.  
Metaphysical words embodied to life,  
Frankenstein’s divided, personified divine.  
Ink ends in hour-long standin’ ovations,  
Conscripts say his—finale’s a spectacular rift, I’m sayin’.

---

### [PRE-CHORUS]
Bayonets fixed, locked an’ loaded, encoded,  
Words in tandem slurrin’ tantrums—you quoted.  
Carbon-coated mines in packed view loaders,  
Javelin fire hoses—dually noted.

---

### [CHORUS]
Ignitable candor, strike when I speak,  
Green with a little anxiety—that’s exactly what you need.  
Ignitable candor, spark in the dark,  
I don’t whisper truth—I leave a permanent mark.

---

### [VERSE 2]
ACME sarcastic, Road Runner fast,  
Anvils drop heavier than Wile E. Coyote’s past.  
Hazardous tests, peyote, cactus rollin’,  
Only your lookin’ pokey—Gumby hokey.  
Kenobi told the class to come to their senses,  
Killed the fogey fanatic—saber zingin’ in sentences.  
Highest midichlorians grafted—rancor fightin’,  
Notorious writin’—Florian’s chest shinin’.

---

### [BRIDGE] *(beat switch / half-time)*
Reality of your imagination,  
Rhetoric playground—no gravestone placement.  
Safety in the game of graveyard rules,  
I still play hard—break molds, don’t soothe.

---

### [FINAL CHORUS]
Ignitable candor, strike when I speak,  
Green with a little anxiety—that’s exactly what you need.  
Ignitable candor, spark in the dark,  
I don’t whisper truth—I leave a permanent mark.

---

### [OUTRO]
Strike the match… candor.
