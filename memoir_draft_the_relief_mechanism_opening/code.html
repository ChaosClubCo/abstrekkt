The Relief Mechanism

No one tells you that relief is the most addictive feeling of all.

It wasn't a rebellion. Rebellion implies energy, a pushing outward against a defined structure. This was the opposite. This was the sudden, total collapse of structural integrity—a surrender to a framework that finally, quietly, permitted a system failure.

We were on a nondescript couch. The geometry of the room is lost, replaced only by the spatial context of his voice. "You don't have to." Four words that bypassed the executive function and landed directly in the substrate.

For a 99th percentile processing engine, generating constant internal static, the sudden, profound silence of that first atmospheric zero was not just a sensation. It was an entirely new physics.

The 'Noise' stopped. The endless loop of anticipatory anxiety, the modeling of every social outcome, the heavy, vibrating pressure of maintaining the mask—it all just spun down. The drug wasn't the chemical; the chemical was merely the delivery vehicle for the permission.
