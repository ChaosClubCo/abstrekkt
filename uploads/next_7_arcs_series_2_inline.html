The next seven arcs.

After the foundational thirteen — platform depth, business formation, developer ecosystem, security, knowledge layer, workflow ops, brand, teaching — these are the next layer. Two infrastructure pre-reqs, two product-vs-automation gaps, two discipline deepeners, one leverage play.

Sequencing notes

  • GCP and Azure Foundry are pre-reqs for arcs already built. GCP feeds the Gemini arc's M11 cert. Azure Foundry deepens the Copilot arc's Head-of-AIaaS positioning. Build these before the arcs they support, not after.
  • Two HIGHs do not run in parallel. GCP and Full-Stack are both HIGH-load. Running them simultaneously alongside existing technical arcs and an exam window collides on weeks 4–8.
  • Three are LOW or MED and slot in beside almost anything. Cloudflare Edge, AI Product Strategy, and Agentic Evaluation produce different leverage — infrastructure, advisory, and quality discipline.
  • Voice & Realtime has the only real market window. Text-to-voice is the interaction shift. Build it within nine months or the early-mover advantage compresses.
