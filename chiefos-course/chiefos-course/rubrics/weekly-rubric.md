# Weekly Rep Rubric

Applied to each week's `CHECK.md` submission. 4 points per week.

| Score | Meaning | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 4 — Full | Did the reps AND understands them | Output shows real practice; written explanation is correct and in own words |
| 3 — Solid | Did the reps, minor gaps in understanding | Works, but explanation is thin or one concept fuzzy |
| 2 — Partial | Incomplete reps or copied without understanding | Ran commands but can't explain them; missing pieces |
| 1 — Attempted | Started, didn't finish, little understanding | Fragments, no explanation |
| 0 — Missing | No submission | — |

## The understanding test (how to score 4 vs 2)
The difference between a 4 and a 2 is almost always the **written/verbal explanation**, not
the commands. Commands can be copied. Understanding can't. When in doubt, ask the student to
explain one step out loud. If they can, it's a 4. If they recite without meaning, it's a 2 —
and that's the moment to intervene, because it's the vibe-coder pattern forming.

## Anti-pattern flags (auto-drop a level)
- Commit messages all "update"/"asdf" → they didn't internalize *why*.
- Pasted AI output declared correct without testing → prompt-and-pray.
- Skipped the "explain in your own words" prompt → the actual assessment.
