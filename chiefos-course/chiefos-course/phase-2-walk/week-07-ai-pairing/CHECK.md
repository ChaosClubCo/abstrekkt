## Instructor grading note
- Full: student found a genuine edge-case weakness and treated AI output as reviewable, not final.
- Redo: pasted AI output and declared it correct without testing — that's the exact habit this week exists to break. Give them the `a@b` case and ask if it's really a valid email.

**FSR skill proved:** You can use AI to move fast in the field WITHOUT shipping its mistakes to the customer.
