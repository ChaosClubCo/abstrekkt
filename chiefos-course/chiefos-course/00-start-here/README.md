# Start Here

Welcome. Before Week 1, do three things. Total time: ~45 minutes.

## 1. Read the syllabus
[`SYLLABUS.md`](SYLLABUS.md) — 5 minutes. Know the map before you walk it.

## 2. Set up your machine
[`SETUP.md`](SETUP.md) — install the tools. You do **not** need to understand them yet.
Week 1 explains everything. Right now, just get them installed.

## 3. Take the "floor check"
[`FLOOR-CHECK.md`](FLOOR-CHECK.md) — a 10-minute, ungraded self-test so *you* know your
starting point. This is not a filter. 66% of your classmates have never built an app.
You are in exactly the right place.

---

## The one rule

**Do the weeks in order.** The survey proved this cohort skips fundamentals and stalls.
If you already know Git, Week 3 will take you 20 minutes — do it anyway and help a neighbor.
Teaching someone else is the fastest way to lock in a skill (and it's an FSR job requirement).

---

## The mindset shift (read this twice)

Most people using AI to code are stuck at **"prompt and pray"**: they ask the AI for code,
paste it, and hope. When it breaks, they're helpless because they can't read what the AI wrote.

You are learning the opposite: **"direct and verify."** You will learn enough of the
fundamentals that AI becomes a fast assistant you supervise — not a black box you obey.

That difference is the entire course.
