TRUST ME

[Intro — fragmented, almost off-beat]

You ever notice—
how memory…

doesn’t argue…

…it edits

[Verse 1 — controlled → slipping]

Spoke in a loop—my thoughts rotate
same lane, same frame, same “was it fate?”

Same sentence bent when I say it again
same truth shift shape when it hits your lens

I said what I said—
then questioned it twice

then checked your face
like you held the knife

But you didn’t—
…or maybe you did

I rewrote the moment
before it could live

[Collapse Hook — NOT an anthem, intrusive thought]

Don’t trust me either
I bend what I see

If I stay in this long enough
it rewrites me

Don’t trust me either
I’m part of it too

Truth don’t stay still
when it passes through you

[Verse 2 — sharper, self-accountability hits]

I caught myself fillin in blanks you left
manufactured meaning just to make it make sense

That’s on me—
not just you

I needed a reason
so I made one true

Spun every silence into hidden intent
read every pause like a coded event

But not everything’s deeper—
some shit just exists

and I twisted it up
till it felt like this

[Bridge — fast, unraveling cadence]

Hyperdrive head—no brakes in the system
pattern addiction—predictive condition

loopin conclusions, confusion intrusion
proof gets removed when the doubt gets permission

I don’t remember—
I reconstruct

then defend that version
like it’s the one

[Breakdown — heavy, stripped]

Say it again…

…what did you say?

No—
say it EXACTLY how you said it

[Final Hook — more desperate, louder]

Don’t trust me either
I bend what I see

If I stay in this long enough
it rewrites me

Don’t trust me either
I’m part of it too

Truth don’t stay still
when it passes through you

[Outro — quiet, unsettling]

I was there…

…I think
