Why Claude Desktop

Three reasons the work concentrates here.

MCP is a real protocol. Not an SDK lock-in, not a webhook patchwork. Any service that can speak it becomes a first-class tool in the model's context. The cost of adding capability is the cost of writing one small server.

Skills make domain knowledge portable. A well-written skill is readable by a human and executable by a model. The same artifact trains new hires, documents the process, and teaches the AI.

The surface is composable. Plugins, skills, MCP servers, and sub-agents compose. Solutions get smaller and more specific, not bigger and more general.
