## Authoring rules (delete before publishing)

- **No fabricated metrics.** If you don't have a number, don't put a number. Qualitative wins are fine.
- **Get explicit permission before naming the client.** Default to "an enterprise SaaS company" or similar until consent confirmed in writing.
- **Avoid vendor logos until the client confirms.** Some clients have legal that forbids public attribution.
- **No screenshots of client UI.** Use diagrams, mockups, or generic UI references unless explicitly cleared.
- **No mention of internal tooling, MSP, or "Intinc"** in any case study, ever. FlashFusion is the brand. Period.
- **Word count target:** 600-900. Anything longer loses the reader.
- **Reading level target:** Plain. A 25-year-old PM should follow it. A 50-year-old CTO should respect it.
