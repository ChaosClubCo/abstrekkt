# Event Management

## Overview

Events are high-touch opportunities to build relationships, demonstrate expertise, and generate leads. For SMBs, well-executed events can create outsized impact relative to investment, whether hosting webinars, sponsoring conferences, or exhibiting at trade shows. This skill covers the complete event lifecycle from planning through post-event conversion.

Strategic event management balances brand awareness goals with lead generation objectives, creating memorable experiences that move prospects through the funnel while strengthening relationships with existing customers.

## When to Use

**Invoke this skill when:**
- Planning a webinar or virtual event
- Preparing for trade show or conference participation
- Organizing customer or partner conferences
- Coordinating speaker logistics and content
- Building event promotion strategies
- Managing registration and attendee experience
- Developing post-event follow-up sequences
- Calculating event ROI and optimization

**Trigger phrases:**
- "Plan a webinar"
- "Prepare for trade show"
- "Event logistics checklist"
- "Attendee management"
- "Post-event follow-up"
- "Virtual event platform"

#### Webinar Strategy Framework

```markdown
## Webinar Planning Template

### Event Definition
- **Title**: [Compelling, benefit-driven title]
- **Date/Time**: [Day, Date at Time Timezone]
- **Duration**: [45-60 minutes typical]
- **Format**: [Presentation, panel, demo, workshop, AMA]
- **Target Audience**: [Specific persona and stage]

### Goals & Success Metrics
Primary Goal: [Lead generation / Education / Product demo / Thought leadership]

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Registrations | [X] | Registration platform |
| Attendance rate | 40-50% | Attendees/Registrations |
| Engagement rate | >60% | Polls, Q&A, chat participation |
| Lead quality | [X]% qualified | Post-event scoring |
| Pipeline generated | $[X] | CRM attribution |

### Content Planning
**Working title options:**
1. [How to {outcome} without {pain point}]
2. [{Number} {strategies/mistakes/secrets} for {outcome}]
3. [The {adjective} guide to {topic}]

**Key takeaways (3-5):**
1. [What they'll learn/be able to do]
2. [What they'll learn/be able to do]
3. [What they'll learn/be able to do]

**Content outline:**
- 0-5 min: Welcome, housekeeping, speaker intro
- 5-10 min: Context setting, why this matters now
- 10-35 min: Main content (3-4 sections)
- 35-45 min: Demo/case study (if applicable)
- 45-55 min: Q&A
- 55-60 min: Wrap-up, CTA, next steps
```

#### Webinar Promotion Timeline

```markdown
## Webinar Promotion Calendar

### 4 Weeks Before
- [ ] Create landing page and registration form
- [ ] Write email invitation copy (3 versions)
- [ ] Design social graphics (announcement, countdown, speaker)
- [ ] Brief sales team on webinar and registration link
- [ ] Schedule paid promotion if budget allows

### 3 Weeks Before
- [ ] Send Email #1: Announcement to full list
- [ ] Social post #1: Announcement with registration link
- [ ] Publish blog post about webinar topic
- [ ] Reach out to partners for cross-promotion

### 2 Weeks Before
- [ ] Send Email #2: Value-focused reminder
- [ ] Social post #2: Speaker/content highlight
- [ ] Sales team personalized outreach to key accounts
- [ ] Confirm speaker logistics and content

### 1 Week Before
- [ ] Send Email #3: Final call to register
- [ ] Social post #3: Countdown/urgency
- [ ] Technical rehearsal with speakers
- [ ] Send calendar reminder to registered attendees

### Day Before
- [ ] Send reminder email to registrants
- [ ] Social post #4: "Tomorrow" reminder
- [ ] Final tech check

### Day Of
- [ ] Send 1-hour reminder email
- [ ] Social post #5: "Starting soon" / "Join now"
- [ ] Go live 10-15 minutes early for tech check

### Post-Event
- [ ] Send recording to all registrants (attendees + no-shows)
- [ ] Social post #6: Thank you + recording link
- [ ] Begin follow-up sequence
- [ ] Debrief and analyze results
```

#### Booth Design & Staffing

```markdown
## Booth Strategy

### Booth Design Principles
1. **Stop traffic**: Eye-catching visual from 20+ feet
2. **Communicate quickly**: Key message visible in 3 seconds
3. **Enable conversation**: Open design, no barriers
4. **Demonstrate product**: Interactive demo station
5. **Capture leads**: Easy scan/entry point

### Booth Size Guidelines
| Booth Size | Best For | Staff Needed |
|------------|----------|--------------|
| 10x10 | Awareness, smaller shows | 2-3 people |
| 10x20 | Lead gen, demos | 3-5 people |
| 20x20 | Major presence, meetings | 5-8 people |
| Island | Flagship, large shows | 8-15 people |

### Staff Roles
**Lead Qualifier**: First contact, quick qualification
- "What brings you to the show?"
- "What challenges are you looking to solve?"

**Demo Specialist**: Deep dives with qualified prospects
- Technical questions, custom scenarios

**Senior Exec**: High-value meetings, partnership discussions
- C-level conversations, closing

### Booth Schedule Template
| Time | Position 1 | Position 2 | Position 3 | Break |
|------|------------|------------|------------|-------|
| 9-10 | [Name] | [Name] | [Name] | - |
| 10-11 | [Name] | [Name] | - | [Name] |
| 11-12 | [Name] | - | [Name] | [Name] |
...

### Engagement Tactics
- Live demo schedule (every 30 min)
- Raffle for badge scans
- Interactive game/quiz
- Charging station
- Refreshments
- Photo opportunity
```

#### Lead Capture Best Practices

```markdown
## Lead Capture System

### Capture Methods
| Method | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| Badge scan | Fast, low friction | Limited data | High volume |
| Paper forms | No tech issues | Manual entry | Backup |
| App + notes | Rich data | Slower | Quality convos |
| Business cards | Familiar | Manual entry | Relationship building |

### Lead Qualification Framework
Rate each lead (use simple A/B/C or numeric):

**A Lead (Hot)**
- Budget: Confirmed
- Authority: Decision maker present
- Need: Active project
- Timeline: <6 months
Action: Sales follow-up within 24 hours

**B Lead (Warm)**
- Interest confirmed
- Some criteria met
- Researching/evaluating
Action: Follow-up within 1 week

**C Lead (Cool)**
- Information seeker
- No clear timeline
- May be competitor
Action: Add to nurture

### Capture Data Points
Required:
- Name, email, company
- Lead rating (A/B/C)
- Key pain point or interest

Optional but valuable:
- Current solution
- Buying timeline
- Follow-up preference
- Specific questions asked

### Staff Notes Template
"[Rating] - [Name] from [Company]. Interested in [topic].
Challenge: [pain point]. Timeline: [when].
Follow-up: [specific action needed]"
```

### 3. Virtual Event Production

#### Production Checklist

```markdown
## Virtual Event Technical Setup

### Pre-Event (1 week before)
Equipment:
- [ ] Camera tested (webcam or professional)
- [ ] Microphone tested (USB mic preferred over laptop)
- [ ] Lighting checked (ring light or natural)
- [ ] Background set (clean, branded, or virtual)
- [ ] Backup internet ready (mobile hotspot)

Platform:
- [ ] Host and speaker accounts active
- [ ] Registration page live and tested
- [ ] Integration with CRM confirmed
- [ ] Recording settings configured
- [ ] Branding/logos uploaded

### Rehearsal (2-3 days before)
- [ ] Full run-through with all speakers
- [ ] Screen share tested
- [ ] Slides reviewed for virtual display
- [ ] Polls and Q&A tested
- [ ] Backup presenter assigned
- [ ] Timing confirmed

### Day Of Event
**60 min before:**
- [ ] Platform green room open
- [ ] All speakers online
- [ ] Final tech check
- [ ] Slides loaded
- [ ] Chat/Q&A moderation ready

**15 min before:**
- [ ] Recording started
- [ ] Waiting room open
- [ ] Music/holding slide active

**During event:**
- [ ] Producer monitoring chat
- [ ] Time checks for speakers
- [ ] Q&A collection for live session
- [ ] Technical issues handled

**Post-event:**
- [ ] Recording stopped and saved
- [ ] Thank you message sent
- [ ] Analytics pulled
```

#### Speaker Coordination

```markdown
## Speaker Management

### Speaker Briefing Template
Send to all speakers 2+ weeks before:

---
**Event**: [Name]
**Date/Time**: [Details including timezone]
**Platform**: [Name] - access link: [URL]
**Your session**: [Time slot and duration]

**Technical Requirements:**
- Stable internet connection (ethernet preferred)
- Quiet environment
- Camera and microphone
- Join green room 30 minutes early

**Content Guidelines:**
- Slide template: [Link]
- Due date for slides: [Date]
- Key messages to include: [Points]
- Promotion we'll provide: [Details]

**What we need from you:**
- [ ] Headshot (high-res)
- [ ] Bio (75-100 words)
- [ ] Social handles
- [ ] Slides by [date]
- [ ] Availability for rehearsal

**Contacts:**
- Event producer: [Name, email, phone]
- Day-of emergency: [Phone]
---

### Speaker Support During Event
- Dedicated Slack/text channel for real-time communication
- Producer to give time warnings (5 min, 1 min)
- Pre-loaded backup slides in case of technical issues
- Moderator to feed curated Q&A
```
