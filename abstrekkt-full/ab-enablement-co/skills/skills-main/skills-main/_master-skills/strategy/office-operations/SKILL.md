# Office Operations

## Overview

Office operations encompasses the management of physical and virtual workspaces, equipment, supplies, and the policies that govern how work environments function. For SMBs, effective office operations creates an environment that supports productivity, attracts talent, and optimizes costs.

This skill provides frameworks for managing all aspects of office operations - from physical facilities to remote work infrastructure. The focus is on creating efficient, cost-effective operations that scale with business growth.

### Why Office Operations Matters for SMBs

- **Productivity**: Well-designed workspaces improve employee effectiveness
- **Cost Control**: Efficient operations reduce overhead expenses
- **Talent Attraction**: Modern work environments attract quality candidates
- **Business Continuity**: Proper infrastructure ensures uninterrupted operations
- **Compliance**: Workplace safety and accessibility requirements
- **Culture**: Physical environment reflects and shapes company culture

## When to Use

### Primary Triggers
- Setting up new office spaces or relocating
- Procuring office equipment and technology
- Establishing or updating remote work policies
- Managing facilities maintenance and vendors
- Planning office budgets and cost optimization

### Specific Use Cases
- "We're moving to a new office space"
- "What equipment do we need for new employees?"
- "Help me create a remote work policy"
- "How do we manage office supplies efficiently?"
- "We need to set up a facilities maintenance schedule"
- "What should our office budget include?"
- "How do we design our workspace layout?"

### 3. Supplies Management

#### Supplies Inventory System

```markdown
## OFFICE SUPPLIES MANAGEMENT

### Supplies Categories
| Category | Examples | Reorder Frequency |
|----------|----------|-------------------|
| Paper goods | Copy paper, notebooks | Monthly |
| Writing | Pens, markers, highlighters | Monthly |
| Filing | Folders, labels, binders | Quarterly |
| Desk supplies | Staplers, tape, scissors | As needed |
| Kitchen | Coffee, snacks, utensils | Weekly/bi-weekly |
| Cleaning | Wipes, sanitizer | Weekly/bi-weekly |
| Bathroom | Towels, soap, tissue | Weekly |

### Par Level System
| Item | Par Level | Reorder Point | Order Qty |
|------|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Copy paper (ream) | 20 | 8 | 15 |
| Pens (box) | 5 | 2 | 4 |
| Notebooks | 15 | 5 | 12 |
| Coffee (bag) | 4 | 1 | 3 |
| Paper towels | 10 | 4 | 8 |

### Ordering Process
1. Weekly inventory check (designated day)
2. Compare to par levels
3. Create order list for items at/below reorder point
4. Submit order to approved vendor
5. Receive and verify delivery
6. Restock and update inventory

### Cost Control Strategies
- Consolidate vendors for volume discounts
- Set up automatic reorder for staples
- Limit high-cost items to request basis
- Monitor usage trends and adjust pars
- Review vendor pricing quarterly
```

This policy applies to [all employees / eligible roles] of [Company Name].

## 3. Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is determined by:
- Nature of job duties
- Performance history
- Manager approval
- Business needs

### Eligibility Criteria
Employees must:
- Be employed for [X] months minimum
- Have satisfactory performance ratings
- Demonstrate ability to work independently
- Have suitable home workspace

## 4. Remote Work Options

### Full Remote
- Work from home 100% of the time
- Required for roles designated as "remote"
- May require periodic in-office attendance

### Hybrid
- Split time between office and remote
- Typical split: [X] days office, [Y] days remote
- Core in-office days: [Specify if applicable]

### Occasional Remote
- Primarily office-based
- Remote work for specific situations
- Requires manager approval per instance

## 5. Work Hours and Availability

### Core Hours
Employees must be available during core hours:
[Time] to [Time] [Timezone]

### Response Expectations
- Respond to messages within [X] hours during work hours
- Update calendar with availability
- Communicate absences proactively

### Overtime
Remote employees follow same overtime policies as office employees.

### Home Office Setup
Employees must maintain:
- Dedicated workspace with minimal distractions
- Reliable internet connection ([X] Mbps minimum)

- Secure environment for confidential work

### Equipment

Employee provides:
- [List employee-provided items]

### Stipends (if applicable)
