# Inspiration Curation Skill

> Master creative reference collection with systematic curation, organization, and sharing of visual and conceptual inspiration to fuel creative excellence and team alignment.

## Overview

Inspiration curation is the systematic collection, organization, and application of creative references to inform and inspire creative work. This skill enables creative teams to build and maintain rich reference libraries, develop compelling moodboards, and ensure creative decisions are informed by relevant precedents and possibilities. It transforms inspiration from individual, ad-hoc activity into a strategic, shared creative resource.

## Why This Matters

### For Creative Teams
- Provides rich reference material for creative development
- Enables shared creative language and vision alignment
- Reduces creative blocks through diverse inspiration
- Builds institutional creative knowledge over time

### For Project Quality
- Elevates creative ambition through benchmark exposure
- Ensures awareness of category and cross-category excellence
- Informs stakeholder conversations with visual reference
- Reduces miscommunication through shared reference points

### For Creative Culture
- Cultivates continuous learning and curiosity
- Democratizes access to creative inspiration
- Encourages cross-disciplinary thinking
- Builds creative community through sharing

## When to Use

### Primary Triggers
- New project or campaign kickoff
- Creative brief development support
- Stakeholder alignment and presentation
- Team creative development sessions
- New team member onboarding
- Capability building initiatives

### Use This Skill When
- Need to establish visual direction
- Building moodboards for stakeholder alignment
- Researching creative precedents for a challenge
- Looking for innovative approaches to brief
- Developing creative rationale and references
- Training or inspiring creative teams

### Do Not Use When
- Conducting competitive analysis (use design-research)
- Tracking cultural trends (use trend-spotting)
- Testing creative concepts (use creative-testing)
- Measuring content performance (use content-performance)

### Moodboard Development

```yaml
moodboard_methodology:
  moodboard_types:
    visual_direction:
      purpose: "Establish aesthetic direction"
      content: "Color, typography, imagery style, layout"
      use_case: "Project kickoff, stakeholder alignment"

    conceptual_direction:
      purpose: "Communicate creative concept"
      content: "Varied references conveying single idea"
      use_case: "Creative exploration, idea communication"

    competitive_landscape:
      purpose: "Show category visual landscape"
      content: "Competitor and category examples"
      use_case: "Differentiation strategy, positioning"

    benchmark_excellence:
      purpose: "Demonstrate quality aspiration"
      content: "Best-in-class examples across categories"
      use_case: "Quality setting, inspiration"

    audience_culture:
      purpose: "Capture target audience world"
      content: "Lifestyle, cultural references, media"
      use_case: "Audience understanding, tone guidance"

  moodboard_construction:
    planning:
      steps:
        - "Define moodboard purpose"
        - "Identify key themes/messages"
        - "Determine audience"
        - "Plan narrative structure"

    collection:
      quantity: "3-5x final selection"
      diversity: "Range of sources and styles"
      relevance: "Direct connection to purpose"

    curation:
      selection_criteria:
        - "Strongest representation of theme"
        - "Visual variety within coherence"
        - "Clear communication of intent"
        - "Appropriate fidelity to context"

      typical_composition:
        hero_images: "2-3 dominant references"
        supporting_images: "6-10 secondary references"
        detail_callouts: "3-5 specific elements"
        text_context: "Minimal explanatory notes"

    layout:
      principles:
        - "Clear visual hierarchy"
        - "Intentional image relationships"
        - "Breathing room and balance"
        - "Narrative flow"

      tools:
        digital:
          - "Figma/FigJam"
          - "Miro"
          - "InVision Boards"
          - "Pinterest Boards"
          - "Canva"

        physical:
          - "Printed images"
          - "Physical boards"
          - "Wall displays"

    annotation:
      what_to_include:
        - "Brief context for obscure references"
        - "Specific element callouts"
        - "Relationship explanations"
        - "Source credits"

      what_to_avoid:
        - "Over-explanation"
        - "Defensive justification"
        - "Literal interpretation notes"
```
