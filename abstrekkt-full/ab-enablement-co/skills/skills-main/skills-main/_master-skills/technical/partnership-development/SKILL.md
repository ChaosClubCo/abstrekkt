# Partnership Development

## Overview

Strategic partnerships allow SMBs to expand reach, access new markets, and add value without building everything in-house. Well-structured partnerships create mutual benefit through shared audiences, complementary capabilities, and combined resources. This skill covers the partnership lifecycle from opportunity identification through program scaling.

For SMBs, partnerships can be a force multiplier - accessing enterprise accounts through larger partners, reaching new audiences through co-marketing, or adding capabilities through integrations. The key is creating clear value exchange and operational excellence in execution.

## When to Use

**Invoke this skill when:**
- Identifying potential partnership opportunities
- Structuring partnership agreements
- Creating co-marketing campaigns
- Building referral programs
- Enabling and training partners
- Developing joint value propositions
- Managing partner relationships
- Scaling partner channels

**Trigger phrases:**
- "Build a partnership"
- "Create referral program"
- "Partner enablement"
- "Co-marketing agreement"
- "Joint value proposition"
- "Alliance building"

- [ ] Serves similar audience (not same buyer)

**Strategic Value:**
- [ ] Access to desired market segment
- [ ] Complementary capabilities

- [ ] Established partner program (or willingness)
- [ ] Clear partnership team/owner

- [ ] Adequate resources to commit

#### Commercial Model Options

```markdown
## Partnership Commercial Models

### Referral Model
Best for: Light-touch partnerships, services businesses

| Tier | Condition | Referral Fee | Additional Benefits |
|------|-----------|--------------|---------------------|
| Basic | Any qualified referral | 10% first year | Listing in directory |
| Silver | 5+ referrals/quarter | 15% first year | Co-marketing budget |
| Gold | 15+ referrals/quarter | 20% first year | Dedicated support |
| Platinum | 30+ referrals/quarter | 25% first year | Strategic planning |

### Reseller Model
Best for: Product distribution, geographic expansion

**Pricing Structure:**
- Partner buy price: [X]% discount from list
- Suggested retail: List price
- Margin protection: [Territory/account rules]

**Volume Tiers:**
| Annual Revenue | Discount | Support Level |
|----------------|----------|---------------|
| <$50K | 20% | Standard |
| $50-150K | 25% | Enhanced |
| $150K+ | 30% | Premium |

### Revenue Share Model
Best for: Platform integrations, embedded solutions

**Typical Structures:**
- 70/30: Product owner 70%, distribution partner 30%
- 60/40: More significant partner contribution
- 50/50: Equal partnership in joint solution

### Hybrid Models
Combine elements based on activity:
- Base referral fee + volume bonuses
- Revenue share + marketing co-investment
- License fee + per-transaction fee
```

### 4. Referral Program Design

#### Referral Program Structure

```markdown
## Referral Program Blueprint

### Program Tiers

**Tier 1: Casual Referrer**
Commitment: Ad-hoc referrals, no minimum
Benefits:
- [X]% commission on closed deals
- Partner directory listing
- Referral tracking portal

**Tier 2: Active Partner**
Commitment: 2+ referrals per quarter
Benefits:
- [X]% commission (higher than Tier 1)
- Co-marketing opportunities
- Quarterly partner call
- Early access to features

**Tier 3: Strategic Partner**
Commitment: 5+ referrals per quarter
Benefits:
- [X]% commission (highest tier)
- Dedicated partner manager
- Joint marketing budget
- Product roadmap input
- Customer success priority

### Referral Process
1. Partner submits referral via portal/form
2. Referral acknowledged within 24 hours
3. Sales team follows up within 48 hours
4. Partner notified of status changes
5. Commission paid within 30 days of closed deal

### Referral Qualification
For referral to be accepted:
- [ ] New prospect (not in system)
- [ ] Contact information complete
- [ ] Basic qualification shared
- [ ] Partner introduction made
- [ ] Prospect expecting contact

### Commission Structure
| Deal Size | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|-----------|--------|--------|--------|
| <$10K ACV | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| $10-50K ACV | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| $50K+ ACV | 10% | 15% | 20% |

**Payment Terms:**
- Paid after customer's first payment received
- Net 30 payment via [method]
- Monthly reporting on referral status
- Annual 1099 provided (US partners)
```

#### Referral Tracking & Management

```markdown
## Referral Management System

### Referral Submission Form
Required Fields:
- Partner name/company
- Referred contact name
- Referred contact email
- Referred company name
- Company size (employees/revenue)
- Primary need/pain point
- How they know the prospect
- Best way to introduce

### Status Workflow
1. **Submitted**: Referral received
2. **Accepted**: Meets criteria, assigned to sales
3. **Contacted**: First outreach made
4. **Engaged**: Active conversation
5. **Qualified**: Opportunity created
6. **Proposal**: Deal in negotiation
7. **Closed Won**: Commission earned
8. **Closed Lost**: Document reason

### Partner Portal Features
- Submit new referrals
- Track referral status
- View commission earned
- Download marketing materials
- Access training resources
- Update partner profile

### Reporting Cadence
**To Partners (Monthly):**
- Referrals submitted vs. accepted
- Stage progression
- Commissions pending and paid
- Upcoming renewals (if applicable)

**Internal (Weekly):**
- New referrals by partner
- Conversion rates by stage
- Time in each stage
- Revenue from referrals
```
