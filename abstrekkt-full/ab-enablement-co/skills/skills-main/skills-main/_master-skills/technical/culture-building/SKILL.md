# Culture Building

> Values definition, culture program design, and organizational identity development that creates engaged and aligned workplaces.

## Metadata

- **Skill ID**: culture-building
- **Category**: Back of House - Executive Operations
- **Complexity Level**: Advanced
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Organizational psychology fundamentals
  - Leadership experience
  - Communication skills
  - Change management understanding

## Overview

Culture Building encompasses the deliberate design and nurturing of organizational culture through values articulation, cultural programs, rituals, and practices. This skill enables leaders to create intentional cultures that attract talent, drive performance, and create sustainable competitive advantage.

## Core Capabilities

### Recognition Program Design

**Recognition System Framework**
```markdown
## Recognition Program Design

### Recognition Tiers

**Tier 1: Everyday Recognition**
- Peer-to-peer shoutouts
- Slack/Teams kudos
- Team meeting callouts
- Informal thanks

*Purpose*: High frequency, low formality
*Frequency*: Daily
*Reward*: Social recognition

**Tier 2: Manager Recognition**
- One-on-one praise
- Team celebrations
- Spot bonuses
- Public acknowledgment

*Purpose*: Manager-driven reinforcement
*Frequency*: Weekly/Monthly
*Reward*: Social + small tangible

**Tier 3: Formal Awards**
- Monthly/quarterly awards
- Values-based recognition
- Leadership nominations
- Company-wide celebration

*Purpose*: Formal reinforcement
*Frequency*: Monthly/Quarterly
*Reward*: Significant tangible + visibility

**Tier 4: Milestone Recognition**
- Annual awards
- Career milestones
- Major achievements
- Cultural champions

*Purpose*: Major accomplishment celebration
*Frequency*: Annual
*Reward*: Major tangible + prestige

### Program Mechanics
- Nomination process
- Selection criteria
- Award distribution
- Communication approach
- Budget allocation
```
