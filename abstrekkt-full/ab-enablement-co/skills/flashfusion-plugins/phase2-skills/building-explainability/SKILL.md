# Building Explainability

Makes AI decisions understandable at Intinc — from technical interpretability for engineers to user-facing explanations that build trust and meet regulatory transparency requirements.

**Example 1 — Implement building explainability for our AI platform**

## Golden Path Workflow

1. Identify applicable regulatory and policy requirements
2. Assess current building explainability maturity level
3. Define target state and gap analysis for building explainability
4. Design implementation plan with clear milestones
5. Build tooling, processes, and documentation
6. Validate with test scenarios and edge cases
7. Train relevant teams on new processes
8. Deploy to production with monitoring
9. Collect compliance evidence and maintain audit trail
10. Schedule periodic reviews and updates

## Edge Cases & Anti-Patterns

- **Regulatory variation** — building explainability requirements differ by jurisdiction. What's compliant in the US may not be in the EU.
- **Retroactive requirements** — New regulations may apply retroactively to existing AI systems. Maintain an inventory of deployed models for rapid building explainability reassessment.
- **Third-party components** — building explainability applies to vendor-provided AI components too, not just internally built systems.
- **Edge case escalation** — When automated building explainability checks are inconclusive, have a clear human escalation path with defined SLA.
