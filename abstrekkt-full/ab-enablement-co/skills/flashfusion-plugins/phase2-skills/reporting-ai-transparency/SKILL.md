## Edge Cases & Anti-Patterns

- **Regulatory variation** — reporting ai transparency requirements differ by jurisdiction. What's compliant in the US may not be in the EU.
- **Retroactive requirements** — New regulations may apply retroactively to existing AI systems. Maintain an inventory of deployed models for rapid reporting ai transparency reassessment.
- **Third-party components** — reporting ai transparency applies to vendor-provided AI components too, not just internally built systems.
- **Edge case escalation** — When automated reporting ai transparency checks are inconclusive, have a clear human escalation path with defined SLA.
