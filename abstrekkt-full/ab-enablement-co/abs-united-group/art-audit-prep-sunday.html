Discovery questions — tuned for Art

Click any question for the why. Ordered by leverage.
Q1Walk me through one full coaching session, start to finish. What do you prepare, what do you show, what do they leave with?
This surfaces his delivery workflow — the thing he repeats 2×/week per client. Whatever is manual here is the highest-ROI automation target, because savings multiply across every client and every week.
Q2When a new client signs up, what happens between "they paid" and "first session"? How much of that is copy-paste?
Onboarding is the classic coaching-business leak. Intake forms, welcome docs, scheduling, baseline assessment — if he's doing this manually per client, a Projects + templates setup pays for the audit by itself.
Q3Do your prompts live anywhere? If a client asks "send me that prompt from last week," what do you do?
Tests prompt asset management. If the answer is "scroll back through chat history," that's a finding. His prompts are literally his product — they should be a versioned library he can hand out, not chat archaeology.
Q4What does a client do between sessions? Do they have homework, and how do you know if they did it?
Surfaces whether there's any async layer. If everything happens live 2×/week, his income has a hard ceiling = his calendar. Self-serve materials between sessions is the unlock toward team tiers and scale.
Q5Where does your real-estate business and your coaching business share tools or data — and where do they collide?
He runs two businesses on one stack, same as Kyle. Look for context bleed: client coaching chats mixed with his own deal work, no project separation. Lane discipline is a finding he'll feel immediately.
Q6What's the question your coaching clients ask you most often — the one you're tired of answering?
That recurring answer is a reusable asset waiting to exist: FAQ doc, custom project, recorded walkthrough. Also tells you what his clients struggle with, which is market intel for SparkAcademy later. Don't pitch that Sunday — just note it.
Q7When the AI gives you something wrong in front of a client, what do you do?
Tests error handling + credibility protection. A coach teaching AI live needs a recovery move. If he doesn't have one, that's a teachable framework — and exactly the diagnostic-layer thinking that separates this audit from a prompt-tips session.
