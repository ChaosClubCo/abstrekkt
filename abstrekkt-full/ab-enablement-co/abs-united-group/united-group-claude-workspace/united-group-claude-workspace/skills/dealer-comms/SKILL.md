# Dealer Communications

## What this does
Drafts partner-to-partner communications that help dealers sell IRON HORSE into
mission-critical environments.

## Rules
- B2B partner tone — respectful, useful, not salesy at them.
- Reference the dealer's market where known (pull from Dealer Enablement project).
- Verified specs only; exact series names.
- Respect co-branding rules in the Dealer Enablement knowledge.

## Common outputs
- New-product or price-update announcements
- "Which IRON HORSE for which environment" selector guides
- Objection-handling one-pagers
- Co-branded one-pager drafts (note where dealer logo/contact drops in)
