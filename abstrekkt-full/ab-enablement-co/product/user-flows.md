    _Generated: 2026-06-21. Reconstruction confidence is bounded by accessible project memory/context and the current chat session._


## Lead to Paid Audit

1. Visitor lands on readiness/audit page.
2. Visitor submits intake.
3. System scores fit, urgency, and pricing alignment.
4. Operator reviews and chooses proof-of-value, paid audit, workshop, or nurture.
5. Client receives scoped proposal.
6. On approval, workspace and engagement artifacts are created.

## Audit Delivery

1. Operator imports approved source material.
2. System builds entity/workflow/opportunity map.
3. Agents draft findings and questions.
4. Human reviewer approves claims.
5. Dashboard and report are delivered.
6. Reusable patterns are extracted into libraries.
