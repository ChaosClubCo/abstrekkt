## Product Name

FlashFusion Core: AI Readiness, Revenue, and Delivery Workspace.

## Problem

SMBs and teams know AI matters but lack a practical path from vague curiosity to safe, revenue-producing workflows. FlashFusion also needs a reusable system that turns each engagement into productized assets instead of one-off consulting work.

## Goals

- Convert qualified leads into paid audits/workshops.
- Produce domain-specific opportunity maps and implementation plans.
- Maintain claim-safe, IP-safe, evidence-tagged deliverables.
- Capture reusable patterns into prompt/agent/template libraries.
- Provide a future path to commerce/studio automation products.

## Non-Goals

- Do not claim unverifiable outcomes.
- Do not build a full public marketplace before the consulting engine works.
- Do not make Notion a required dependency.
- Do not allow agents to perform destructive actions without review.

## Core Features

1. Lead intake and qualification.
2. Organization/client workspace.
3. AI readiness audit builder.
4. Workflow/opportunity map.
5. Agent-assisted artifact generation.
6. Human review and claims validation.
7. Proposal and pricing engine.
8. Prompt/agent/template library.
9. Engagement delivery dashboard.
10. Analytics and action-yield review.

## Success Metrics

- Qualified leads created per week.
- Paid audit conversion rate.
- Average delivery cycle time.
- Reusable artifact count per engagement.
- Percentage of artifacts passing claims review on first pass.
- Schedule action-yield rate.
