VIDEO 2 — THE CHAMPION MODEL (90s, LinkedIn)

SCRIPT

─────── I don't sell AI tools. I train the one person inside your company who will own them.

My background is diagnosis. Reading rooms in hospitality. Catching failures on a manufacturing floor. Finding the real problem in a tangled system. Figuring out what's actually happening — not what everyone assumes is happening.

Most AI consulting is solution-first. Someone shows up with a tool and goes looking for a place to use it. The result is wasted spend and a team that feels further behind, not less.

I work problem-first. I find the domain expert your team already trusts, and over six to twelve weeks I build them into your AI champion. They learn to apply it to their real workflows, train their colleagues, and judge new tools on their own. Then I exit — when they're capable, not when a contract runs out.

I don't install AI in your business. I install an AI expert in your team. Follow along on LinkedIn — I post the real process.
