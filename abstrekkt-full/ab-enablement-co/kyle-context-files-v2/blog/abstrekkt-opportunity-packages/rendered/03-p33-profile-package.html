WHAT P33 IS AND WHAT YOU'RE USING IT FOR

P33 is a nonprofit ecosystem connector — they don't fund you, they introduce you. The value is warm access to Chicago corporates, community organizations, and civic partners that would otherwise require a cold outreach chain.

For FlashFusion specifically:

  • Corporate introductions → potential enterprise clients for AI enablement programs
  • Community college and workforce partners → validates and accelerates the CLC/union pipeline
  • Tech community visibility → useful when the Lee Chong Ming pitch is ready to send

P33 also runs TechRise — a grants/accelerator program for diverse founders. Worth monitoring even if you don't qualify for the current cohort.
