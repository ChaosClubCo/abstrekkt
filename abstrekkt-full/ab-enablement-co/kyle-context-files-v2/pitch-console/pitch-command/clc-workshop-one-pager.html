The problem
Students know AI exists. They don't know what to do with it.

Most AI coverage in education is conceptual. This workshop is applied. There is a difference between knowing ChatGPT exists and knowing how to use AI in a real workflow, a job search, or a career being built from scratch.

Employers are already filtering for AI literacy. Students need practical reps, not another overview.

About the presenter
Non-traditional path. Earned expertise.

Kyle Rosebrook came from hospitality management, not engineering. He taught himself AI and automation, was hired at an MSP in Lincolnshire, IL, and built the company's AI subject matter function from the ground up.
