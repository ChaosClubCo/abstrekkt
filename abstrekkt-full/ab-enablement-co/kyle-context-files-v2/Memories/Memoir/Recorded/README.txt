The Root Cause
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not lack of skill. Lack of integration. You're trying to do 6 different jobs:
1. Staff Engineer (technical leadership)
2. Product founder (FlashFusion)
3. Automation expert (13 MCP servers)
4. Thought leader (personal brand)
5. Dad (Kinsley time + educational apps)
6. Income builder (passive revenue)

The Solution
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This system integrates all 6. Instead of 6 separate jobs, it's 1 job with 6 facets that feed each other:
  - Staff Engineer work → Thought leadership (KB articles, AI governance)
  - Thought leadership → Product credibility ("Trust me, I built this")
  - Product → Automation (delivery, repurposing)
  - Automation → Time savings (enables other work)
  - All of it → Kinsley educational apps (using same tech stack)

Result: Same time invested, 3x progress visible
