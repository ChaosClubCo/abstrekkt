### Key Insights

**Market Adoption**: ChatGPT Enterprise leads with 92% Fortune 500 usage, followed closely by Google Gemini Enterprise at 80% usage, while Microsoft Copilot shows 70% adoption across the F500. Anthropic Claude Enterprise is emerging with growing enterprise traction.

**Technical Capabilities**: Google Gemini Enterprise stands out with an exceptional 2 million token context window, enabling processing of significantly larger documents and datasets. Anthropic Claude Enterprise offers 200K tokens with a 1 million token beta version, while both Microsoft and OpenAI platforms operate at 128K token capacity. These context windows are critical for enterprises handling complex document analysis and code review tasks.

**Positioning Strategy**: Each platform emphasizes distinct value propositions—Microsoft focuses on seamless integration with its enterprise ecosystem, Google targets cross-platform compatibility and multilingual support, OpenAI prioritizes general-purpose flexibility and creative applications, and Anthropic emphasizes safety and compliance-focused features for regulated industries.
