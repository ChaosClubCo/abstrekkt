- **Actions**:
    - Immediate platform suspension for affected department

    - Enhanced DLP policies
    - Mandatory security retraining
    - Potential platform switch if vendor at fault

**Scenario 3: Vendor Pricing Increase (>20%)**

- **Triggers**: Renewal pricing significantly higher
- **Actions**:
    - Negotiate based on multi-year commitment
    - Evaluate competitive platforms
    - Consider hybrid reduction (reduce seats, strategic deployment)
- **Contingency**: Multi-platform approach reduces single-vendor dependency

**Scenario 4: Regulatory Compliance Failure**

- **Triggers**: Audit findings, regulatory penalties
- **Actions**:
    - Immediate compliance review of all AI usage
    - Switch to Claude for compliance-critical workflows
    - Enhanced governance and monitoring
    - Legal/compliance team AI oversight

**Scenario 5: User Resistance (>50% negative feedback)**

- **Triggers**: Poor satisfaction scores, low adoption despite training
- **Actions**:
    - User feedback sessions (focus groups)
    - Adjust use cases to "quick wins"
    - Increase executive visibility and sponsorship
    - Gamification and incentives
- **Contingency**: Slow rollout, focus on early adopters only
