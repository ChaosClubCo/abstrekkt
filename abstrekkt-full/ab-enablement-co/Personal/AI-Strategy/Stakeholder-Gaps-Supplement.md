## SECTION 1: BOARD OF DIRECTORS - GOVERNANCE & COMPETITIVE RISK

### The Cost of Inaction

**Competitive Disadvantage > Security Risk**
- **CLAIM:** 74% of boards fear falling behind competitors MORE than they fear AI security risks
- **SOURCE:** [RSM AI and the Board of Directors Report (March 2025)](https://rsmus.com/insights/services/digital-transformation/ai-and-the-board-of-directors-concerns-and-strategic-considerati.html)
- **IMPLICATION:** Boards view marketplace differentiation through AI as higher priority than risk mitigation

**Board AI Knowledge Gap**
- **CLAIM:** Only 38% of boards have sufficient knowledge to respond effectively to AI presentations
- **SOURCE:** [Heidrick AI Focus: How Boards Are Finding Expertise (November 2025)](https://www.heidrick.com/en/insights/frontier-tech/ai-focus_how-boards-find-expertise)
- **CONTEXT:** Majority of boards lack technical expertise to govern AI strategy effectively

**Current Board AI Usage**
- **CLAIM:** Only 35% of directors report using generative AI in their board role (as of 2025)
- **SOURCE:** [PwC 2025 Annual Corporate Directors Survey (October 2025)](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/governance-insights-center/library/ai-use-in-the-boardroom.html)
- **TREND:** Adoption expected to grow as tools mature and guardrails strengthen

### AI Governance Frameworks for Boards

**Deloitte AI Governance Roadmap (April 2025)**
- **CLAIM:** Structured framework applying Deloitte Governance Framework to AI oversight
- **SOURCE:** [Harvard Law School Forum - Deloitte AI Governance Roadmap (April 23, 2025)](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2025/04/24/strategic-governance-of-ai-a-roadmap-for-the-future/)
- **SIX KEY AREAS:**
  1. Strategy - AI alignment with business objectives
  2. Risk - Identifying bias, hallucinations, IP challenges, regulatory scrutiny
  3. Governance - Policies, responsible innovation, Trustworthy AI
  4. Performance - Monitoring AI ROI and business outcomes
  5. Talent - Skills, executive ownership, management committee structure
  6. Culture & Integrity - Tone from top, ethical considerations

**Critical Board Questions (Deloitte Framework)**
- Does the board have expertise to advise on AI strategy and monitor implementation?
- Does management have an "inventory" of ML AI and GenAI usage across organization?
- Are changes to board structure (new/existing committees) needed for effective oversight?
- Does board understand which executive/team is responsible for AI strategy execution?
- What is the cadence for AI on board agenda and who presents?

**PwC Board Oversight of AI Framework (March 2025)**
- **SOURCE:** [PwC Board Oversight of AI (March 2025)](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/governance-insights-center/library/board-oversight-ai.html)
- **KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:**
  - Ensuring AI strategy drives value creation aligned with business objectives
  - Overseeing key risks associated with AI deployment
  - Making sure management implements appropriate risk controls
  - Monitoring AI performance to prevent "financial black holes"
  - Fostering culture change and talent transformation

**Audit Committee as Primary AI Overseer**
- **BEST PRACTICE:** Audit Committee typically owns AI governance oversight initially
- **RATIONALE:** Risk management, compliance, data governance naturally align with Audit Committee charter
- **EVOLUTION:** As AI strategy matures, may shift to full board or Strategy Committee

### Regulatory Compliance Roadmap

**EU AI Act Timeline**
- **STATUS:** Passed 2024, implementation timeline established
- **RISK CLASSIFICATION:** Four tiers (Unacceptable, High, Limited, Minimal Risk)
- **IMPLICATION:** Boards need compliance monitoring for EU operations

**State-Level AI Regulations**
- **ACTIVE JURISDICTIONS:** California, New York, Illinois (user's home state)
- **BOARD ACTION NEEDED:** Monitor emerging requirements, assess compliance obligations

**Sector-Specific Requirements**
- **FINANCIAL SERVICES:** Enhanced scrutiny on algorithmic bias, fair lending
- **HEALTHCARE:** HIPAA compliance, clinical decision support regulations
- **INSURANCE:** Actuarial fairness, discrimination prevention

## SECTION 2: CFO PERSPECTIVES - ROI METHODOLOGY EVOLUTION

### The 2025 Shift: AI Agents Change Everything

**CFO AI Strategy Transformation**
- **CLAIM:** 79% of CFOs indicate AI budgets will increase in 2025
- **SOURCE:** [Salesforce CFO AI Investment Survey (October 28, 2025)](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cfos-invest-ai-for-growth/)
- **DRAMATIC SHIFT:**
  - 2020: 70% of CFOs maintained conservative AI strategy
  - 2023: 34% maintained conservative strategy
  - 2025: Only 4% maintain conservative AI strategy
  - 2025: 33% have adopted aggressive AI strategy

**AI Agents Redefine ROI Evaluation**
- **CLAIM:** 61% of CFOs say AI agents are changing how they evaluate ROI
- **SOURCE:** [Salesforce CFO AI Investment Survey (October 28, 2025)](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cfos-invest-ai-for-growth/)
- **OLD ROI FOCUS:** Immediate, measurable financial returns (cost savings)
- **NEW ROI FOCUS:** Mix of long-term and short-term outcomes including:
  1. Cost savings, risk/compliance improvements, revenue growth (tied #1)
  2. Productivity and efficiency improvements (#2)
  3. Improved decision-making (#3)

**AI Agent Budget Allocation**
- **CLAIM:** CFOs dedicate 25% of total AI budget to AI agents specifically
- **SOURCE:** [Salesforce CFO AI Investment Survey (October 28, 2025)](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cfos-invest-ai-for-growth/)
- **CONTEXT:** Represents massive shift from analytical AI to agentic AI investment

### Revenue Generation > Cost Cutting

**CFO Revenue Expectations from AI Agents**
- **CLAIM:** 74% of CFOs believe AI agents will drive revenue, not just cut costs
- **CLAIM:** CFOs implementing AI agents expect 20% revenue increase
- **SOURCE:** [Salesforce CFO AI Investment Survey (October 28, 2025)](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cfos-invest-ai-for-growth/)
- **STRATEGIC IMPLICATION:** AI viewed as growth engine, not efficiency tool

**Business Model Transformation**
- **CLAIM:** 72% of CFOs say AI agents will transform their business model
- **SOURCE:** [Salesforce CFO AI Investment Survey (October 28, 2025)](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cfos-invest-ai-for-growth/)

**Strategic vs. Routine Work**
- **CLAIM:** 55% of CFOs think AI agents will take on more strategic work than routine tasks
- **SOURCE:** [Salesforce CFO AI Investment Survey (October 28, 2025)](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cfos-invest-ai-for-growth/)
- **CONTEXT:** Challenges assumption that AI only automates low-value work

### CFO Top Concerns

**Security and Privacy Threats**
- **CLAIM:** 66% of CFOs cite security/privacy threats as top concern about AI strategy
- **SOURCE:** [Salesforce CFO AI Investment Survey (October 28, 2025)](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cfos-invest-ai-for-growth/)

**Long Time to ROI**
- **CLAIM:** 56% of CFOs concerned about long time to ROI
- **SOURCE:** [Salesforce CFO AI Investment Survey (October 28, 2025)](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cfos-invest-ai-for-growth/)
- **CFO QUOTE:** "The ongoing investment required for retraining, monitoring, and improving AI models makes ROI more fluid than for fixed-function tools"

**Risk of Ethical/Reputational Damage**
- **CFO QUOTE:** "Other technology does not typically involve the ethical risks AI does. If AI goes wrong, the reputational cost affects ROI in ways regular tools never would"
- **SOURCE:** [Salesforce CFO AI Investment Survey (October 28, 2025)](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cfos-invest-ai-for-growth/)

## SECTION 3: EMPLOYEE PERSPECTIVES - JOB DISPLACEMENT & TRUST

### Job Displacement: The Reality vs. The Fear

**Worker Anxiety Nearly Doubles**
- **CLAIM:** 52% of US workers fear job displacement due to AI (nearly doubled from 2024)
- **SOURCE:** [KPMG Fear of AI-Driven Job Displacement (November 24, 2025)](https://www.cfodive.com/news/fear-of-ai-driven-job-displacement-nearly-doubles-in-a-year-kpmg/806380/)
- **CONTEXT:** Survey of 2,100+ US employees across major industries (June-July 2025)

**AI-Related Layoffs Already Happening**
- **CLAIM:** 14% of workers have already been displaced by AI
- **CLAIM:** 49% of companies using ChatGPT say it has replaced workers
- **SOURCE:** [National University AI Job Statistics (September 2025)](https://www.nu.edu/blog/ai-job-statistics/)

**Legislative Response**
- **CLAIM:** Sens. Hawley (R-Mo.) and Warner (D-Va.) introduced S. 3108 requiring AI-related layoff reporting
- **QUOTE:** "Artificial intelligence is already replacing American workers, and experts project AI could drive unemployment up to 10-20% in the next five years"
- **SOURCE:** [KPMG Fear of AI-Driven Job Displacement (November 24, 2025)](https://www.cfodive.com/news/fear-of-ai-driven-job-displacement-nearly-doubles-in-a-year-kpmg/806380/)

### Goldman Sachs: Slower Displacement Than Feared

**Baseline Projection: 6-7% Over Next Decade**
- **CLAIM:** AI could displace 6-7% of US workforce if widely adopted, but impact likely transitory
- **SOURCE:** [Goldman Sachs: How Will AI Affect the Global Workforce? (August 12, 2025)](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/how-will-ai-affect-the-global-workforce)
- **RANGE:** Displacement rates could vary from 3% to 14% under different assumptions
- **CONTEXT:** Analysis of 800+ occupations; displacement lower than sensationalized headlines suggest

**Productivity Gains with Minimal Unemployment**
- **CLAIM:** Generative AI will raise labor productivity by ~15% when fully adopted
- **CLAIM:** This translates to half-percentage-point (0.5%) rise in unemployment above trend during transition
- **SOURCE:** [Goldman Sachs: How Will AI Affect the Global Workforce? (August 12, 2025)](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/how-will-ai-affect-the-global-workforce)

**Most-Affected: Young Tech Workers**
- **CLAIM:** Unemployment among 20-30 year-olds in tech-exposed occupations rose by ~3 percentage points since start of 2025
- **IMPLICATION:** Recent college graduates in technology facing hiring headwinds
- **SOURCE:** [Goldman Sachs: How Will AI Affect the Global Workforce? (August 12, 2025)](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/how-will-ai-affect-the-global-workforce)

**Currently at Risk: Only 2.5% of US Employment**
- **CLAIM:** If current AI use cases expanded across economy, just 2.5% of US employment would be at risk of displacement
- **SOURCE:** [Goldman Sachs: How Will AI Affect the Global Workforce? (August 12, 2025)](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/how-will-ai-affect-the-global-workforce)

### Trust and Communication Gaps

**Low Trust in Employer AI Safety**
- **CLAIM:** Only 31% of social sector employees trust employers will develop AI safely (vs. 71% cross-industry average)
- **SOURCE:** [McKinsey: AI in the Workplace Report (January 27, 2025)](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/superagency-in-the-workplace-empowering-people-to-unlock-ais-full)

**Employees Uncomfortable with AI Decision-Making**
- **CLAIM:** 41% of employees uncomfortable with AI replacing human decision-making
- **SOURCE:** [Oliver Wyman: Navigating the Impact of Generative AI on the Workforce (February 27, 2024)](https://www.oliverwymanforum.com/artificial-intelligence/2024/feb/addressing-employee-concerns--navigating-the-impact-of-generat)

**Low Personal Benefit Perception**
- **CLAIM:** Only 13% of workers believe AI will personally help them (despite 2/3 expecting major workforce impact)
- **SOURCE:** [Forbes: The Rise of AI Resentment at Work (February 2, 2025)](https://www.forbes.com/sites/dianehamilton/2025/02/03/the-rise-of-ai-resentment-at-work-why-employees-are-pushing-back/)

**AI Resentment**
- **KEY DRIVER:** Employees feeling "replaced" rather than "valued"
- **CONSEQUENCES:**
  - Concerns about surveillance and loss of autonomy
  - Increased pressure to adapt without support
  - 57% more likely to experience productivity decline when concerned about AI automation
- **SOURCE:** [Oliver Wyman: Navigating the Impact of Generative AI on the Workforce (February 27, 2024)](https://www.oliverwymanforum.com/artificial-intelligence/2024/feb/addressing-employee-concerns--navigating-the-impact-of-generat)

### The Data Leakage Crisis

**Critical Training Gap**
- **CLAIM:** 84% of generative AI users have leaked company data due to lack of training
- **SOURCE:** [Oliver Wyman: Navigating the Impact of Generative AI on the Workforce (February 27, 2024)](https://www.oliverwymanforum.com/artificial-intelligence/2024/feb/addressing-employee-concerns--navigating-the-impact-of-generat)
- **IMPLICATION:** Employee training on responsible AI use is URGENT security requirement

### What Employees Need (INT Inc. Action Items)

1. **Transparent Communication**
   - Clear distinction between job augmentation vs. replacement
   - Honest timelines for AI adoption across departments
   - Regular updates on AI strategy and employee impact

2. **Upskilling Programs**
   - AI literacy training for all employees
   - Advanced training for roles most affected
   - Career pathway guidance in AI-augmented environment

3. **Clear Performance Measurement**
   - How will productivity be measured with AI tools?
   - What happens if employees don't adopt AI tools?
   - How do bonuses/promotions work in AI-assisted roles?

4. **Responsible AI Use Training**
   - Data security protocols
   - What data can/cannot be shared with AI tools
   - IP protection and confidentiality guidelines

- **CLAIM:** 84% of SaaS applications purchased outside IT oversight
- **SOURCE:** [Zylo: The Dark Side of AI Data Security (April 9, 2025)](https://zylo.com/blog/ai-data-security/)
- **IMPLICATION:** Massive shadow AI risk - employees adopting AI tools without security vetting

### Vendor Due Diligence Framework (Client Expectations)

**Critical Questions Clients Ask AI Vendors:**

1. **Unauthorized Data Collection**
   - Are you collecting more data than necessary for service delivery?
   - What data minimization practices are in place?

2. **Data Repurposing**
   - Will client data be used to train AI models?
   - Will data be sold or shared with third parties?
   - Can clients opt out of training data usage?

3. **Lack of Transparency**
   - How do you address "black box AI" concerns?
   - Can you explain how AI makes decisions affecting our organization?
   - What visibility do we have into AI processing?

4. **Global Compliance**
   - GDPR compliance (EU operations)
   - CCPA compliance (California)
   - EU AI Act adherence
   - Industry-specific regulations (HIPAA, SOC 2, etc.)

**SOURCE:** [VeraSafe: AI Vendors and Data Privacy (June 30, 2025)](https://verasafe.com/blog/ai-vendors-and-data-privacy-essential-insights-for-organizations/)

### What Clients Want to See (INT Inc. Deliverables)

**Data Processing Addenda (DPAs)**
- Clear definition of data use, security measures, compliance obligations
- Explicit limitations on data repurposing
- Audit rights and transparency reporting

**Third-Party Security Certifications**
- SOC 2 Type II ✓ (Already verified in master document)
- ISO 27001 ✓ (Already verified in master document)
- Industry-specific certifications as applicable

**Privacy-Enhancing Technologies**
- Encryption at rest and in transit ✓ (Already verified)
- Data minimization practices
- Anonymization/pseudonymization where applicable

**Transparency Reports**
- Regular security audits and findings
- Data breach notification procedures
- AI model performance and bias testing results

**Clear Data Retention and Deletion Policies**
- How long is client data stored?
- What triggers data deletion?
- Can clients request immediate deletion?

**Client-Facing AI Privacy Policy**
- Plain-language explanation of AI use
- Opt-out mechanisms for AI-assisted services
- Contact for data subject access requests
