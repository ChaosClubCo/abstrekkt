# suggest next

The next logical step for your Enterprise AI Decision Platform is to launch a pilot deployment focused on high-ROI use cases, gather actionable feedback, and scale adoption across departments. This approach ensures measurable value, smooth integration, and continuous improvement. Below are concrete next steps, prioritized by impact and feasibility, with guidance for both technical and executive stakeholders.[^1][^2]

***

### Launch a High-ROI Pilot Deployment

Begin with a targeted pilot in departments where AI adoption delivers the fastest and most visible returns. This builds momentum, demonstrates value, and provides real-world data for broader rollout.

#### 1. Prioritize Quick-Win Departments

- **Compliance/Evidence Gathering:** Use Perplexity or Claude for automated evidence collection, audit prep, and policy drafting. These use cases typically deliver 70–90% time savings and 3–4 month ROI, making them ideal for rapid proof-of-concept.[^1]
- **Sales/Proposal Generation:** Deploy ChatGPT or Copilot for proposal automation, CRM integration, and personalized outreach. Expect 500–1000% ROI and 2–3 month payback, with measurable improvements in sales cycle efficiency.[^1]
- **HR/Workforce Planning:** Leverage Claude or Gemini for workforce analytics, scenario modeling, and talent acquisition. These solutions reduce planning cycles by 65–75% and deliver 400–600% ROI within 4–6 months.[^1]


#### 2. Measure Baseline Metrics

- Track pre-implementation metrics for each pilot department (e.g., hours spent on manual tasks, error rates, compliance audit cycle time, proposal turnaround).
- Use the platform’s built-in ROI calculator and reporting tools to quantify time and cost savings post-deployment.[^2]


#### 3. Train Department Champions

- Identify and train AI champions in each pilot department to drive adoption, answer questions, and share best practices.
- Provide onboarding materials, quick guides, and demo sessions tailored to non-technical users.[^2]

***

### Gather Feedback and Iterate

Collect structured feedback from pilot users to refine workflows, address pain points, and optimize platform features.

#### 1. Conduct Post-Pilot Surveys

- Survey users on ease of use, time savings, and perceived value.
- Gather qualitative feedback on workflow integration, feature gaps, and usability issues.[^2]


#### 2. Analyze Usage Data

- Monitor platform analytics to identify high-impact features, underutilized tools, and adoption bottlenecks.
- Use this data to prioritize enhancements and expand pilot scope.[^2]


#### 3. Iterate and Optimize

- Refine workflows based on user feedback and usage patterns.
- Document best practices and lessons learned for broader rollout.[^2]

***

### Scale Adoption Across Departments

Once pilot success is demonstrated, expand AI adoption to additional departments, leveraging lessons learned and proven workflows.

#### 1. Roll Out to Additional Departments

- Prioritize departments with high research needs, manual processes, or compliance requirements (e.g., Finance, Legal, IT, Operations).
- Customize platform features and training materials for each department’s unique needs.[^1][^2]


#### 2. Institutionalize AI-Driven Workflows

- Integrate AI tools into standard operating procedures and performance metrics.
- Establish ongoing training and support programs to sustain adoption.[^2]


#### 3. Monitor and Report ROI

- Continuously track ROI, productivity gains, and compliance improvements across all departments.
- Share success stories and metrics with executive leadership to secure buy-in for further investment.[^1][^2]

***

### Continuous Improvement and Governance

Ensure long-term success by establishing governance structures, monitoring risks, and planning for future enhancements.

#### 1. Establish Governance and Risk Mitigation

- Set clear usage policies, monitor costs, and adjust provisioning as needed.
- Conduct quarterly ROI reviews to ensure value realization and address emerging risks.[^2]


#### 2. Plan for Future Enhancements

- Explore advanced features like multi-year TCO modeling, integration ecosystem visualizers, and customizable whitepaper builders.
- Stay updated on new AI capabilities and platform updates to maintain competitive advantage.[^2]


#### 3. Foster a Culture of Innovation

- Encourage departments to experiment with new AI use cases and share success stories.
- Position your organization as a leader in AI-driven transformation.[^1][^2]
