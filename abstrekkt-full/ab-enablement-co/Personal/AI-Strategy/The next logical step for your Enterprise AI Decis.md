## Implementation Playbook: Enterprise AI Pilot Execution

### Phase 1: Pilot Preparation (Weeks 1-2)

#### Department Selection and Scoping

**Immediate Actions:**

- Convene stakeholder meetings with Compliance, Sales, and HR leadership to identify specific pain points and establish success criteria
- Define 2-3 concrete use cases per pilot department with clear before/after metrics
- Secure executive sponsorship and resource commitments (time, budget, personnel)

**Documentation Requirements:**

- Create department-specific success metrics dashboards
- Establish baseline measurement protocols (time logs, error tracking, cycle time documentation)
- Draft pilot participation agreements with clear expectations and timelines


#### Technical Setup

**Platform Configuration:**

- Provision accounts for Claude, Perplexity, ChatGPT, or Copilot based on department needs
- Configure SSO/authentication integration with existing corporate identity systems
- Set up usage tracking and analytics infrastructure
- Establish data governance and access control policies

**Compliance and Security:**

- Complete security reviews and data handling assessments
- Document information classification policies for AI tool usage
- Establish acceptable use policies and training requirements
- Configure audit logging and monitoring

***

### Phase 2: Champion Training and Onboarding (Weeks 2-3)

#### Champion Identification

**Selection Criteria:**

- Technical aptitude and willingness to learn new tools
- Influence and respect within department
- Availability for 3-5 hours/week during pilot period
- Strong communication and teaching skills

**Training Program:**

- **Week 2:** 2-hour interactive workshop covering platform basics, prompt engineering fundamentals, and use-case walkthroughs
- **Week 3:** Department-specific deep-dives with custom prompts, templates, and workflow integration
- Provide champion resource kit: quick reference guides, troubleshooting documentation, escalation contacts


#### End-User Rollout

**Onboarding Approach:**

- Host 60-minute department kickoff sessions demonstrating specific use cases
- Distribute role-based quick-start guides (2-page maximum, visual-heavy)
- Schedule daily 15-minute "office hours" during first two weeks
- Create Slack/Teams channel for real-time support and knowledge sharing

***

### Phase 3: Active Pilot Execution (Weeks 4-10)

#### Weekly Monitoring Protocol

**Metrics Collection:**

- Track daily/weekly usage statistics (login frequency, query volume, feature adoption)
- Log time savings estimates from pilot participants (weekly self-reporting)
- Document specific examples of high-value outputs (proposals generated, research completed, analyses produced)
- Monitor support tickets and user questions to identify friction points

**Check-In Cadence:**

- Weekly champion sync (30 minutes) to review progress, address blockers, share wins
- Bi-weekly stakeholder updates with quantitative and qualitative insights
- Monthly executive briefing with ROI projections and early results


#### Rapid Iteration Cycle

**Continuous Improvement:**

- Review feedback weekly and prioritize workflow adjustments
- Update prompt templates and guidance based on user challenges
- Expand or refine use cases based on what's working
- Share success stories and best practices across pilot groups

***

### Phase 4: Evaluation and Scale Planning (Weeks 11-12)

#### Comprehensive Assessment

**Data Analysis:**

- Calculate actual time savings across all use cases
- Compute ROI based on labor hours saved, error reduction, and output quality improvement
- Compare against baseline metrics established in Week 1
- Document qualitative benefits (employee satisfaction, strategic work time, innovation opportunities)

**Post-Pilot Survey:**

- 10-question survey covering ease of use, productivity impact, satisfaction, and scale readiness
- Conduct 30-minute interviews with champions and power users
- Gather recommendations for broader rollout


#### Scale Readiness Assessment

**Evaluation Framework:**

- **Success Criteria:** Did pilots meet 70%+ of target metrics?
- **Adoption Rate:** Are 60%+ of pilot users actively engaged?
- **Sustainability:** Can current support model scale to additional departments?
- **ROI Validation:** Is payback period tracking to projections (3-6 months)?

***

### Phase 5: Departmental Expansion (Months 4-12)

#### Rollout Prioritization

**Next Wave Departments:**

1. **Finance:** Focus on financial analysis, reporting automation, and audit support using Claude or Gemini
2. **Legal:** Deploy for contract review, legal research, and document drafting using Claude or Perplexity
3. **IT/Operations:** Implement for documentation, incident response, and workflow optimization using Copilot or Gemini

**Staged Approach:**

- Launch 2-3 departments per quarter
- Apply lessons learned from initial pilots
- Leverage trained champions as peer mentors for new departments


#### Institutionalization Strategy

**Standard Operating Procedures:**

- Integrate AI tools into role-specific job descriptions and performance expectations
- Create formal training curriculum for new hires
- Establish monthly "AI Innovation Hour" for knowledge sharing and experimentation
- Build library of validated prompts, templates, and workflows

**Support Infrastructure:**

- Designate internal AI adoption lead (0.5-1.0 FTE)
- Create tiered support model (self-service, champion network, specialist escalation)
- Establish quarterly training refreshers and advanced workshops

***

### Phase 6: Governance and Continuous Improvement (Ongoing)

#### Governance Framework

**Policies and Oversight:**

- Establish AI Steering Committee with cross-functional representation
- Implement quarterly usage and cost reviews
- Conduct semi-annual security and compliance audits
- Monitor vendor roadmaps and evaluate new capabilities

**Risk Management:**

- Track and mitigate emerging risks (data leakage, over-reliance, quality drift)
- Establish incident response protocols for AI-related issues
- Maintain competitive intelligence on alternative platforms


#### Innovation Pipeline

**Future Enhancement Roadmap:**

- **Q1 Year 2:** Advanced analytics dashboard with cross-department benchmarking
- **Q2 Year 2:** Custom GPT/Claude Project development for specialized workflows
- **Q3 Year 2:** API integration with core enterprise systems (CRM, ERP, HRIS)
- **Q4 Year 2:** Pilot local LLM deployment for sensitive workflows
