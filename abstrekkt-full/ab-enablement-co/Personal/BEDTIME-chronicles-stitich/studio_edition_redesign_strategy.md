# Research & Redesign Strategy: My Sticker Room (Studio Edition)

## Competitive Insights (2025-2026 Cozy Meta)
Based on research into titles like *Cats & Soup*, *Secret Cat Forest*, and *Ades's Meadow*, the following features are trending and highly effective for retention:
- **Digital Terrarium & Slow-Craft:** Deliberate real-time delays for crafting/growing to reduce burnout.
- **Environmental Synchronicity:** Using local weather/time data to trigger rare in-game spawns (e.g., "Rainy Day Visitors").
- **Collaborative Kindness:** Asynchronous social features like "Silent Gifting" or "Ghost Watering."
- **Tactile Intimacy (Knolling):** High-fidelity, multi-sensory feedback for arranging objects.

## Redesign Goals for Studio Edition
1. **Weather-Dynamic Interfaces:** UI that shifts lighting and asset availability based on the player's local weather.
2. **Slow-Craft Stations:** New diegetic mini-games for "Pulp Pressing" and "Ink Distillation" that utilize real-time countdowns.
3. **The Alchemist's Nook:** A new specialized room for synthesizing rare resin stickers using bioluminescent flora.
4. **Social Address Book 2.0:** Expanding the friend list into a "Collaborative Kindness" hub for silent item trades.

## Proposed New Suite
- **The Alchemist's Nook:** A dark, atmospheric lab for "Sticker Distillation."
- **The Paper Mill:** A bright, tactile station for "Pulp Pressing" (Handmade Paper stickers).
- **The Conservatory:** A specialized greenhouse for growing bioluminescent seeds.
