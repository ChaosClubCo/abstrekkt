# Product Requirements Document: My Sticker Room (Desktop Expansion & Rebrand)

## Vision
Transform the mobile "sticker-to-object" cozy decorator into a high-fidelity desktop experience. The desktop version will emphasize the "Digital Diorama" and "Creative Journaling" aspects, utilizing the larger screen space for a "Sticker Desktop" aesthetic where the UI feels like physical stationery spread across a workspace.

## Rebrand & Design Direction
- **Name:** *My Sticker Room: Studio Edition*
- **Visual Style:** "Tactile Stationery" – Paper textures, washi tape accents, hand-drawn 2.5D illustrations, and soft pastel palettes (lavender, mint, peach).
- **Desktop Adaptation:** Move from vertical-first to a wide "Workbench" layout. Menus will appear as sticker sheets, notebooks, or polaroids rather than traditional mobile overlays.
- **Key Mechanic:** The "Soft Grid" allows for organic, messy-chic furniture placement.

## Proposed Screen List
1. **The Grand Studio (Main Room):** The primary room view, now expanded for wide displays. Features a "Hidden HUD" that fades out to create a live wallpaper effect.
2. **The Sticker Journal (Collection):** A multi-page scrapbook UI where players manage their sticker inventory and track "Cat Attraction" combinations.
3. **The Neighborhood Map (Cat City):** A sprawling, isometric world map for exploring different districts, visiting friend's rooms, and unlocking regional stickers.
4. **The Gacha Boutique (Shop):** A diegetic storefront UI modeled after a physical stationery shop for purchasing sticker packs and lucky wheels.
5. **The Creative Suite (Settings & Profile):** A personalized desk surface where settings are accessed via an in-game planner/diary.

## UI/UX Enhancements
- **Diegetic Menus:** Settings are on a "Clock" or "Planner," Friend List is an "Address Book."
- **Expanded Real Estate:** Sidebars for quick-access sticker sheets during decoration.
- **Enhanced Graphics:** Higher resolution line-work and more complex shading for furniture.
