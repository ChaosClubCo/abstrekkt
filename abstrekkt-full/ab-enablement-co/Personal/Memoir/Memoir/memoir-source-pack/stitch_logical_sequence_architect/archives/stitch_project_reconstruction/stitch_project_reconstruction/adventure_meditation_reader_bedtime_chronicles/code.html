Visualizing: The Crystal Glade

The stars above began to pulse in unison with your breath. As you stand at the edge of the velvet horizon, the air smells of night-blooming jasmine and distant rain. Every inhale brings a soft, lavender light into your lungs, and every exhale releases the weight of the day. Ahead of you, the path diverges into two realms of wonder...

You feel a gentle hum beneath your feet, as if the earth itself is dreaming. To your left, a range of peaks carved from obsidian and moonlight pierces the clouds. To your right, a lake of liquid silver reflects a thousand forgotten constellations, its surface as smooth as a mirror.
