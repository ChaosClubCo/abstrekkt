The Whispering Woods

Chapter 1

The silver leaves of the Whispering Woods rustled, a sound more like an exhale than a breeze. Elara stepped beneath the ancient canopy, her lantern casting long, golden shadows that seemed to dance just out of sight. Here, the boundary between the waking world and the dreamscape was thin—as fragile as spun glass.

She followed the winding path, guided only by the faint, luminescent moss that clung to the roots of the elder trees. With every step, the air grew cooler, smelling of ozone and crushed lavender. The forest was awake, watching her with silent, shimmering eyes hidden deep within the foliage.

"Show yourself," Elara whispered, the words slipping from her lips like mist. She held her lantern higher.

From the darkness ahead, a soft glow began to pulse. It wasn't the harsh light of fire, nor the cold gleam of moonlight, but a warm, ambient radiance that felt like a memory of summer. A creature, small and ethereal, stepped into the light. Its coat resembled woven starlight, and its eyes held the deep, infinite mystery of a nebula.

The creature didn't speak, but Elara heard a melody in her mind—a lullaby forgotten by time. It turned, padding softly deeper into the woods, leaving a trail of shimmering dust in its wake. Without hesitation, she followed, letting the story unfold as the woods whispered her name.

For hours they walked, the trees twisting into impossible, beautiful shapes. The deeper they went, the more the forest resembled the night sky itself. Constellations seemed to form in the branches above, guiding their way to the heart of the sanctuary.
