Implementation Rules

Core system behaviors for the ABSTREKKT visual language.

The Fire Rule

Fire is a transition, never a decoration. It must reveal structure. Once the ember burns the parchment, the resulting dark navy interface must be immutable.

Motion Decay

All movement follows mathematical precision. No "bouncing" or "elastic" ease. Use cubic-beziers that feel heavy, mechanical, and architectural.

Easter Egg Logic

Labels like "Built to Last" must be rendered at 9px to 11px, anchored to structural borders at 40% opacity. They are for the curious, not the casual.
