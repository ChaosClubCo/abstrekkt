Tier 1 Delivery
AI Launchpad — Interview Protocol
2–3 week fixed-scope diagnostic. Run these questions across 3–5 stakeholder interviews. Aim for 45 min per session. Goal: surface the highest-value, lowest-risk AI use cases and build a prioritized backlog.
Before the first call: Confirm scope (single dept vs multi), number of interview participants, and who owns AI decisions internally. If no internal champion exists, flag this before signing.
Pre-Engagement Checklist
  • Signed SOW and deposit received
  • Kickoff call scheduled with internal champion
  • Stakeholder list confirmed (name, role, department)
  • Calendar access or scheduling link sent to all participants
  • Client NDA on file if required
  • Data handling agreement in place if sensitive data will be discussed
  • Output format agreed (slide deck, written report, or both)
Exec / Sponsor Interview (30–45 min)
1
Business context
What are the 2–3 biggest operational drags on the business right now? Where is time being lost that shouldn't be? What does the team spend time on that doesn't require human judgment?
2
AI appetite
Have you tried any AI tools so far (Copilot, ChatGPT, others)? What happened? What made you decide to bring in outside help rather than figure it out internally?
3
Success criteria
If this engagement goes exactly right, what changes 6 months from now? How would you know it worked? What metric or outcome would you point to?
4
Constraints
Are there specific systems, data sources, or vendors that are off-limits? Any compliance requirements (HIPAA, SOC2, state-level data laws) we need to work within?
5
Budget signal
Have you allocated budget for implementation after the discovery? Is this a board-level decision or something you can move on at the department level?
Operations Lead Interview (45 min)
1
Workflow mapping
Walk me through a typical day. What are the 3 most repetitive tasks your team handles? Which ones have the most room for error? Which ones take the most time per occurrence?
2
Data audit
What information does the team regularly produce or consume? Where does it live (spreadsheets, CRM, email, shared drives)? Is it structured or mostly unstructured?
3
Tool landscape
What tools does the team use every day? Which ones are already on M365 / Google Workspace? Any tools that have APIs or automation capabilities that aren't being used?
4
Pain point ranking
If you could eliminate one workflow bottleneck tomorrow, what would it be? What about second and third? Who besides you feels this most acutely?
5
Change appetite
How has the team responded to tool changes in the past? Is there anyone who would be skeptical of AI tools? What would make them more willing to try?
IT / Technical Lead Interview (30 min)
1
Stack and access
What cloud providers are in use? Who owns Microsoft licensing? Is MFA enforced? Do you have DLP or data classification policies in place?
2
Data sensitivity
What's the most sensitive data type in the organization? What controls exist today? Are there any systems where external API calls to an LLM would be a non-starter?
3
Integration constraints
Are there legacy systems that can't be integrated? Any SOWs or vendor agreements that restrict data portability? What does the approval process look like for new SaaS tools?

Social Proof
Case Study Framework
Build one of these after every completed AI Deploy engagement. Even anonymized, it is the highest-credibility sales asset you have. Two complete case studies is enough to close Phase 2 outreach without warm referrals.
Permission first: Ask the client during the Deploy close whether they're open to a named or anonymized reference. Get it in writing (email confirmation is fine). Anonymized is almost always a yes — named reference requires more trust.
Structure (follow this order)
1
The company in one line
Industry, size, what they do. No jargon. "A 120-person regional logistics company" or "[COMPANY NAME], a professional services firm in the Midwest."
2
The problem (before state)
One paragraph. Specific — not "they had AI challenges." "The operations team was spending 8–10 hours/week manually formatting reports from 4 different data sources." Use their words where possible.
3
What we did
2–3 sentences on the approach. What engagements were run, what was deployed. No methodology jargon — plain language about what actually happened.
4
The result (after state)
The single most compelling measurable outcome. "8 hours/week recovered across the ops team — that's $41,600/year in recovered capacity at their loaded staff cost." If no number is available, a direct client quote.
5
The quote (if named)
One sentence from the internal champion. This is worth more than any paragraph you write. If anonymous, skip.
