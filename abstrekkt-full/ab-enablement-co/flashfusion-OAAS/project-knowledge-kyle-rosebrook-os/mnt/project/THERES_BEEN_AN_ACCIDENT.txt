THERES BEEN AN ACCIDENT

[Verse 1 – Mom]
There’s a knock that doesn’t belong to this hour
The kind that bends the hallway light
Your shoes still by the door where you left them
Like you’re coming home tonight

He won’t look me in the eyes
Says my name like it’s breaking glass
I feel my heart fall through the floor
Before he even asks me to sit down

[Pre-Chorus – Mom]
I’m counting words instead of breaths
Accident
Hospital
Critical
God, please don’t let this be it

[Chorus – Both Parents]
They said there’s been an accident
And time just stopped obeying
Every future I was saving for you
Started shaking
If love could pull you back from the edge
You’d be standing here instead
So I’m begging every ceiling, every star
Please don’t take our kid

[Verse 2 – Dad]
I keep my voice steady like that means something
Like fathers are built for this
I nod like I understand the sentences
But I don’t understand any of it

I picture you small, asleep in the backseat
Your hand wrapped around my thumb
I’d trade every year I have left
Just to give you one more morning sun

[Pre-Chorus – Dad]
I sign forms I don’t remember reading
Say thank you to the man who broke our world
I’d carry this pain forever
If it means you get to stay our child

[Chorus – Both Parents]
They said there’s been an accident
And the walls started closing in
I’d crawl through fire, I’d beg on my knees
Just to hear you breathe again
If love could pull you back from the edge
You’d be standing here instead
So I’m begging every ceiling, every star
Please don’t take our kid

[Bridge – Mom (quiet, breaking)]
I found you once…
Cold skin, fading light
Shaking hands learned CPR
From pure terror and instinct

I screamed your name like it was oxygen
Like it could restart your heart
I don’t know how many miracles I get
But I’m not done asking God

[Bridge – Dad (underneath, spoken or half-sung)]
You scared us twice and we still stayed
That’s not weakness, that’s love
We don’t need perfect
We just need you alive

[Final Chorus – Full, restrained power]
They said there’s been an accident
But you’re still here somehow
Every breath you steal back from the dark
Is a promise now
If love could pull you back from the edge
Then maybe this is proof
You’re still our kid, you’re still our heart
And we’re not letting go of you


Mom:
Come home… even if it’s slow
