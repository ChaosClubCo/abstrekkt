# Energy Tracking & Kinsley Time Protection

**Purpose**: Monitor burnout before it happens + protect non-negotiable family time  
**Timeline**: Start immediately (today)  
**Tools**: Phone note app OR Notion database (whichever is faster)

---

## Why This Matters

### The Burnout Pattern (Research-Backed)

```
Week 1: Normal → Energy 7/10
Week 2: Busy → Energy 6.5/10
Week 3: Stressed → Energy 6/10  ← Alert threshold
Week 4: Overwhelmed → Energy 5/10  ← Warning
Week 5: Exhausted → Energy <5/10  ← Intervention needed
Week 6: Burnout → Everything stops  ← Too late
```

**The insight**: You can see burnout coming 2-3 weeks early if you track it.

**The solution**: Energy log + simple alerts = catch it before it happens.

### Kinsley Time Pattern (Prevention)

```
Week 1: Protected Friday → Quality 8/10 ✅
Week 2: Work creeps in → Quality 7/10 ⚠️
Week 3: More creep → Quality 6/10 🛑
Week 4: Friday lost → Quality 3/10 ❌ (undo all three weeks of work!)
```

**The insight**: Kinsley time erodes slowly until it's gone.

**The solution**: Simple tracking + immediate action = never loses quality.

## Energy Log System

### What is Energy?

Not happiness. Not busy-ness. **Capacity to do good work without resentment.**

- **10/10**: Fresh, focused, excited to work, plenty of buffer
- **8/10**: Good, normal pace, able to handle surprises
- **6/10**: Tired, working harder to stay focused, little buffer
- **5/10**: Exhausted, struggling with focus, burning out
- **<5/10**: Burnout, nothing feels possible, needs intervention

### Daily Energy Log (30 seconds)

**Where**: Phone note app OR Notion (fastest wins)

**Template** (copy and paste each day):

```
[Date]
Morning: [1-10 number]
Stress: [1-10 number]
Sleep: [hours slept]
Win of the day: [1 sentence]
Blocker: [What's hard? Or "None"]
Note: [Anything else?]
```

**Example entry:**

```
Jan 28

Stress: 3
Sleep: 7 hours
Win: Created brag doc + stakeholder map
Blocker: None
Note: Felt productive, good pace

Jan 29

Stress: 4

Blocker: Had to stay late for meeting
Note: A bit tired but manageable

Jan 30
Morning: 6
Stress: 5
Sleep: 6 hours
Win: Automation audit started
Blocker: INT Inc demands unexpected spike
Note: Noticing stress creeping up
```

## Alert System: When to Take Action

### Green Zone: All Good ✅

**Conditions**:
- Energy: 6.5+/10
- Stress: <5/10
- Sleep: 6.5+ hours
- Kinsley quality: 7+/10

**Action**: Keep going, maintain current pace

---

### Yellow Zone: Watch Carefully ⚠️

**Conditions**:
- Energy: 6-6.5/10, OR
- Stress: 5-6/10, OR
- Sleep: 6-6.5 hours, OR
- Kinsley quality: 6-7/10

**Triggers**: This lasts 2+ weeks

**Actions to take**:
1. [ ] Reduce optional activities (pause less-critical projects)
2. [ ] Increase sleep target (aim for 7+ hours)
3. [ ] Talk to manager: "Noticing capacity getting tight, want to discuss priorities"
4. [ ] Review if work is distributing fairly
5. [ ] Protect Kinsley time even more fiercely

**Example response**:
- Pause automations build #2 temporarily (still do #1)
- Reduce content writing by 50%
- Keep INT Inc + FlashFusion MVP on track

---

### Red Zone: Intervention Needed 🛑

**Conditions**:
- Energy: 5-6/10, AND stress: 6+/10, OR
- Sleep: <6 hours for 3+ days, OR
- Kinsley quality: <6/10 for 2+ weeks, OR
- You're skipping exercise/meals/sleep

**Triggers**: This for 3+ days

**Actions to take IMMEDIATELY**:
1. [ ] **PAUSE non-essential projects** (FlashFusion, all side projects)
2. [ ] **Focus INT Inc work only** (don't leave your job hanging)
3. [ ] **Talk to manager immediately**: "I need to dial back on side projects temporarily. Here's why..."
4. [ ] **Protect Kinsley time absolutely** (this is your recovery)
5. [ ] **Sleep target**: 8 hours/night minimum
6. [ ] **Exercise**: 30 min/day (releases stress)

**Example response**:
- All Phase 2-6 projects → pause
- Phase 1 Foundation → keep (it's small time commitment)
- INT Inc → full focus
- Friday → protected family time
- Timeline: Reassess in 2 weeks

---

### Crisis: Burnout 🚨

**Conditions**:
- Energy: <5/10 for 3+ days
- You feel: Cynical, exhausted, like nothing matters
- You're: Avoiding work, sleeping too much or too little, irritable

**Actions to take IMMEDIATELY**:
1. [ ] **Talk to manager**: "I'm burning out. I need to adjust my workload."
2. [ ] **Pause EVERYTHING except INT Inc**
3. [ ] **Consider PTO**: Even 1 week can reset
4. [ ] **See doctor**: Check if health issue (sleep apnea, depression, etc.)
5. [ ] **Therapy**: Talk to someone professional
6. [ ] **Don't quit or make big decisions**: Wait until you recover

**Support**:
- Partner/family: Tell them what you need
- Manager: Be transparent about struggles
- Doctor: Rule out health issues
- Therapist: Process the stress

**Timeline**: Recovery takes 2-4 weeks minimum. Be patient with yourself.

## Kinsley Time Quality Tracking

### What is Quality?

Not duration (always Friday). **Presence + Connection**.

- **10/10**: Fully present, engaged, fun activity together, no work thoughts
- **8/10**: Mostly present, one work check-in, enjoyed time
- **6/10**: Partially present, some work worry, activity was ok
- **4/10**: Distracted by work, work call during, activity felt rushed
- **<4/10**: Missed time or heavily interrupted, no quality

### Friday Kinsley Log (5 minutes, Friday evening after time together)

**Template**:

```
[Date] Friday

Quality score: [1-10]

What we did: [Activity, learning, building]

My presence: [Fully present / Mostly present / Partially distracted / Distracted / Missed]

Work interruptions: [None / 1 check-in / Multiple / Constant]

How it felt: [Sentence or two]

Time protected: [Fully / Mostly / Partially / Not really]

Notes: [Any obstacles? How to protect better next week?]
```

**Examples:**

```
Jan 29 Friday (Good)

Quality score: 8/10

What we did: Built a React component for a learning app together

My presence: Fully present

Work interruptions: None (had out-of-office on, phone silent)

How it felt: She asked great questions, I loved building with her. She's really thinking like an engineer.

Time protected: Fully

Notes: Left the office by 3 PM, no temptation to check Slack.

---

Feb 5 Friday (Needs Work)

Quality score: 5/10

What we did: Started building but interrupted

How it felt: Rushed, kept thinking about the INT Inc meeting on Monday

Time protected: Partially

Notes: Started work at 3 PM (should have 3 PM hard stop). Next week: actual PTO block, phone in another room.
```

## FAQ

**Q: Won't this add MORE tracking burden?**  
A: No. Daily takes 30 seconds (faster than checking Slack once). Friday takes 5-10 minutes total. Less time than one meeting.

**Q: What if I forget to log?**  
A: Estimate at end of week. It's not about perfect data, it's about pattern recognition. Even rough estimates reveal trends.

**Q: What if Kinsley quality is always lower on Fridays because I'm tired?**  
A: That's the point. If you're too tired to be present, you need to change something (work less, sleep more, work differently). The tracking reveals the problem.

**Q: What if I'm just naturally low energy?**  
A: Track baseline for 4 weeks, then look for trends. Natural variation is fine. Problem is when it's trending down or staying low.

**Q: Can I do this in a spreadsheet instead of phone notes?**  
A: Sure, but phone notes are faster. You're more likely to log if it takes 30 seconds vs 2 minutes opening a spreadsheet.
