## Communication Frameworks

### STAR-L Framework (Use in 1:1s & conversations)

A proven way to communicate impact:

```
S - SITUATION
  "Here's the context you need to understand..."
  Example: "The platform team was spending 30 min/week on ticket triage"

T - TASK
  "Here's what I was responsible for..."
  Example: "I owned the automation to streamline this process"

A - ACTION
  "Here's what I did..."
  Example: "I built a triage workflow in n8n that auto-tags + route tickets"

R - RESULT
  "Here's what happened..."
  Example: "Now takes 10 min/week. Team saved 20 hours/week across all tools"

L - LESSON
  "Here's what it means..."
  Example: "This is how I think about solving problems: find where time is wasted, automate it, measure impact"
```

**Practice**: Use STAR-L in your next 3 conversations. It makes impact clear + memorable.

---

### The "Ask" Framework (Use when you need something)

```
CONTEXT: "As you know, I've been working on [X]..."

PROGRESS: "So far, we've [accomplished Y], which has [helped Z people/saved X hours]"

ASK: "I need your [input / support / approval] on [specific thing]"

BENEFIT: "This will [benefit your team / move us toward / solve the problem of]"

WHEN: "I'm hoping to get this by [date]"
```

**Example**:
"As you know, I'm building automation systems to improve team efficiency. So far, I've created 3 key workflows that have saved our team 15+ hours per week. I need your buy-in on the AI governance framework I'm drafting—this will help standardize how we approach AI across our org. I'm hoping to get your feedback by end of February."

## Red Flags & Fixes

**Flag**: Stakeholder seems distant / unresponsive
**Fix**: Back off, don't force it. Focus on manager + one exec sponsor instead.

**Flag**: You realize you're annoying someone with too many updates
**Fix**: Reduce frequency. Switch to monthly instead of weekly.

**Flag**: Manager seems unsupportive of your goals
**Fix**: Discuss directly in 1:1: "I want to make sure we're aligned on my growth. What would success look like?"

**Flag**: No one outside your immediate team knows your work
**Fix**: Share monthly updates. Write KB articles. Speak up in meetings.
