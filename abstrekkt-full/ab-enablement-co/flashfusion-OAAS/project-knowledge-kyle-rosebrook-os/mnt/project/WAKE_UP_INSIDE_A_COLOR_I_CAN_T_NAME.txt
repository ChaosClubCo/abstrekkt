WAKE UP INSIDE A COLOR I CAN'T NAME

[Movement I – Clean, Odd Meter, Dreamlike]

Wake up inside a color I can’t name
Time bends sideways when I blink
The clock speaks in fractions
And I swear it knows my face

A choir hums in broken numbers
Every note misplaced on purpose
I’m not lost, I’m everywhere at once
And nowhere feels the same

[Movement II – Abrupt Shift, Screamed, Technical]

TEAR THE GRID FROM THE SKY
MEASURE GOD IN DECIMALS
WE BUILT A WORLD FROM SYMBOLS
THEN DROWNED IN THE TRANSLATION

I swallowed the map
And choked on direction
Every answer fractures
Into ten more questions

[Refrain – Clean, Unsettling, Melodic]

If this is truth, why does it feel rehearsed?
If I’m awake, why does it hurt like sleep?

[Movement III – Jazzy Groove, Spoken / Clean]

Step lightly
The floor is thinking

Walls inhale
Exhale expectation

I asked the mirror for forgiveness
It handed me a script and said
“Improvise.”

[Movement IV – Chaos Section, Screamed, Rapid Fire]

I TRIED TO PRAY
BUT THE WORDS KEPT SWINGING
TIME SIGNATURES
AROUND MY NECK

MATH IS JUST POETRY
THAT FORGOT HOW TO FEEL
COUNT IT OUT
MISCOUNT IT
REPEAT

[Break – Sudden Silence → Piano / Clean]

I remember being simple
Back when silence didn’t scream
Before every thought collapsed
Into a theory of me

[Movement V – Epic Build, Clean → Harsh Harmony]

Clean:
Lay me down in shifting keys
Let me rest between the beats

Screamed (layered):
Bury me in color
I’ll come back redesigned

[Finale – Grand, Theatrical, Resolving]

I am not the sum of my control
I am the mistake that learned to grow
Every fracture sings in harmony
When I stop demanding certainty

[Outro – Clean, Resolute]

Close the book
Not the story
