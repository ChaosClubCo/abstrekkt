MIRRORS AND MUZZLE FLASHES

[Intro – radio static, distant siren, metallic hits]
(Static)
Ain’t no window in this house…
Just mirrors and muzzle flashes.

[Verse 1 – rap, tight and cold]
Atop the world, hard to see the cards
When the table get flipped before you get the chance to start
Crown on a ghost, throne made of debt
They smile for the camera then they place another bet
Throw down your hand, hear the crowd complain
Same pain recycled, different name
Learn to stay calm with the storm in your chest
Pray with your jaw clenched, pass the test
They call and they bluff, pressin’ your switch
Tryna make you swing while they playin’ rich
Unshakable face, let the pressure ring
Metal in the drum like a funeral hymn

[Pre-Chorus – rising, more melodic]
Open the books, read the cost
Dead walk through what we lost
Say “justice” like it means somethin’
But it’s smoke when the sirens comin’

[Chorus – shouted/melodic, anthemic]
NO WINDOW, NO WAY OUT (hey!)
Cards stacked, lights blacked out (hey!)
Royal flush in a rigged house
Still they tell us “kneel down”
NO WINDOW, NO WAY OUT (hey!)
Blood on the deal, hands out (hey!)
Say a prayer, hold your ground
Royal flush but the deck’s locked down

[Verse 2 – harder, industrial imagery]
Once was a man, now a number on paper
If that wasn’t enough, they just raise the danger
Impossible odds, we carry the weight
While the rich burn the poor at the edge of the gate
Ancestors still got a say in my hands
They feel every chain in the modern plans
Green paper gospel, sweet smell of rot
They kneel to the money then forget what they bought
Plutonium prayers, hydro-bomb hymns
Spears in the sky where the future begins
Vets in the rearview, backs on the line
Prosthetic hope and a war-time mind

[Bridge – spoken, minimal beat, heavy sub swell]
No restarts in real life.
Not for the average kid.
Was it school… or skin… or the name they gave you…
When they put a bullet through the window?

[Verse 3 – most intense, percussive delivery]
Young black boy, brick through the glass
Headline fades but the trauma lasts
Fatherless math, mother in rage
Brother in silence, sister off stage
See the day when the bullet-time comes
Might’ve been yours if the dice got spun
Grey goose nights, white collars grin
Shake hands clean while the blood sinks in
Street truth talk, cameras blink
They sell the fear then they sell the “fix”
I seen the smile on the wolf’s mouth
When they said “calm down” in a burning house

[Final Chorus – bigger, add ad-libs]
NO WINDOW, NO WAY OUT (no way!)
Cards stacked, lights blacked out (all day!)
Royal flush in a rigged house
Still they tell us “kneel down”
NO WINDOW, NO WAY OUT (no way!)
Blood on the deal, hands out (hands out!)
Say a prayer, hold your ground
Royal flush but the deck’s locked down

[Outro – siren fades, metal hits, static]
Dollars and that Houdini trick…
White-leaf line make the sweetest bleed…
(Static)
No window.
