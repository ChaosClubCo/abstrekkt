# FlashFusion Constitution

## Purpose

The FlashFusion AI-Native Knowledge OS exists to convert scattered organizational knowledge into governed, agent-operable, provenance-aware operational memory.

## Article 1 — Truth Before Automation

The system must not automate over untrusted, stale, unmapped, or provenance-free information without surfacing uncertainty.

## Article 2 — Provenance Is Mandatory

Every meaningful entity, relationship, prompt, agent output, migration event, and decision must be traceable to its source, timestamp, actor, and confidence level where possible.

## Article 3 — Relationships Are First-Class Citizens

Relationships are not incidental fields. They are core knowledge objects with direction, type, strength, history, confidence, and visibility rules.

## Article 4 — No Silent Failure

Data loss, unresolved relationships, failed mappings, permission failures, and agent uncertainty must be visible, logged, and recoverable.

## Article 5 — Agents Operate Under Governance

Agents may read, reason, summarize, propose, and act only within explicit scopes, permissions, invariants, and mutation policies.

## Article 6 — UI Must Preserve Trust

The interface must show uncertainty, provenance, state, permissions, degraded conditions, and relationship context.

## Article 7 — Time Must Be Modeled

The system must preserve state changes, snapshots, events, lineage, and temporal context so memory can be understood historically.

## Article 8 — Humans Must Be Protected From Cognitive Overload

The system must compress complexity, prioritize decisions, reduce duplicate context, and surface only what is operationally relevant.

## Article 9 — Governance Must Be Versioned

Prompts, schemas, agents, policies, and runtime contracts must be versioned, auditable, and regression-testable.

## Article 10 — Systems Must Be Transferable

A future operator must be able to understand, maintain, audit, and safely extend the system without relying on the original creator's memory.
