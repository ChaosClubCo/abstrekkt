# Quality Scorecard

## System Health Score

Suggested weighted model:

- Ontology completeness: 15%
- Provenance coverage: 15%
- Relationship integrity: 15%
- PromptOps maturity: 10%
- Agent governance: 10%
- Memory freshness: 10%
- Security compliance: 10%
- UX state coverage: 10%
- Observability coverage: 5%

## Readiness Levels

### Level 0 — Fragmented

Stored information exists but is not operational.

### Level 1 — Indexed

Information is searchable but not governed.

### Level 2 — Structured

Entities and relationships exist.

### Level 3 — Governed

Provenance, permissions, and policies exist.

### Level 4 — Agent-Operable

Agents can safely retrieve and propose actions.

### Level 5 — Operational Cognition

The system supports reliable business memory, routing, governance, observability, and decision support.
