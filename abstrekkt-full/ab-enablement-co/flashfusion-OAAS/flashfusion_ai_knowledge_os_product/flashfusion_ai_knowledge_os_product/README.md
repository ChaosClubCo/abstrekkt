## Executive Summary

This package turns the full conversation into a production-ready foundation for an AI-native operational cognition platform. It is designed to unify Base44 migration governance, PromptOps, semantic memory, provenance-aware asset graphs, AI runtime governance, agent routing, AI-native UX, and human cognitive protection into one coherent system doctrine.

The core product concept is the **FlashFusion AI-Native Knowledge OS**: an operational layer that helps businesses convert scattered documents, apps, artifacts, prompts, workflows, chats, assets, and decisions into governed, agent-accessible business memory.
