Onboarding Checklist
First 14 days

Day 0: Kickoff

  • Send welcome email with team introductions
  • Schedule kickoff call with all stakeholders
  • Share project charter and success criteria
  • Set up shared communication channel (Slack/Teams)

Week 1: Foundation

  • Complete discovery documentation
  • Conduct stakeholder interviews
  • Finalize project timeline and milestones
  • Establish weekly status meeting cadence
  • Complete technical environment access

Week 2: Momentum

  • Deliver first working prototype/POC
  • Conduct initial user training session
  • Document early wins and share with sponsor
  • Complete 2-week check-in with executive sponsor

Adoption Playbooks
Driving user engagement

🟢 Low Adoption (<30%)

Users not actively engaging with implemented solutions

  • Schedule 1:1s with power users to understand blockers
  • Create quick-win use case workshops
  • Implement "lunch and learn" sessions
  • Share peer success stories
  • Simplify access and reduce friction

🟡 Medium Adoption (30-70%)

Partial engagement, some champions emerging

  • Identify and enable champions
  • Create department-specific use cases
  • Measure and share productivity gains
  • Address specific team objections
  • Gamify adoption with leaderboards

🟢 High Adoption (>70%)

Strong engagement, ready for expansion

  • Document success for case study
  • Explore advanced use cases
  • Connect champion network
  • Plan expansion to other departments
  • Request referrals and testimonials
💡

Pro Tip: The Champion Strategy

Identify 2-3 enthusiastic users early. Invest extra time with them. Their success stories become your most powerful adoption tool—peer influence beats top-down mandates every time.

Value Documentation
Proving ROI

ROI Calculation Framework

Capture both quantitative and qualitative value:

💰 Hard Savings

Measurable cost reductions

  • Time saved (hours × hourly rate)
  • FTEs avoided or redirected
  • Error reduction costs
  • Vendor consolidation
  • Infrastructure savings

📈 Revenue Impact

Top-line improvements

  • Sales cycle acceleration
  • Lead conversion improvement
  • Customer retention lift
  • New capability revenue
  • Market speed advantage

✨ Strategic Value

Competitive advantages

  • Employee satisfaction
  • Innovation capability
  • Risk reduction
  • Compliance improvement
  • Brand perception

Quarterly Business Review Agenda

  • Review: Progress against original success criteria (15 min)
  • Validate: ROI calculations with actual data (15 min)
  • Celebrate: Wins and recognize champions (10 min)
  • Plan: Next quarter roadmap and priorities (15 min)
  • Explore: Expansion opportunities (10 min)

Expansion Playbook
Growing the partnership

Expansion Signals

🎯

Look for These Triggers

High adoption rates (>70%), executive praise, requests for additional training, mentions of other departments asking about AI, budget planning discussions, organizational changes creating new opportunities.

Expansion Opportunities

Type Trigger Approach
Department Expansion Success in initial department Propose pilot for adjacent team
Use Case Expansion High adoption of initial use cases Present advanced use case roadmap
Managed Services Project completion + desire for ongoing Transition to retainer model
Training Programs New hires or capability gaps Propose training subscription
Governance/Security Scaling creates risk concerns Governance framework engagement

Referral Request Script

After a Successful QBR

"[Name], I'm really pleased with the progress we've made together—particularly [specific achievement]. It sounds like you're seeing real value."

"I'm curious—do you know any peers at other companies who might be facing similar challenges? We've found that warm introductions lead to the best partnerships, and I'd love to help others achieve what you have."

"No pressure at all, but if anyone comes to mind, I'd be grateful for an introduction."

Health Score Framework
Early warning system
Healthy (3.5-4.0) High adoption, strong engagement, expansion discussions
At Risk (2.5-3.4) Declining adoption, missed meetings, slower responses
Critical (<2.5) Disengagement, complaints, sponsor changes, budget concerns

Risk Response Playbooks

🔴 Executive Sponsor Change

New sponsor may not understand value

  • Request immediate intro meeting
  • Prepare value summary document
  • Identify internal champion to advocate
  • Offer "state of the union" presentation
  • Align on their priorities quickly

🔴 Budget Concerns

Client questioning investment

  • Accelerate ROI documentation
  • Propose payment restructuring
  • Identify quick wins to demonstrate value
  • Escalate to partner for strategic discussion
  • Consider scope adjustment if needed

🔴 Project Delays

Milestones being missed

  • Root cause analysis immediately
  • Reset expectations with sponsor
  • Add resources if internal issue
  • Document client-side blockers
  • Create recovery plan with new dates
🚨

Escalation Triggers

Immediately escalate to Partner/Director if: sponsor departure, budget cut discussions, competitor evaluation, legal/contract disputes, or NPS <6 from key stakeholder.
