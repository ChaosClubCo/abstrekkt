Workshop Overview

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:

  • Understand the current AI landscape and key technologies
  • Identify and prioritize AI use cases for their organization
  • Recognize governance and risk considerations for AI adoption
  • Create an actionable 90-day AI roadmap
  • Align on next steps and resource requirements

👥 Target Audience

👔

Executive Sponsors

C-Suite, VPs who will champion and fund AI initiatives

📊

Business Leaders

Department heads who will implement AI in their teams

💻

IT/Tech Leaders

CTO, IT Directors who will enable and secure AI

🔒

Risk/Compliance

Leaders who will ensure responsible AI adoption

Pre-Workshop Preparation

📋 Facilitator Checklist (1 Week Before)

  • Confirm participant list and roles (8-20 ideal)
  • Send pre-work survey to gather current AI usage data
  • Review organization's industry and competitive landscape
  • Customize examples and case studies for their sector
  • Prepare materials (print handouts, set up digital tools)
  • Test all technology (projector, wifi, collaboration tools)
  • Arrange room setup (U-shape or pods for group work)
  • Confirm catering and breaks
📦

Materials Needed

Printed agendas, sticky notes (4 colors), markers, flip charts, voting dots, laptop/projector, Miro/Mural board access, name tents, participant workbooks

Detailed Facilitation Guide

🎬 Opening (9:00 - 9:30)

Facilitator Script

"Good morning everyone, and thank you for dedicating this time to something that will shape the future of [Company Name]. I'm [Name] from INT Inc., and I'll be facilitating our session today."

"By the end of our four hours together, you'll walk away with three things: a shared understanding of what AI can—and can't—do for your organization, a prioritized list of use cases with real ROI potential, and a concrete 90-day roadmap to get started."

"Before we dive in, let's do quick introductions. Share your name, role, and complete this sentence: 'The one thing I hope AI could do for my team is...'"

💡

Facilitator Tip

Capture the "hopes" on a flip chart. Reference them throughout to show how agenda addresses their goals. This builds buy-in.

🎯 Icebreaker: AI Experience Spectrum
10 min

Purpose: Gauge the room's AI familiarity and create psychological safety

  1. Draw a line on flip chart: "AI Skeptic" ← → "AI Enthusiast"
  2. Have participants place a dot where they fall
  3. Ask 2-3 people from each end to share why they're there
  4. Acknowledge that all perspectives are valuable today
✨

Debrief Point

"Great diversity of perspectives—this is exactly what we need. Skeptics keep us grounded, enthusiasts push us forward."

Module 1: AI Landscape (45 min)

🎯 Module Objectives

  • Demystify AI terminology (LLM, GenAI, ML, etc.)
  • Understand what AI can and cannot do today
  • Learn from success stories and failure patterns

📊 Key Content Points

Topic Time Key Message
AI Terminology 10 min GenAI vs. traditional ML, LLMs explained simply
The Frustration Gap 10 min 88% experimenting, only 6% are high performers
Why Projects Fail 10 min 95% fail: tech-first thinking, no governance, change ignored
Success Stories 10 min 2-3 relevant case studies from their industry
Discussion 5 min Q&A and reactions
Key Talking Point: The 95% Failure Rate

"Here's the uncomfortable truth: MIT research shows 95% of AI pilots never make it to production. Why? Three reasons..."

"First, technology-first thinking—starting with 'let's use AI' instead of 'what problem are we solving.' Second, perpetual pilot syndrome—running experiments forever without a clear path to scale. Third, ignoring the human side—expecting people to just adapt without proper change management."

"The good news? These are all preventable. That's exactly what we're going to address today."

⚠️

Watch For

Participants who want to dive into specific tools. Redirect: "We'll get to tools later—first we need to establish what problems we're solving."

5
Module 2: Use Case Prioritization (60 min)

🎯 Module Objectives

  • Generate 15-25 potential AI use cases
  • Apply RICE framework for prioritization
  • Select top 3-5 use cases for roadmap
💡 Exercise 1: Use Case Brainstorm
20 min

Materials: Sticky notes, markers, flip charts by department

  1. Individual (5 min): Each person writes 3-5 use cases on sticky notes (one per note): "In my area, AI could help with..."
  2. Share (10 min): Go around, each person shares top 2 and posts on wall. Group similar ideas.
  3. Cluster (5 min): Facilitator groups into themes (productivity, customer, operations, etc.)
✨

Prompt Cards

Have cards ready: "What takes too long?", "What's prone to errors?", "What do customers complain about?", "What do employees hate doing?"

📊 Exercise 2: RICE Prioritization
25 min

Framework: RICE Scoring

Factor Question Scale
Reach How many people/processes affected? 1-10
Impact How significant is the improvement? 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3
Confidence How sure are we this will work? 0.5, 0.8, 1.0
Effort How many person-months to implement? 1-10

Score = (R × I × C) / E

  1. Split into 3-4 groups, assign 4-6 use cases each
  2. Groups score each use case using RICE template
  3. Calculate scores, rank within group
  4. Report top 2 back to full room
  5. Full group dot-votes on final top 5
💡

Facilitation Note

If groups get stuck on scoring, suggest: "Don't aim for perfection—aim for relative ranking. Is this higher or lower impact than the others?"

Module 3: Governance & Risk (45 min)

🎯 Module Objectives

  • Understand key AI risks and how to mitigate
  • Define data classification for AI use
  • Align on governance requirements

📊 Key Content

🔐

Security Risks

Data leakage, prompt injection, model extraction

⚖️

Compliance Risks

HIPAA, GDPR, SOC2 requirements for AI

🎭

Ethical Risks

Bias, misinformation, transparency

💼

Operational Risks

Vendor lock-in, cost overruns, quality

🚦 Exercise: Data Classification
15 min

Purpose: Agree on what data can be used with AI tools

  1. Present 4-tier classification: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted
  2. Groups discuss: Which data types fall into each category?
  3. Share back: What surprised you? What needs clarity?
  4. Highlight: Some AI tools OK for Internal, only approved tools for Confidential
Key Message: 5-Layer Defense

"Think of AI security like castle defense. One wall isn't enough. You need guards at the gate (input validation), strong walls (prompt structure), a moat (output filtering), watchtowers (monitoring), and finally, human judgment for the tough calls. Any single layer can fail—you need all five."

7
Module 4: Roadmap Planning (30 min)

🎯 Module Objectives

  • Create 90-day action plan
  • Assign owners to each initiative
  • Define success metrics
🗓️ Exercise: 90-Day Roadmap
20 min

For each of the top 3-5 use cases:

  1. Days 1-30: What needs to happen? (Policy, vendor selection, pilot scope)
  2. Days 31-60: What's the pilot? (Users, success criteria, go/no-go)
  3. Days 61-90: What's the decision? (Scale, iterate, or stop)
  4. Owner: Who is accountable? (Name, not role)
  5. Resources: What's needed? (Budget, people, tools)
⚠️

Push for Specificity

Don't let groups say "IT will handle it." Push for: "Sarah from IT will own the vendor evaluation by March 15."
