🎯 AI Discovery Kit

Qualification • Demo Flow • Objection Handling

🎯 BANT Qualification Framework
  • Budget: "What's your allocated budget for AI/automation initiatives this year?"
    Qualifies financial readiness. Target: $25K+ for meaningful engagement.
    → If unclear: "Are you exploring pilot budgets ($5-15K) or full deployment?"
  • Authority: "Who else would need to be involved in this decision?"
    Maps buying committee. Need: IT + Business + Finance alignment.
    → Follow-up: "Should we include them in our next conversation?"
  • Need: "What's driving your interest in AI right now?"
    Uncovers pain points. Listen for: efficiency, competition, board pressure.
    → Probe: "What would success look like in 6 months?"
  • Timeline: "When are you hoping to have something in place?"
    Creates urgency. Ideal: 30-90 days. Red flag: "exploring for next year."
    → If slow: "What would need to happen to accelerate this?"
🚦 Qualification Scorecard
  • Budget confirmed or realistic path to budget ($5K+ for pilot)
  • Decision maker identified or accessible within 2 meetings
  • Clear pain point articulated (not just "exploring AI")
  • Timeline within 90 days for initial engagement
  • Technical environment compatible (cloud/modern stack)
  • No active competing evaluation in final stages

💡 Scoring Guide

5-6 checks = Hot lead, fast-track | 3-4 checks = Warm, nurture | 0-2 checks = Cold, marketing drip

0-2 min Opening Script
Rapport Builder
"Thanks for making time today, [Name]. Before we dive in, I want to make sure this is valuable for you. I've done some homework — saw [company news/LinkedIn post/recent hire]. How's that going?"
Agenda Setting
"Here's what I'm thinking: I'll ask a few questions to understand where you are with AI, then share what we're seeing work for similar companies, and we can figure out if there's a fit. Does that work, or is there something specific you wanted to cover?"
2-15 min Discovery Questions
  • "What's your current AI landscape look like?"
    Maps existing tools, maturity, and gaps. Listen for: point solutions vs. platform.
  • "Where are you seeing the biggest time drains for your team?"
    Identifies automation opportunities. Connect to ROI later.
  • "What's worked and what hasn't with AI so far?"
    Reveals past failures to avoid. Shows you understand the landscape.
  • "How does your leadership view AI — strategic priority or nice-to-have?"
    Gauges executive sponsorship. Critical for large deals.
  • "What would 'winning' look like for you personally on this initiative?"
    Uncovers personal motivations. Use for champion building.
15-25 min Value Positioning
Transition to Value
"Based on what you've shared, here's what I'm thinking... You've got [pain point 1] and [pain point 2], and you need something that [requirement]. Does that sound right?"
INT Differentiator
"What makes us different: We're not generalists. We've deployed AI for 50+ organizations just like yours. Our approach: 30-day pilot, measurable ROI, no 6-month strategy decks. We literally built a tool that maps 49 AI platforms to your specific use case — want me to show you?"
25-30 min Close & Next Steps
Trial Close
"Based on our conversation, here's what I'd recommend: A focused 30-day pilot with [department] targeting [use case]. Investment is $5-15K, and you'll have hard ROI numbers to take to leadership. Does that approach make sense for where you are?"
Calendar Close
"Great. Let's get something on the calendar. I'll send over a brief summary of what we discussed and a pilot proposal. Can we schedule a 45-minute session next week to walk through the specifics with [stakeholder name]?"
🖥️ 30-Minute Demo Structure
0:00
Agenda & Context (2 min)

Recap discovery findings. Confirm attendees and their goals. Set demo agenda.

0:02
Platform Explorer Demo (8 min)

Show filtering by their industry/use case. Highlight MCP scores. Compare 2-3 relevant platforms. Export comparison for them.

0:10
ROI Calculator Live (5 min)

Input their actual numbers (employees, salary, departments). Show projected savings. Highlight payback period.

0:15
Maturity Assessment (5 min)

Quick self-assessment with prospect. Show where they land. Reveal gap to "optimized" state.

0:20
Case Study / Social Proof (5 min)

Share 1-2 relevant examples. Focus on: similar size, similar challenge, measurable outcome.

0:25
Proposal & Close (5 min)

Present pilot proposal. Confirm scope and timeline. Ask for commitment or identify blockers.

⚡ Demo Power Moves
  • Use THEIR data
    Pre-load their industry, approximate headcount, and likely tools. Makes it feel custom.
  • Name drop strategically
    "We saw similar patterns at [competitor/peer]" — creates FOMO.
  • Leave something behind
    Export the comparison, ROI report, or maturity assessment. Gives them internal ammunition.
  • Ask "What would prevent you from moving forward?"
    Surfaces hidden objections before they derail the deal.
🛡️ Common Objections & Responses
"We're already working with [Big 4 / Accenture / Deloitte]" +
1Acknowledge: "That's great — they bring valuable enterprise experience."
2Reframe: "What we typically see is they're excellent at strategy, but implementation is where things stall. 95% of AI pilots fail to scale."
3Differentiate: "We're the implementation layer. Many of our clients use us alongside their Big 4 engagement to actually ship."
4Proof: "Would it help to see a case where we worked alongside [Deloitte/Accenture] to accelerate their roadmap?"
"We don't have budget for this right now" +
1Empathize: "Totally understand — budgets are tight everywhere."
2Reframe: "Quick question: is this a timing issue, or a prioritization issue? Because if AI can save you $500K/year, the question becomes where does that budget come from, not if."
3Offer: "What if we did a $5K pilot scoped to one team? We can prove ROI before you need a bigger budget conversation."
4Create urgency: "Our Q1 pilots are almost full — if timing works, we can lock in current pricing."
"We need to talk to IT / Security / Legal first" +
1Support: "Absolutely — that's the right process. We actually love working with IT/Security early."
2Offer resources: "We have a security FAQ and architecture doc specifically for those conversations. Want me to send those over?"
3Accelerate: "Even better — should we schedule a joint call? I can walk them through our SOC2 compliance, data handling, and integration approach."
4Timeline: "When do you think that conversation can happen? I'll follow up to make sure you have everything you need."
"We tried AI before and it didn't work" +
1Validate: "You're not alone — 95% of AI pilots fail. Can you tell me more about what happened?"
2Diagnose: Listen for: wrong use case, poor change management, no success metrics, vendor oversold.
3Differentiate: "That's exactly why we start with a maturity assessment and ROI model before touching technology. We don't let you buy until we know it'll work."
4Risk reversal: "Our pilots are designed to fail fast and cheap if something's wrong. 30 days, clear metrics, go/no-go decision. No 12-month commitment."
"Can you just send me some information?" +
1Clarify intent: "Happy to. What specifically would be most useful — case studies, pricing, technical specs?"
2Qualify: "Before I bury you in PDFs — are you actively evaluating solutions, or is this more for future reference?"
3If active: "Got it. Our materials are pretty generic — a 15-minute call lets me customize to your situation. Can we do Tuesday or Thursday?"
4If future: "No problem. I'll add you to our monthly AI insights newsletter and check back in [timeframe]. Sound good?"
"Your company is too small / I've never heard of you" +
1Own it: "Fair point — we're a 55-person specialist firm, not a global consultancy."
2Reframe as strength: "That's actually why clients choose us. You get senior people who do the work, not a partner pitch followed by junior staff."
3Social proof: "We've deployed AI for [X] organizations in your industry. Want to talk to one of them?"
4Risk reversal: "And honestly — a $5K pilot with us is a lot less risky than a $500K engagement with someone who subcontracts the actual work."
🤝 Closing Techniques
Assumptive Close
"So we'll start with Engineering for the pilot, kick off the first week of [month]. Should I send the SOW to you, or is there someone else who handles contracts?"
Alternative Close
"We can go two directions here: a focused 30-day pilot with one team at $5K, or a 90-day department rollout at $15K with more comprehensive support. Which feels like the better fit for where you are?"
Urgency Close
"I should mention — our Q1 pilot slots are filling up. If you want to start in January, we'd need to get the paperwork moving this week. Is that realistic on your end?"
Blocker Identification
"On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to move forward with something like this in the next 30 days? [If <8]: What would need to happen to get that to a 9 or 10?"
📋 Post-Call Checklist
  • Send recap email within 2 hours
  • Attach relevant exports (ROI report, comparison, assessment)
  • Include clear next step with date/time
  • CC any stakeholders mentioned
  • Set follow-up reminder for 48 hours
  • Update CRM with notes and deal stage
  • Prep any promised materials (case study, security docs)

🎯 The Golden Rule

Every call ends with a committed next step with a date. "I'll follow up" is not a next step. "We'll talk to [stakeholder] on Tuesday at 2pm and I'll send the agenda by EOD Monday" is a next step.
