# Ops Autopilot — Investor One-Pager

---

## Problem
SMB IT teams (50–500 employees) lose 10–40 hours/week to manual operations: ticket routing, onboarding, password resets, access provisioning. Enterprise tools cost $50K+/year. DIY tools lack security and compliance. The market gap: no affordable, secure, managed IT automation for SMBs.

## Solution
**Ops Autopilot** — managed IT automation platform. Pre-built workflows with human-in-the-loop approvals, zero-knowledge security, and immutable audit trails. Deployed in 1 week, not 6 months.

## How It Works
Teams front door (employees interact) → n8n engine (customer-hosted, data never leaves) → Intinc control plane (approvals, audit, monitoring). Three tiers: $49/mo (Starter), $149/mo (Growth), $500+/mo (Enterprise).
