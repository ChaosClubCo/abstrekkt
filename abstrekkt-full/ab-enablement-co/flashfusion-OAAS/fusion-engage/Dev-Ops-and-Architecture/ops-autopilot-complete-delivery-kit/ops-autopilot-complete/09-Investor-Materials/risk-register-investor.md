# Risk Register — Business Risks

> **Audience:** INV | **Phase:** ALL

---

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| B1 | **Product-market fit gap** — SMBs don't value automation enough to pay | Medium | Critical | Beta validation with 30+ customers before GA. NPS >50 required for launch. Pivot to professional services if PMF not achieved. |
| B2 | **Enterprise competitor enters SMB market** — ServiceNow or Microsoft launches SMB-specific product | Low | High | Speed to market advantage (12 months head start). Managed service model is defensible (competitors are platforms, not services). Deepen customer relationships during beta. |
| B3 | **Customer acquisition cost higher than modeled** — $200 CAC assumption too optimistic | Medium | Medium | Start with warm leads (INT client base, conference contacts). Content marketing for organic. If CAC >$500, shift to sales-assisted only (higher ACV). |
| B4 | **Engineering capacity bottleneck** — Single-point-of-failure in technical knowledge | High | High | Comprehensive documentation (this kit). Hire Eng #2 by Q3 2026. Pair programming from Day 1 of beta. |
| B5 | **Security incident damages trust** — Vulnerability in beta damages reputation | Low | Critical | Pre-launch security audit, kill-switch procedure, responsible disclosure process, cyber insurance, transparent communication. |
| B6 | **n8n platform risk** — n8n changes licensing or breaks backward compatibility | Low | Medium | Pin n8n version, maintain fork option, evaluate alternative engines quarterly. |
| B7 | **Churn exceeds model** — >5% monthly churn during beta | Medium | High | Weekly customer syncs, proactive churn detection, save offers, exit interviews. Adjust product based on churn reasons. |
| B8 | **Regulatory change** — New compliance requirements increase cost/complexity | Low | Medium | Architecture designed for compliance (audit trail, RBAC). Flexibility to add controls. Monitor regulatory landscape quarterly. |
| B9 | **Pricing too low** — $49–149 not viable at scale | Medium | Medium | Validate with beta customers. Enterprise tier ($500+) provides margin. Raise prices post-GA if warranted by value delivery. |
| B10 | **Team bandwidth** — Building product while running IT services business | High | Medium | Dedicated engineering time (not shared with client work). Clear sprint boundaries. Executive alignment on product investment. |
