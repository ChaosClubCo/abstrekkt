## Market Drivers

1. **IT labor cost increasing:** Average IT staff cost $85K–$120K/year. Automating 10 hrs/week = $22K/year savings per company.
2. **Compliance pressure growing:** SOC2, GDPR, HIPAA requirements expanding to mid-market. Audit trails becoming mandatory.
3. **Shadow IT risk:** 38% of employees use unapproved AI tools. Governance gap drives demand for controlled automation.
4. **Remote work complexity:** Distributed teams increase onboarding/offboarding frequency and access management burden.

## Expansion Strategy

**Year 1:** Tech/SaaS SMBs (prove model, build case studies)
**Year 2:** Add professional services, healthcare (compliance-driven)
**Year 3:** Financial services, education, nonprofit
**Year 4+:** International expansion (UK, EU, ANZ)
