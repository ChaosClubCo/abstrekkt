# Executive Summary — Ops Autopilot for SMB

> **Audience:** CEO, Board, Investors, VP Engineering | **Phase:** All

---

## The Problem

SMBs (50–500 employees) lose 10–40 hours/week to manual IT operations: routing tickets, onboarding employees, resetting passwords, provisioning licenses. Enterprise platforms cost $50K–$200K/year. DIY tools lack security and audit trails. Most SMBs run on spreadsheets and tribal knowledge.

**The gap:** No managed automation platform exists that is affordable, secure, and purpose-built for SMB IT operations.

---

## The Solution

**Ops Autopilot** combines three components:

1. **Teams Front Door** — Employees interact via Microsoft Teams (Copilot Studio). Approvals via Slack/Teams modals.
2. **n8n Execution Engine** — Each customer gets a dedicated, self-hosted n8n instance. Customer data and secrets never leave their environment.
3. **Intinc Control Plane** — SaaS layer handles approvals, audit logging, policy enforcement, and monitoring. Zero-knowledge secrets model.

**Results:** Ticket routing: 15min → 5sec. Onboarding: 2–3 days → 30min. Password resets: helpdesk call → self-service in <2min. Every action logged, approved, auditable.
