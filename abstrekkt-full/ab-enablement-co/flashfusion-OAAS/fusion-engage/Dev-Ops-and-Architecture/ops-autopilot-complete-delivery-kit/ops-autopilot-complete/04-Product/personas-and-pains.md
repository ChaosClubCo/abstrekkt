# Personas and Pain Points

> **Audience:** SALES, EXEC | **Phase:** ALL

---

## Persona 1: IT Director (Primary Buyer)

**Profile:** Manages 5–15 person IT team at 100–300 employee company. Reports to CTO or CEO.
**Pain:** Team spends 40%+ of time on repetitive tasks. Can't justify enterprise tooling budget. Worried about shadow IT and compliance gaps.
**Motivation:** Free team for strategic projects, demonstrate ROI to leadership, reduce manual errors.
**Willingness to pay:** $149–500/mo if ROI demonstrable within 30 days.
**Objections:** "We've built scripts for this already." "How is this different from Zapier?"
**Win condition:** Show 10+ hours/week saved in first month.

## Persona 2: Service Desk Lead (Champion/User)

**Profile:** Runs L1/L2 helpdesk. Handles 50–200 tickets/week manually. Manages 3–8 agents.
**Pain:** Ticket routing takes 10–15 min per ticket. Password resets consume 20% of agent time. Onboarding is a 2-day checklist nightmare.
**Motivation:** Reduce ticket backlog, eliminate repetitive work, look good to IT Director.
**Willingness to pay:** Not the budget holder, but strong internal advocate.
**Win condition:** Ticket routing automated, password resets self-service.

## Persona 3: HR Manager (Stakeholder)

**Profile:** Manages onboarding/offboarding process. Coordinates with IT for account provisioning.
**Pain:** Onboarding takes 2–3 days because IT is the bottleneck. Offboarding sometimes incomplete (security risk).
**Motivation:** Faster onboarding = better employee experience. Complete offboarding = compliance.
**Willingness to pay:** Influences IT budget, doesn't own it.
**Win condition:** Day-1 readiness for new hires, complete offboarding within 1 hour.

## Persona 4: Compliance Officer (Evaluator)

**Profile:** Responsible for SOC2/GDPR/HIPAA compliance. Reviews audit trails quarterly.
**Pain:** Manual evidence collection takes 2–3 days per audit cycle. No centralized audit trail for IT operations.
**Motivation:** Automated evidence collection, immutable audit logs, compliance reports on demand.
**Willingness to pay:** Strong justification for Enterprise tier if audit requirements are met.
**Win condition:** Compliance report generated in minutes, not days.

## Persona 5: CEO/CTO (Approver)

**Profile:** Approves IT spend. Cares about operational efficiency and risk reduction.
**Pain:** IT costs growing but not clear what team spends time on. Worried about security incidents.
**Motivation:** Reduce IT operational cost, demonstrate governance to board/investors.
**Willingness to pay:** Will approve if IT Director shows clear ROI.
**Win condition:** Cost savings > platform cost within 90 days.
