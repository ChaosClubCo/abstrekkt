# Market Sizing — TAM / SAM / SOM

> **Audience:** INV, EXEC | **Phase:** ALL

---

## Total Addressable Market (TAM)

**IT automation for businesses with 50+ employees (global)**
- 1.2M businesses globally with 50–500 employees and IT departments
- Average spend on IT automation: $1,800/year
- **TAM: $2.1B**

## Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)

**US-based SMBs with active service desks and Microsoft/Slack stack**
- 150,000 US SMBs with 50+ employees and active service desk
- 80% use Microsoft 365 or Slack
- Average addressable spend: $1,800/year
- **SAM: $216M**

## Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)

**Beachhead: tech/SaaS SMBs (100–300 employees) in US with Azure AD + Slack**
- ~15,000 companies matching beachhead profile
- 8–12% adoption at maturity (3–5 years)
- Average revenue per customer: $1,800/year (Growth tier)
- **SOM (Year 3): $21.6M–$32.4M**
- **SOM (Year 1, realistic): $2.4M–$4.8M**

---

## Beachhead Strategy

**Why tech/SaaS SMBs first:**
1. Already use Azure AD + Slack/Teams (connector compatibility)
2. IT teams are technically sophisticated (faster onboarding)
3. High volume of repetitive tasks (ticket routing, onboarding)
4. Budget-conscious but willing to pay for automation
5. Strong word-of-mouth in tech community

**Expansion path:**
1. Tech/SaaS SMBs (Year 1) → prove model
2. Professional services firms (Year 2) → high onboarding volume
3. Healthcare SMBs (Year 2–3) → compliance-driven
4. Financial services (Year 3) → regulatory requirements
5. Education/nonprofit (Year 3+) → budget-sensitive, high volume
