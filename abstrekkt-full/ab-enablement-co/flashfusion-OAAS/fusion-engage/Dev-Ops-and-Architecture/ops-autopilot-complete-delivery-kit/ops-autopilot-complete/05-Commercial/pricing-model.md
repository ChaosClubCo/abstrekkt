# Pricing Model — Ops Autopilot

> **Audience:** EXEC, SALES | **Phase:** ALL

---

## Three-Tier Structure

### Starter — $49/month
**Target:** Any SMB with a service desk (50–100 employees)
**Includes:** 2 workflows (Ticket Routing + Password Reset), 10 concurrent, 100 runs/day, Slack/Teams notifications, basic dashboard, email support, 1-year audit retention
**Upgrade trigger:** Need onboarding/offboarding automation or >100 runs/day

### Growth — $149/month
**Target:** IT teams managing 100–300 employees
**Includes:** 5–6 workflows (Starter + Onboarding + Offboarding + License + VPN optional), 50 concurrent, 1,000 runs/day, approval chains, full dashboard, priority support (4hr response), 3-year audit retention
**Upgrade trigger:** Need security incident response, compliance reports, custom workflows, or >1,000 runs/day

### Enterprise — $500+/month (custom pricing)
**Target:** 300+ employees, regulated industries, complex requirements
**Includes:** All 10 workflows + custom, 200 concurrent, unlimited runs, n8n cluster deployment, dedicated support (1hr response), compliance reports, 7-year audit retention, custom policies, professional services hours
**Pricing factors:** Number of workflows, employee count, compliance requirements, SLA tier
