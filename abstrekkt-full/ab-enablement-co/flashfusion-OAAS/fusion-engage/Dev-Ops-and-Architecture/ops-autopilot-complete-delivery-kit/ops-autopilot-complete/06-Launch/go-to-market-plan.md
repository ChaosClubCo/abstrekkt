# Go-to-Market Plan

> **Audience:** EXEC, SALES | **Phase:** GA

---

## Positioning Statement

**For** IT teams at SMBs (50–500 employees) **who** lose 10–40 hours/week to manual IT operations, **Ops Autopilot** is a managed automation platform **that** eliminates ticket routing, onboarding, password resets, and access provisioning in under 1 week. **Unlike** Zapier (no security), ServiceNow (too expensive), or Power Automate (too complex), **Ops Autopilot** combines pre-built IT workflows with approval gates, zero-knowledge security, and immutable audit trails — all as a managed service starting at $49/month.

## Launch Channels

### Channel 1: Content Marketing
- Blog series: "The True Cost of Manual IT Ops" (3 posts, publish W15–17)
- Case studies: 3 beta customer stories with ROI numbers
- LinkedIn posts: weekly, from INT president + Kyle
- Target: 500 website visitors/month by W20

### Channel 2: Community
- r/sysadmin, r/msp: share case studies (no hard sell)
- IT Slack communities: participate, share insights
- Tech Twitter/X: weekly tips on IT automation
- Target: 10 inbound leads/month from community

### Channel 3: Conferences
- HumanX 2026 (existing commitment): demo booth, case study presentation
- Local IT meetups: 2 per quarter
- Webinars: monthly "IT Automation Office Hours" (30 min, free)
- Target: 20 leads per conference, 5 per webinar

### Channel 4: Direct Sales (Growth + Enterprise)
- Outbound to IT Directors at target companies (LinkedIn + email)
- 30-min demo + trial offer
- Follow-up sequence: Day 1 (demo), Day 3 (case study), Day 7 (trial invite)
- Target: 25 demos/month, 30% conversion

### Channel 5: Self-Service (Starter)
- Website with pricing, features, demo video
- Free trial: 14 days, no credit card required
- In-product upgrade prompts
- Target: 40% of Starter customers convert from free trial
