# Beta Feedback Framework

> **Audience:** LEAD | **Phase:** Beta

---

## Collection Channels

**Weekly Sync Call (Tuesday 2pm ET):**
- 30 min per customer, all beta customers invited
- Agenda: metric review (runs, errors, approvals), issues, feature requests, wins
- Notes captured in shared doc, action items assigned

**NPS Survey (Every 4 Weeks):**
- Single question: "How likely are you to recommend Ops Autopilot?" (0–10)
- Follow-up: "What's the primary reason for your score?"
- Target: NPS >50 by GA

**Feature Request Log:**
- Public Trello board (customers can see + vote)
- INT prioritizes top 3 requests per month using RICE
- Shipped features announced in weekly sync

**Behavioral Metrics (Automatic):**
- Which workflows used most
- Where users get stuck (step failure patterns)
- Approval response time by role
- Dashboard engagement (page views, time on page)
- Connector error patterns

## Prioritization (RICE)

Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

| Rating | Reach (0–10) | Impact (0–10) | Confidence (0–1) | Effort (days) |
|--------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|---------------|
| High | 8–10 | 8–10 | 0.8–1.0 | 1–3 |
| Medium | 4–7 | 4–7 | 0.5–0.7 | 3–10 |
| Low | 1–3 | 1–3 | 0.1–0.4 | 10+ |

**Monthly review:** Product Lead reviews all requests, scores top 20, selects top 3 for next sprint.

## Feedback → Action SLA

| Feedback Type | Response Time | Resolution Time |
|---------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Bug report (critical) | 15 min | Same day |
| Bug report (high) | 1 hour | 2 days |
| Feature request | Acknowledged in weekly sync | Scored within 1 week |
| UX feedback | Acknowledged in weekly sync | Batched into monthly UX sprint |
| Pricing feedback | Escalate to CEO within 24hr | Incorporate into Week 14 pricing review |
