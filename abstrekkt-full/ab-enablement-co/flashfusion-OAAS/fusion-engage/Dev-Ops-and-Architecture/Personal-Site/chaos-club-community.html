The Space Between Calls

The room where you stop
performing.

This community isn't a comment section. It's a private space for the people doing the real work — between the calls, between the chapters, between the public face and the actual life.

How It Works

A private space with a clear structure.

No algorithm. No public performance. No strangers. Just members who bought the book, joined the club, and are actively building something worth building.

01

Where We Live

The community runs inside a private Circle space — no app download required, accessible from any browser. Each room has a purpose. The platform is clean and distraction-free by design.

02

How to Show Up

Drop into the rooms that are relevant to you. Post when you have something real to say. Ask the ugly questions. Share the small wins. Respond when someone else is in it. That's the whole model.

03

What's Expected

You're here because you can handle honesty. That cuts both ways. We don't perform here. We don't self-promote here. We don't catastrophize in public. The agreements are short and they're serious.

The Rooms

Spaces built around real use.

Every room has a job. Nothing's a dumping ground. You'll figure out your orbit in the first week.

# general

The Common Room

Where you introduce yourself and land softly. What's your chaos right now? What brought you here? The standing invite to be honest about where you actually are.

Active cadence: Daily
# real-talk

Real Talk

The honest thread. No performing. No polished takes. The place to say the thing you're not saying anywhere else. Held by the agreements. Moderated when necessary.

Active cadence: High-volume. Check it.
# wins-small-and-large

Wins (Small and Large)

The rule: you must actually feel it. No performative wins. Specific beats vague. "I sent the email I've been avoiding for three weeks" counts. "We're crushing it!" does not.

Active cadence: Ongoing
# questions-and-advice

Questions & Advice

Ask real questions. Get real answers from people who've been in similar positions. Breanna drops in here. Members with relevant experience answer first. No unsolicited coaching.

Active cadence: Weekdays
# session-prep

Session Prep

Post your question, topic, or situation before each live call. This is how Hot Seat submissions get noticed. Active 48 hours before and after every session.

Active cadence: Pre-call windows
# resource-drops

Resource Drops

Links, tools, frameworks, books, articles — shared by members and Breanna with actual context. Not a link farm. Every post requires one sentence: why this, why now.

Active cadence: Curated, not constant

The Weekly Rhythm

What happens when.

The community has a beat. You don't have to hit every note — but knowing the rhythm helps you show up when it matters.

Monday

Weekly Intention Drop

Breanna posts a short provocation or framing question in General. Optional: reply with what you're holding this week. Sets the tone without demanding participation.

Wednesday

Mid-Week Check

Real Talk gets a nudge. Wins room gets active. This is the highest-engagement window in the community — drop in if you've got something to say or someone to support.

Pre-Call Days

Session Prep Opens

48 hours before each live call, Session Prep activates. Post your situation for Hot Seat consideration. Review what others are bringing so you're not walking in cold.

Friday

End-of-Week Release

Wins room runs hot. A weekly thread opens: what are you putting down for the weekend? What actually moved? No pressure. Just a moment to close the loop before you log off.

Community Agreements

Short. Non-negotiable.

These aren't vibes. They're the architecture that makes the room safe enough to actually use.

01
What's said here stays here.No screenshots. No sharing someone else's words outside this space without explicit permission. This is not public content.
02
No unsolicited advice.If someone posts in Real Talk, they're not necessarily asking to be fixed. If you want to offer a perspective, ask first. "Can I share something?" is a full sentence.
03
No self-promotion.No selling. No pitching. No "I just launched" unless it's in the Wins room and it's genuinely personal. There is no exception to this rule.
04
Be specific or don't post.Vague complaints don't help anyone. If you're going to air something, give it enough shape that people can actually respond. The more specific, the more useful.
05
Assume good intent. Verify with kindness.If something lands wrong, say so directly and privately before escalating. The mods are here if you need them, but most things can be handled with a DM.
06
You can leave the room if you need to.Stepping back for a week, a month, whatever — no explanation required. Log back in when you're ready. The door is open. The Wins room will still be there.

A Note From Breanna

From the host
I built this because I needed it.

Every community I'd ever been in had the same problem: everyone was performing. The wins were optimized. The questions were safe. The "vulnerable" shares were pre-packaged. And I left every single one of them more alone than when I walked in.

This room is an experiment in what happens when the agreements are real, the host shows up without armor, and the people in the room are serious about their own life — not performing seriousness for an audience.

We moderate hard. We remove people who treat this like a networking event. We hold the agreements even when it's uncomfortable. And we protect the quiet members who never post but read every thread and need to know the room is safe.

You're here because you bought the book or you joined the club or both. That means something. This room is yours. Use it like you mean it.
