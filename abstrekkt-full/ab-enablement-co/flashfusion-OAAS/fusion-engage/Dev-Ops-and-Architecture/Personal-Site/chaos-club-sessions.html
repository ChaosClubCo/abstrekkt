THE HOT SEAT

Your problem.
Her brain.
Everyone watches.

The Hot Seat is the most uncomfortable and most useful thing in the Club. You submit your situation. Breanna picks one. For 45 minutes, she thinks through it out loud — with no filter, no softening, no telling you what you want to hear.

The catch: everyone is in the room. The whole membership watches while Breanna works through your specific situation in real time. Which means your mess becomes everyone's lesson.

Past hot seats have covered pricing paralysis, client boundaries, scope creep, burnout disguised as laziness, the business idea you're too afraid to say out loud, and the situation you've been describing as "fine" for eight months.

01

Submit by Tuesday midnight

A form link goes out every Monday before Hot Seat Thursday. One paragraph minimum. No essay required — just the actual situation, not the backstory.

02

Breanna picks one

She picks the situation she thinks the most members will learn from. Not the most dramatic. Not the most extreme. The most transferable one in the pile.

03

You're live with her

If chosen, you're unmuted. Breanna asks three questions max, then thinks out loud for 35 minutes. You can respond, push back, or just listen while the room watches.

04

It lives in the vault

The full session goes into the replay library. Future members watch it. Your problem becomes part of the Club's collective brain — and everyone's answer for a long time.
