The rhythm

What to expect when to show up

We're not building a hustle habit. We're building something sustainable. The schedule is designed so you can stay in contact without burning out.

Everything is recorded if you can't make it live. But showing up live is where the conversations happen that nobody puts in a transcript.

Weekly
Open Sessions
60 minutes. Real questions, no slides, no agenda. We work through whatever the room needs — often something nobody expected.
Bi-weekly
Deep Cuts
Longer format. A specific topic, problem, or concept we go all the way into. These are the ones that tend to break something open.
Monthly
Member Spotlights
Someone in the room shares what they're working through. Structured conversation, zero performance. The vulnerability in these is real.
Always
The Community Thread
Not a group chat. Not a content feed. A place to ask, share, and hear from people who are in it — not pretending they've got it figured out.
