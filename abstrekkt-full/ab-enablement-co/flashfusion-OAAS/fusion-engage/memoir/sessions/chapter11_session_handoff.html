Draft opening — first 400 words (approved direction)

Written in Kyle's voice. Direction confirmed. Carry verbatim into the next chat.

The table is a card table.

Not a desk. Not a standing workstation with cable management and a monitor arm bolted to something structural. A card table — the kind that folds in half and leans against a wall when company comes, except company doesn't come and it hasn't folded since I set it up. On it right now: two monitors, an RGB keyboard that I did not need and bought anyway, a Toshiba remote that belongs to a TV I can't see from here, a yellow highlighter, a green drink, cables that go places I've stopped tracking. This is the command center. This is where everything gets built.

I built Bear's website here.

Bear is Soaring Bear — Sacred Spiritual Healing Journeys, Deep Soul Mentorship, permitted guide, the man my mother sent me to when everything else had failed and she was out of options and I was out of arguments. I went to Sedona because I had told myself, out loud, with the specific exhaustion of someone who has tried the available alternatives: I've tried everything else. Now I'm going to try my hardest. That's the whole commitment. That was all I had.

Bear received me when I was still mostly wreckage. This was after the hospital. After the floor — I spent time on the floor of a rehabilitation facility telling myself that mindfulness was invented by people who had never had a brain that operated the way mine operated, which is a sentence that would be funnier if I hadn't believed it completely. Bear didn't argue with any of that. He handed me a binder and sent me assignments and said I choose to love you, period, with the punctuation and everything, and I didn't know what to do with that so I did the work.

Months later — way later, after the work had started to build something — I made him a website.

I had no idea how to make a website. I had no tools, no Figma, no vocabulary for what I was looking at when I looked at his old site. So I did what I do: I took it apart. Scrolled every page. Screenshotted everything. Copy-pasted every word into a document. Then looked up what things were called — accordion menu, parallax background, hero section — so I could ask for what I wanted to build. Bear sold books on Amazon. I got every link. Got every cover image. Real pain in the ass. He helped me where he could.

I built it on this table.
