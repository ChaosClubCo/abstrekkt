## VOICE RULES

### What works
- Declarative observations — let the reader draw conclusions
- Technical descriptions of emotional states
- Admitted uncertainty about memory and meaning
- Profanity where it earns its place — not for shock
- Humor that doesn't defuse tension prematurely
- Leaving room for the reader to work

### What to avoid
- "And that's when I realized..."
- "Looking back, I can see that..."
- "It was then that everything changed..."
- Any construction that makes meaning too available too fast
- Over-explaining what a scene means
- Performing either vulnerability or toughness

### Voice register by context
- Memoir / Personal: all dials up. Raw, metaphor-dense, profanity where earned. Memory gaps visible.
- Chaos Club / Brand: directed outward. Writing TO someone. Profanity is permission.
- Technical / Professional: system metaphors stay, profanity drops.
