[BASE44 PROMPT] Onboarding Wizard — Outcome-First Mode
=======================================================
Paste directly into app.base44. Atomic, self-contained.

You are designing an AI-powered, step-by-step onboarding wizard for a deal-
sourcing / portfolio-management platform. Optimize for "Outcome-First": before
collecting any settings, ask the user what outcome they want (e.g. "find 3
multifamily deals under $2M/mo", "track my angel portfolio", "join a syndicate
community"), then configure the rest of onboarding around that goal.

Generate an interactive flow that:
1. Greets the user and asks for their primary OUTCOME (single, high-signal choice
   + free-text). Echo it back as the wizard's north star.
2. Derives deal criteria (asset class, geography, ticket size, risk band) from
   the outcome; pre-fills sensible defaults and lets the user adjust.
3. Captures portfolio goals (target return, horizon, diversification rules).
4. Sets community/collaboration preferences (solo, team, syndicate visibility).
5. Surfaces a COMPLIANCE gate (accredited-investor / KYC) before unlocking deal
   data — block portfolio features until satisfied.
6. Ends with a personalized "first win" task tied to the stated outcome.

Requirements:
- Emit a structured onboarding_state object (see onboarding-state.schema.json)
  on each step so analytics and the lifecycle engine can read progress.
- Fire events: onboarding_started, step_completed{step}, activation_reached.
- Keep copy friendly and concrete; no jargon. Explain WHY each field matters.
- Provide decision-tree branches for: returning user, invited team member,
  and "just exploring" (defer compliance, offer sandbox).

Output: the wizard UI/UX + state logic, ready for Base44 to render.

--- IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (team handoff, not for paste) ---
- "activation_reached" should map to completing the first-win task, NOT finishing
  the form — measure value, not form completion.
- Do not show monetization/upsell here; that belongs to the Power-User Expansion
  prompt and must be gated behind activation_reached.
- A/B: this prompt is the "Outcome-First" arm; the "Standard" arm collects
  settings first, outcome last. Primary metric = activation rate within 24h.
