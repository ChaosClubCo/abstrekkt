[BASE44 PROMPT] Activation System
==================================
Atomic, self-contained. Paste into app.base44.

You are designing the ACTIVATION layer for a deal-sourcing / portfolio app. Goal:
move a newly-onboarded user to their first real value ("aha") as fast as possible,
where activation = completing the first-win task defined during onboarding (NOT
finishing the signup form).

Generate flow + logic that:
1. Reads onboarding_state.outcome and proposes the single highest-leverage first
   action tied to it (e.g. outcome=find_deals -> "see 3 matched deals now").
2. Removes friction: pre-fill from onboarding, defer anything non-essential.
3. Shows a visible progress indicator toward the first win.
4. On completion, sets onboarding_state.activationReached = true and fires
   activation_reached.
5. If the user stalls (>X minutes / leaves), queues a single, specific nudge
   (email/in-app) referencing their stated outcome — never generic.

Requirements:
- Emit events: activation_started, first_win_task_shown, activation_reached,
  activation_stalled{reason}.
- Define the activation metric precisely and a target time-to-activation.
- Provide branches for the three onboarding personas (returning, invited, exploring).

--- IMPLEMENTATION NOTES ---
- Time-to-activation is the north-star metric for this layer; instrument it first.
- Do NOT show upsell here (belongs to Power-User Expansion, gated on this event).
