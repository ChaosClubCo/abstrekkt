[BASE44 PROMPT] Org / Team Expansion
=====================================
Atomic, self-contained. Paste into app.base44.

You are designing the ORG/TEAM layer for a deal-sourcing / portfolio app. Goal:
turn single users into teams/syndicates (seat growth) with shared workspaces and
clear roles.

Generate logic that:
1. Detects team-readiness signals (sharing a deal, mentioning colleagues, syndicate
   intent from onboarding community preference).
2. Offers to create a shared workspace and invite members (role: owner/admin/member/
   viewer).
3. Sets workspace-level deal criteria + portfolio visibility rules.
4. Handles invitations, seat counts, and the invited-member onboarding branch
   (lightweight: skip outcome capture, inherit workspace context).

Requirements:
- Emit events: team_ready_detected, workspace_created, member_invited, member_joined,
  seat_count_changed.
- Enforce role-based visibility on financial/deal data (least privilege).
- Coordinate with Expansion layer for seat-based monetization.

--- IMPLEMENTATION NOTES ---
- Invited members start at a different onboarding entry point (see wizard branch).
- Workspace data isolation is a security requirement, not just UX (see Security Review).
