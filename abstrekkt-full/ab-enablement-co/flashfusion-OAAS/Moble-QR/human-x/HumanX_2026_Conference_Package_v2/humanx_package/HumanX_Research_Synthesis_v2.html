Bull Case: InVelo Is Perfectly Timed

HumanX's entire content philosophy validates InVelo's thesis. The conference exists because enterprises are confused about AI adoption, drowning in vendor noise, and struggling with governance. If even 5% of 6,500 attendees represent mid-market companies, that's 325 potential buyers — more than enough for Y1 pipeline. SolutionBridge provides pre-qualified warm leads. Competitive landscape at this price point is empty. INT's SOC 2 and 25-year relationships are genuine differentiators no AI startup can match.

Bear Case: Wrong Conference, Wrong Audience

HumanX is designed for enterprise AI leaders at $1B+ companies and VC-backed startups. Mid-market IT directors may not attend a premium SF conference. The real buyers may be at MSP-specific events (IT Nation, DattoCon). The "18-month window" depends on agentic AI maturity that Gartner says is largely aspirational (40%+ canceled). Conference generates interesting conversations but zero closed deals.

The Honest Assessment

The truth is between these cases. HumanX will validate the thesis and provide competitive intelligence that no desk research can match. It will NOT generate immediate revenue. The question is whether intelligence gained justifies cost versus spending those 4 days calling existing INT clients with a pilot offer. Both have value; they serve different purposes.
