## Session 1 — Platform orientation
**Timing:** Week 2 (after ads go live)
**Duration:** 60 min
**Goal:** Trainee understands what is running, why, and where to find everything.

### Agenda

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 0–10 min | Tour: where everything lives (Facebook Ads Manager, Google Sheet, email) |
| 10–25 min | How to read the Ads Manager dashboard: impressions, reach, CPM, leads, CPL |
| 25–40 min | How leads flow: form → Google Sheet → email notification → follow-up |
| 40–55 min | What "healthy" looks like vs. what to flag to Kyle |
| 55–60 min | Questions + homework |

### Homework
- Log into Facebook Ads Manager and write down: how many leads came in this week and what it cost per lead
- Check the Google Sheet and confirm the same number of leads appears there

### Healthy benchmarks to share
- CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions): $10–$25 is normal for local
- CPL (cost per lead): $5–$20 is good; above $40 is a problem
- Lead-to-contact rate goal: 50% of leads should get a reply within 24 hours

## Session 2 — Reading the report
**Timing:** Week 4
**Duration:** 60 min
**Goal:** Trainee can produce and interpret the monthly report without help.

### Agenda

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 0–10 min | Review homework from session 1 |
| 10–30 min | Walk through the monthly report template together — fill in real numbers |
| 30–45 min | What each number means and what to do if it's off |
| 45–55 min | How to export data from Ads Manager to fill the report |
| 55–60 min | Questions + homework |

### Homework
- Fill out the monthly report template using last month's numbers
- Email it to Kyle before session 3

---

## Session 3 — Making small adjustments
**Timing:** Week 5
**Duration:** 60 min
**Goal:** Trainee can refresh ad copy and adjust budget without Kyle's help.

### Agenda

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 0–10 min | Review month 1 report together |
| 10–25 min | How to pause a low-performing ad and duplicate a winning one |
| 25–40 min | How to update ad copy: edit text, swap image, keep campaign structure intact |
| 40–50 min | How to adjust budget per location (raise or lower daily spend) |
| 50–60 min | Questions + homework |

### Homework
- Identify the lowest-performing ad in Ads Manager and write a replacement headline for it
- Do not publish it yet — review with Kyle in session 4

### What NOT to touch (cover explicitly)
- Do not change campaign objective (leads → traffic will break cost tracking)
- Do not delete campaigns — pause instead
- Do not change audience targeting without reviewing with Kyle first
- Do not add new locations without a new campaign structure

---

## Session 4 — Handoff and independence
**Timing:** Week 6
**Duration:** 60 min
**Goal:** Trainee is fully handed the keys. Kyle is a backup, not a dependency.

### Agenda

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 0–10 min | Review session 3 homework — publish the replacement ad together |
| 10–20 min | Walk through the handoff document: where every setting is, what each thing does |
| 20–35 min | Troubleshooting walkthrough — simulate 3 common problems (see troubleshooting guide) |
| 35–50 min | How to use the ad copy prompt library to write new copy with AI |
| 50–60 min | 90-day support explained: what to email Kyle for, what to handle independently |

### Trainee sign-off checklist
By the end of session 4, the trainee confirms they can independently:

- [ ] Log into Ads Manager and read campaign performance
- [ ] Fill out the monthly report template
- [ ] Pause a bad ad and duplicate a good one
- [ ] Update ad copy text
- [ ] Adjust daily budget
- [ ] Check the Google Sheet CRM for new leads
- [ ] Use the ad copy prompt library to generate new variations
- [ ] Know when to email Kyle vs. handle it themselves

## 90-day support scope (after session 4)

**Email Kyle for:**
- Ads rejected by Facebook and you don't know why
- Sudden spike in ad spend (> 2× normal daily spend)
- Zero leads for more than 5 days with no obvious explanation
- Wanting to add a new promotion or campaign

**Handle independently (covered in training):**
- Pausing and duplicating ads
- Updating copy
- Adjusting budget within 20% of current setting
- Reading and sending the monthly report
- Following up with leads in the Google Sheet
