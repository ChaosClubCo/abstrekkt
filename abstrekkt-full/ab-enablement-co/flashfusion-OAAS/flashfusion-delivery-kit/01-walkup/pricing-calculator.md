# Engagement Pricing Calculator

Price at 10–25% of the measurable annual value delivered. Never price by hours.

---

## Step 1 — Estimate client value

Use discovery call notes to fill this in.

| Input | Client answer | Your estimate |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| Average transaction value | $___ | $___ |
| New customers per month (current) | ___ | ___ |
| Target new customers per month | ___ | ___ |
| Months of value per year | 12 | 12 |

**Annual value of improvement:**
`(Target customers - Current customers) × Avg transaction × 12 = $___`

---

## Step 2 — Apply pricing multiplier

| Scenario | Multiplier | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Easy to measure, clear outcome | 15–20% | Facebook leads, bookings, form fills |
| Harder to attribute, mixed signals | 10–15% | Brand awareness, email open rates |
| Very clear ROI, low risk | 20–25% | Repeat order automation, cost reduction |

**Your price target:** Annual value × multiplier = `$___`

---

## Step 3 — Map to tiers

Round to nearest tier. Never quote a custom number without anchoring to a tier first.

| Tier | Setup | Monthly | Best fit |
|------|-------|---------|---------|
| Build+train | $4,500 | $0 | Client wants to own it. Low complexity. |
| Build+coach | $3,500 | $350 | Client capable but wants backup. |
| Build+managed | $3,500 | $650 | Client has no bandwidth. Complex system. |

**Complexity multipliers — add to setup price:**

| Condition | Add |
|-----------|-----|
| 3+ locations (vs 1) | +$1,000 |
| No existing CRM (must build from scratch) | +$500 |
| No creative assets (must source or generate images) | +$500 |
| Rush timeline (under 2 weeks to launch) | +$750 |
| Low tech owner (extra training sessions needed) | +$500 |

---

## Step 4 — Sanity check

Before sending the proposal, verify:

- [ ] Price is 10–25% of estimated annual value
- [ ] Setup price covers at minimum 20 hours of Kyle's time at $150/hr
- [ ] Monthly retainer covers at minimum 4 hours/month at $150/hr
- [ ] A 60-day benchmark metric is agreed upfront and is measurable
- [ ] Scope includes only what Kyle can deliver without subcontracting

---

## Example — Third Coast Guitar Service

- Avg repair transaction: $150
- Current new customers/month: 15 (estimated)
- Target new customers/month: 35
- Annual improvement: 20 × $150 × 12 = **$36,000**
- Pricing at 12.5%: **$4,500**
- Complexity: 3 locations (+$1,000), no CRM (+$500)
- Adjusted price: **$4,500** (already reflects complexity in tier selection)
- Tier: Build+train
- 60-day benchmark: 20+ qualified leads/month
