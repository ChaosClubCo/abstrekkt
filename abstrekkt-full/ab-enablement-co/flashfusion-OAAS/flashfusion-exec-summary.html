Recommended Approach

Four-pillar path — sequenced for minimal disruption and fast early ROI

01

Foundation & Data Readiness

Audit existing data flows, establish AI-safe handling policies, and instrument key workflows for observability. This phase creates the clean substrate everything else runs on — skipping it is the most common reason AI pilots fail to scale.

Weeks 1–3

02

Targeted Automation Pilots

Deploy 2–3 high-confidence automations against the confirmed Phase 1 use cases. Each pilot is scoped to produce measurable output within 2 weeks of deployment and generates a documented ROI benchmark for executive reporting.

Weeks 4–7

03

Agent Architecture & Integration

Build the connective layer — connecting existing tools into an agent-aware stack. Covers API contracts, auth patterns, human-in-the-loop guardrails, and the approval queues that maintain operator control as autonomy scales.

Weeks 7–10

04

Enablement & Capability Transfer

Train the team to operate what has been built. Runbooks, prompt libraries, escalation paths, and governance documentation are delivered as living artifacts — not one-time training sessions. Ends with a formal capability transfer checkpoint.

Weeks 10–12
