# FlashFusion Media Kit

## Approved logo usage
- Use the **logo mark** on dark backgrounds whenever possible.
- Maintain clear space equal to the mark stroke width around the logo.
- Do not recolor the mark outside the palette.

## Color palette (approved)
- Primary: #a855f7
- Secondary: #f472b6
- Dark BG: #0f0618
- Light Text: #f3e8ff

## Screenshots checklist
- Use dark mode as default.
- Show: dashboard, builder, AI HUD overlay, billing.
- Avoid placeholder content; use realistic labels and values.

## Social sizes to export (recommended)
- X header: 1500×500 (3:1)
- LinkedIn banner: 1584×396
- OpenGraph: 1200×630
- App icon: 1024×1024
