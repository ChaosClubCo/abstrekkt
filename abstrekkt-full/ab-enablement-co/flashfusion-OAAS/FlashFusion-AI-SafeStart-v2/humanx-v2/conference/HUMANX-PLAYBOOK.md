## Follow-Up Protocol

**April 10 (Day After):** Personalized email to every Hot lead. Reference specific conversation. Attach case study. Propose Tuesday/Thursday call.

**April 11-12:** Follow up with Warm leads and vendor/partner contacts. LinkedIn connections with personalized notes.

**April 14-16:** Follow up on non-responses. Publish blog post on HumanX takeaways.

**April 17-30:** Discovery calls with qualified leads. Use discovery script:
1. "What AI tools are your employees using — sanctioned and unsanctioned?"
2. "Do you have visibility into what data is shared with AI tools?"
3. "What does your AI governance framework look like today?"
4. "If you could wave a magic wand, what would AI done right look like in 6 months?"
5. "Who else would be involved in a decision like this?"

**Success metrics:** 5+ qualified conversations. 3 discovery calls. 1 pilot engagement by May 15.
