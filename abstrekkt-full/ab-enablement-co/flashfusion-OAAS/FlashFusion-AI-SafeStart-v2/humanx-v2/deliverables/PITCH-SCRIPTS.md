# Pitch Scripts

## 30 Seconds (Elevator / Badge Scan)

"I'm Kyle from FlashFusion. We solve the shadow AI problem — 86% of employees use AI tools nobody approved. We audit the entire ecosystem, deploy role-specific configurations in 4 weeks, train the team, hand everything over. We did it for 72 roles internally. Here's the case study."

[Hand them the one-pager or case study.]

---

## 2 Minutes (SolutionBridge / Peer Matching)

"88% of companies use AI. 6% get enterprise-wide value. The gap isn't technology — every vendor here will tell you their tool is the answer. The gap is governance.

Right now 38% of employees share sensitive data with AI tools their company doesn't know about. 68% use personal accounts. 1,550+ GenAI apps exist. Blocking doesn't work.

We audit the entire AI ecosystem — not one vendor's tool, everything. Then we deploy role-specific configurations: the right tools, the right permissions, the right data access for every role. 4 weeks. You own everything.

We proved it internally — 72 roles, 94% test coverage, 650% ROI. Now we deliver it for clients.

What does AI governance look like at your organization right now?"

---

## 10 Minutes (Deep Dive / Follow-Up Meeting)

**[Setup — 2 min]** "The data is clear: 86% of employees use AI weekly, 38% share sensitive data without permission, 68% use personal accounts. One breach costs $650K. Blocking fails — employees just use personal accounts.

**[The gap — 2 min]** Every vendor at HumanX will pitch their tool. Microsoft helps with Copilot. Google sets up Gemini. Nobody governs the ecosystem. Your real world is Copilot for email, Claude for strategy, Cursor for engineering, personal ChatGPT for everything else. No single vendor touches that.

**[What we do — 3 min]** Week 1: audit every AI tool across every department — sanctioned and shadow. The output is a risk-scored inventory most companies have never seen. Weeks 2-3: build role-specific configurations deployed in their actual platforms. Not a policy doc — working configurations. A VP of Finance gets a completely different setup than an engineer. Week 4: train by department, hand over the governance playbook, done. Client owns everything.

**[Proof — 2 min]** We did this internally first. 65-person company, 72 personas mapped. 81,700 lines of production code, 94% test coverage. Every role has approved tools, configured agents, data permissions, custom skills, and documentation. Shadow AI went to zero.

**[Close — 1 min]** For a company your size, this is a [X]-week engagement at $[X-Y]K. One prevented breach saves $650K. Would it make sense to schedule a deeper conversation?"

## Objection Responses

| They Say | You Say |
|----------|---------|
| "We already use Copilot / Claude" | "We don't replace your tools. We govern the ecosystem across all of them." |
| "Our IT team handles this" | "For one platform. Who governs the other 5 tools employees use?" |
| "We have a committee working on it" | "Committees take 6-12 months. We deploy in 4 weeks." |
| "Too expensive" | "One breach costs $650K. This engagement costs $25K. 26× ROI." |
| "We want to wait" | "Employees are already using AI without guardrails. The risk compounds daily." |
| "Send me more info" | "Here's our case study. Can we do 15 minutes next week?" |
