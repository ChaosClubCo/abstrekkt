### What Drives Pricing Variation Within Tiers
- Number of distinct AI platforms in use (2 platforms = lower, 5+ = higher)
- Number of distinct roles requiring unique configurations (fewer role clusters = lower)
- Compliance requirements (SOC 2 basic = lower, HIPAA + SOC 2 + PCI = higher)
- Existing governance maturity (some policies exist = lower, zero governance = higher)
