The Matt Wolfe Support Doc

Companion notes to The Calum Johnson Show × Matt Wolfe episode
TLDR

A 101-level primer on becoming an "AI Orchestrator" using three tools: ChatGPT for brainstorming + custom assistants, Perplexity for cited research reports, and Gemini for long-context document analysis. The actionable beat is the Wolf Challenge — one hour of unstructured journaling into ChatGPT's reasoning model to get fresh perspective and a next step.

Heads up: the doc references GPT-4o, o3, o4-mini as current. That's a July 2025 snapshot — model lineup has moved on. The orchestrator framing and prompting principles still hold; the model names don't.

Three tools, three roles

ChatGPT
Brainstorming buddy
  • Custom GPT builder for repeat tasks
  • Social media script generation
  • Explain-it-simply for dense topics
  • Image generation from prompts
Perplexity
The new Google
  • Fast cited answers, no rabbit hole
  • Consultant-style research reports
  • Competitor + market validation
  • Business model discovery
Gemini
Research assistant
  • Massive context — full books, transcripts
  • Deep document synthesis
  • NotebookLM audio summaries
  • Reasoning-mode for traceable answers

The Wolf Challenge

Block one hour. Open a reasoning model. Journal everything on your mind. Read what comes back.

Six-step run
0 of 6 complete
Block the hour on calendar
No phone, no Slack, no notifications. One tab only.
Open ChatGPT's reasoning model
Or whichever deep-thinking model is current. The point is back-and-forth, not speed.
Brain dump everything
Wins, struggles, what's stuck, what's stressing you. No structure. Journal-style.
Read the response carefully
Feedback · simplifications · fresh angle · one next step.
Try the first suggested step
Just one. Don't reorganize the whole plan — execute one thing.
Capture the biggest takeaway
One sentence. For your own log, or for sharing.
