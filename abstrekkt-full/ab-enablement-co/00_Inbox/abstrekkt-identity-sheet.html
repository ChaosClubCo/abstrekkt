◆ Application

Do & Don't

Do

  • Set the wordmark in Fraunces on ink — that alone is a complete identity.
  • Use brass as the only chromatic accent; hierarchy elsewhere through fg tone.
  • Keep square corners on cards, panels, and frames.
  • Express elevation with border + background depth, never shadow.
  • Apply the ambient gradient once at root.
  • Reserve oxblood for error/destructive states — one per screen.

Don't

  • Don't build an icon/SVG library — ornaments are text only.
  • Don't use pure white; parchment #E8E0CC is the ceiling.
  • Don't add a second accent hue to the Reading Room system.
  • Don't mix FlashFusion tokens into Abstrekkt surfaces.
  • Don't set body text in mono or headings in Crimson Pro.
  • Don't re-introduce ChaosClubCo / Chaos Collective — retired.
