# Kyle's 2026 Brag Document

**Purpose**: Living record of impact, wins, and evidence for promotion case  
**Update frequency**: Daily (2 min) + Weekly synthesis (15 min) + Monthly (30 min)  
**Audience**: Self (primary), Manager (reviewed quarterly), Promotion committee (at review time)

---

## How to Use This Document

### Daily (2 minutes)
- **Evening**: Log 1-3 wins from today
- **Format**: Keep it short (1-2 sentences per win)
- **Capture**: What did you do? Who benefited? Any metrics?

### Weekly (15 minutes, Friday)
- **Synthesize**: What was the week's theme?
- **Patterns**: What recurring wins are happening?
- **Visibility**: Who needs to know about this?

### Monthly (30 minutes, end of month)
- **Narrative**: Tell the story of the month
- **Impact**: Quantify if possible (hours saved, security improved, etc.)
- **Relationships**: Who did you help? Who should you follow up with?

### Quarterly (1 hour, end of quarter)
- **Narrative**: Full quarter story for manager + skip-level
- **Metrics**: Aggregate impact numbers
- **Gaps**: Where are you still weak for promotion?

## TIPS FOR SUCCESS

### 1. Keep It Simple
Don't overthink entries. 1-2 sentences is fine. Aim for speed (2 min/day).

### 2. Be Specific
"Improved automation" → Bad  
"Reduced ticket triage time from 30 min to 10 min, saving platform team ~5 hrs/week" → Good

### 3. Think About Visibility
Each win should ask: "Who needs to know about this?"
- INT Inc colleagues need to know about work wins
- Manager needs to know for 1:1s
- Skip-level needs to know for promotion case

### 4. Track Relationships
Every conversation is an opportunity to strengthen ties. Log them.

### 5. Update Monthly
Don't wait until promotion review. Monthly synthesis keeps you honest and reveals patterns.

### 6. Share Quarterly
Show your manager (or skip-level) your quarterly synthesis. Gets them invested in your growth.

---

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Don't**: Wait until promotion review to document wins  
✅ **Do**: Log weekly, synthesize monthly

❌ **Don't**: Only track technical wins (code, systems)  
✅ **Do**: Track relationship wins, learning wins, visibility wins

❌ **Don't**: Assume manager knows about your work  
✅ **Do**: Explicitly share updates (brag doc helps)

❌ **Don't**: Ignore visibility  
✅ **Do**: Tell people about your wins (it's not bragging, it's information)

❌ **Don't**: Forget about metrics  
✅ **Do**: Quantify impact wherever possible (hours saved, security improved, revenue)
