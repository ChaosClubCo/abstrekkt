## PART D: ATTORNEY VETTING — HOW TO EVALUATE THE ATTORNEY

> These are not more questions to ask. These are how you evaluate the attorney BASED ON their answers and their conduct during the consultation.

### Green Flags
- **Immediately cites Wisconsin Statute 786.36** when you raise the name change issue. No looking it up, no hedging.
- **Knows Milwaukee County family court judges by name** and can speak to their tendencies on placement schedules.
- **Talks about shared placement formula and the 92-overnight threshold** without you prompting them.
- **Leads with offense on the sobriety issue** — reframes it as proof of current fitness, not a liability to manage.
- **Gives you a specific 30-day action plan**, not vague "we'll file some motions."
- **Asks about YOUR daughter's relationship with you** — shows they're building your narrative, not running a template case.
- **Asks about the mother's income**, not just yours — shows they understand the shared placement child support calculation.
- **Tells you what they think the case is worth** (realistically) before you ask.

### Red Flags
- Doesn't know 786.36 off the top of their head.
- Never mentions the 92-overnight threshold or shared vs. sole placement formulas.
- Leads with "this will be difficult given your substance use history" rather than "here's how we address that proactively."
- Vague on timeline — "these things take time" without specifics.
- Asks you almost no questions about your daughter, your relationship, or your history.
- Cannot name any Milwaukee County family court judges.
- Quotes a very low retainer and then buries the rest in hourly billing language.
- The senior attorney takes the consultation but a junior associate handles your case — clarify this immediately.

### Questions to Ask About the Attorney Themselves
- How many paternity/placement cases did you handle in Milwaukee County in the last 12 months specifically (not Wisconsin generally)?
- Who in your office would actually handle day-to-day work on my case? Can I meet them?
- What is your philosophy on co-parenting cases — are you trying to get me the maximum or establish a workable long-term arrangement?
- Have you represented a parent with a substance use history in their background? How did you approach that proactively?
- Have you appeared before the judges in Milwaukee County Family Court recently? What can you tell me about how they approach placement schedules in paternity cases?
- What's your communication style — email, phone, portal? What's your response time expectation?
