# Per-Role Runbooks

Half a page per role. Hand each person theirs. This is the layer that decides whether
the workspace gets used or abandoned. Pair with one 30-minute live walkthrough.

---

## Marketing (Ryan + team)
**Your projects:** Marketing & Content, Dealer Enablement, Product Knowledge Base
**Start here:**
1. Paste the member profile block; select the United Group Style when drafting.
2. "Draft a one-pager for the 4000 series for dispatch centers" → spec-sheet-generator.
3. "Write a dealer announcement for [update]" → dealer-comms.
**Daily wins:** web copy, campaign drafts, dealer materials, repurposing one asset across channels.

---

## Sales / Account Management
**Your projects:** Dealer Enablement, Lead Gen & Enrichment, Product Knowledge Base
**Start here:**
1. "Which IRON HORSE fits a 24/7 control room with big-and-tall users?" → Knowledge Base.
2. "Build a comparison sheet: 4000 vs 5000" → spec-sheet-generator.
3. "Draft follow-up to this inbound lead" → brand Style.
**Daily wins:** faster quotes-support, instant product answers, lead qualification.

---

## Operations
**Your projects:** Product Knowledge Base, Warranty & Customer Service
**Start here:**
1. Keep the Product Knowledge Base current — you own accuracy of specs/lead times.
2. "Summarize this week's warranty issues by failure mode" → Warranty project.
**Daily wins:** process docs, internal SOPs, knowledge-base upkeep.

---

## Customer Service
**Your projects:** Warranty & Customer Service, Product Knowledge Base
**Start here:**
1. Paste a customer warranty message → warranty-triage skill.
2. Review the draft, check the coverage read, send or escalate.
**Daily wins:** faster, consistent responses; nothing slips; clear escalation.

## Leadership (Paul)
**Your view:** the activity feed + the measurement tracker.

before/after numbers show time saved. You don't have to operate it — you sponsor it.
