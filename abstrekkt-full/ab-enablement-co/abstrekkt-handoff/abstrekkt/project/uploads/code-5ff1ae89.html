[STATUS: ACTIVE RECONSTRUCTION]
FIGURE 01: THE INITIAL WRECKAGE SITE

Recovery is a soft word. It suggests a return to a prior state of grace, a healing of tissue until the scar is invisible. But this is not recovery. This is construction. We are building something entirely new out of the timber of what was broken. The Card Table is where the sorting happens—the raw materials of memory laid out under cold, fluorescent light.

WALL NOTE #112

"The adhesive between memory and fact has degraded. Structural reinforcement required."

Every scrap of paper, every digital artifact, and every haunting fragment of dialogue is a component. On the Card Table, we don't look for meaning; we look for structural integrity. If a memory doesn't hold weight, it is discarded. If it bears the load of the narrative, it is pinned to The Wall.

[ORPHAN]

The Wall is where the architecture reveals itself. It is a lattice of string and pushpins, a physical manifestation of a psychological blueprint. Here, the 'Wall Notes' act as editorial asides—the inner dialogue of the engineer observing the human. We track the drift of the psyche as if it were a tectonic plate, measuring the millimeters of movement towards a stable future.

SYSTEM PULSE: NOMINAL

There is no room for sentiment in construction. If the foundation is cracked, you dig it out. You pour new concrete. You wait for it to cure. The transition from wreckage to residence is slow, methodical, and occasionally brutal. But at the end, there is a structure. Something that can withstand the wind.
