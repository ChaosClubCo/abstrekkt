### SEED 1: The Age Oscillation
**Source:** Storyteller Pro conversation
**What happened:** Kyle bounced between "it started at 19" → "this started at age like 21" → "i was 19" across three consecutive messages.
**Status update (Apr 2026):** PARTIALLY RESOLVED — but more interesting than before.
Confirmed substance timeline (from Amen eval + excavation sessions):
- Nicotine: 2005, age ~19
- Marijuana: 2007, age ~21
- Xanax: prescribed 2015, became extreme addiction
- Cocaine: ~20x 2018–2021
- Heroin: 2021, OD Sept 2021
- Hypoxia event: summer 2020 (Ambien + Xanax)
**What this means:** Kyle wasn't misremembering. He was conflating two different firsts — nicotine at 19, marijuana at 21. Both are real. Neither is a lie. The oscillation is the correct answer wearing the shape of confusion.
**New potential use:** The book can now name this explicitly. "I told it I was 19. Then I said 21. I wasn't wrong either time. Nicotine at 19. Weed at 21. My memory didn't fail me — it failed to distinguish between two events that both feel like the same before-and-after. That's a different problem."
**Emotional charge:** High (upgraded from medium-high). The resolution makes it more devastating, not less.
**Remaining excavation:** What does the first cigarette feel like vs. the first hit? Are they the same relief or different flavors of it?
