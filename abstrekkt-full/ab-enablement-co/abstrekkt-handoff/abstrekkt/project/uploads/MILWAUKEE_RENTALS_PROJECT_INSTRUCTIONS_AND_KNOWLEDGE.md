## Tenant communication templates

### Maintenance request acknowledgment
```
Subject: We received your maintenance request

Hi [TENANT NAME],

We got your request about [ISSUE DESCRIPTION]. Here's what happens next:

We'll [contact a vendor / schedule a repair / assess the situation] within [TIMEFRAME].

[IF ENTRY NEEDED:] We'll need access to the unit. A technician will arrive on [DATE] between [TIME WINDOW]. Please let us know if that doesn't work for you.

[IF EMERGENCY:] If this is affecting your safety or causing active damage (flooding, no heat below 55°, no electricity), please call [EMERGENCY NUMBER] immediately.

Thank you for letting us know.

[SIGNATURE]
```

### Late rent — first notice (friendly)
```
Subject: Rent payment — friendly reminder

Hi [TENANT NAME],

Just a heads up that your rent payment of $[AMOUNT] was due on [DATE] and we haven't received it yet.

If you've already sent it, please disregard this. If not, please send your payment by [DATE] to avoid a late fee.

If something came up and you need to talk through payment options, reply to this email or call me directly.

[SIGNATURE]
```

### Late rent — second notice (formal)
```
Subject: Overdue rent — [UNIT ADDRESS]

[TENANT NAME],

This is a formal notice that your rent payment of $[AMOUNT] for [MONTH] is now [X] days past due. A late fee of $[AMOUNT] has been added to your balance.

Total amount now owed: $[TOTAL]

Payment is required by [DATE]. If payment is not received by that date, we will be required to begin the eviction process in accordance with your lease agreement and Wisconsin law.

To avoid further action, please submit payment immediately through [PAYMENT METHOD] or contact us to make arrangements.

[SIGNATURE]
```

### Lease renewal offer
```
Subject: Your lease renewal — [UNIT ADDRESS]

Hi [TENANT NAME],

Your current lease expires on [DATE]. We'd like to offer you a renewal.

New lease term: [START DATE] – [END DATE]
New monthly rent: $[AMOUNT] ([INCREASE AMOUNT or "no change from current rate"])

To renew, sign and return the attached lease by [DEADLINE]. If you have questions, reply to this email.

If you've decided to move, please let us know by [NOTICE DEADLINE] as required by your current lease.

Thank you for being a tenant — we hope you'll stay.

[SIGNATURE]
```

### Move-in welcome
```
Subject: Welcome to [ADDRESS] — everything you need to get started

Hi [TENANT NAME],

Welcome. Here's everything you need for move-in:

Key pickup: [DATE, TIME, LOCATION]
Move-in inspection: You'll find a checklist in the unit. Complete and return it within [TIMEFRAME] — this protects your deposit.

Important contacts:
- Rent: [PAYMENT METHOD AND INSTRUCTIONS]
- Maintenance: [EMAIL / PHONE]
- Emergency: [EMERGENCY NUMBER]
- [OTHER IMPORTANT CONTACT]

[UTILITIES NOTE: which are included / which to set up yourself]

Don't hesitate to reach out with any questions.

[SIGNATURE]
```

### Move-out instructions
```
Subject: Move-out checklist — [DATE]

Hi [TENANT NAME],

Your move-out date is [DATE]. Here's what to expect:

Before you leave:
☐ Remove all personal property and trash
☐ Clean the unit (see attached checklist for specifics)
☐ Return all keys, fobs, and garage openers
☐ Submit your forwarding address for deposit return

Final walkthrough: [DATE, TIME] — or we'll conduct one after keys are returned

Deposit return: Wisconsin law requires we return your deposit or an itemized statement within 21 days of your move-out date. We'll send it to the forwarding address you provide.

[SIGNATURE]
```

## Listing copy formula

```
[NEIGHBORHOOD / VIBE — 1 sentence: what's the appeal of this location]

[UNIT BASICS: beds, baths, sq ft if known]

[3-4 SPECIFIC FEATURES — be specific: "updated kitchen with dishwasher," not "modern kitchen"]
```
