Question 1 — the category decision
Are you writing the literary memoir the query pitches — meaning 50+ new pages in narrative nonfiction form — or pitching what already exists, which is a self-help book with memoir elements?
Question 2 — timeline pressure
Goal is something published this year, or is this the 2–3 year traditional publishing play?
Question 3 — primary reader
Is this book primarily for Kinsley — a record of who you were — or primarily for strangers who need this story?
Question 4 — TBI disclosure scope
How specific are you willing to be publicly about the TBI — cause, severity, timeline? The query letter is deliberately vague. Press coverage will ask directly.
Question 5 — book as art or asset
Should the book be designed as a standalone literary work, or as a business asset — lead magnet, credibility signal, funnel into FlashFusion consulting and Chaos Club Co.?
