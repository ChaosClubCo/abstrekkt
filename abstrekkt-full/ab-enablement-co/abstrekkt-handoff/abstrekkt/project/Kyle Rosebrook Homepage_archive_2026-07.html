§ About

The path wasn't linear.

I bartended at O'Toole's in my twenties. I worked in quality control. I taught myself to program in the margins of jobs that didn't require it. Somewhere in there, the cognitive architecture I was born with stopped working the way it used to — by injury, by choice, by circumstance, depending on which part of the story you ask about.

Recovery assumes there's something to go back to. That wasn't the situation. So instead I started building — tools, systems, documentation, frameworks — and slowly figured out that what I was actually building was a replacement self, made out of non-standard materials, on a timeline nobody recommended.

This site is where I make that work legible. What I'm building, why I'm building it that way, and what it has to do with what I had to walk through to get here.
