Theory of Explanatory Depth

Understanding fails where explanation cannot continue.

"I test ideas until they survive reality."

◈ Core Claim

The value of an idea is proportional to the depth of understanding it generates. When you can't explain it further, you don't understand it enough. Explanatory depth is a primary signal for truth, robustness, and transfer.

★ Why It Matters

Surface-level understanding creates fragile results. Depth creates leverage. This principle helps you build models that generalize, teach clearly, and survive new domains, stress, and time.

⚠ Failure Modes
  • Stopping at the first plausible story.
  • Confusing description with explanation.
  • Relying on authority instead of reasoning.
  • Accepting jargon as understanding.
  • Ignoring edge cases and counterexamples.
? Questions to Use
  • Can I explain this in simpler terms?
  • What is the first principle underneath?
  • What would falsify this?
  • How would I teach it to a beginner?
  • Where does this explanation break?
✐ Model Sketch
Observation→ Pattern→ Hypothesis→ Mechanism→ Principle⇢ Transfer

↺ Test & Refine

Move iteratively from what is seen to what is true. Each level must be explainable before moving deeper.
