The Card Table and The Wall

A forensic study in fragmented memory, deliberate assembly, and near-fatal dissolution.

[01.0 - DISINTEGRATION]

The card table was where the debris first began to take shape. It wasn't about recovery then; it was about inventory of survival. I started with the scraps—the pawns tickets, the torn photographs from before, the handwritten notes on dirty napkins. They were laid out not chronologically, but by their weight in my chest and the cost of the next fix.

[01.1 - HARSH VERTICALITY]

Then came the wall. Gravity demanded that the horizontal chaos of the table find a vertical order, a desperate attempt to prove I was still standing. I pinned a single thread—a stained red string—across the cracked drywall. It wasn't a timeline. It was a structural axis. Everything pinned above the line was "Survival." Everything below was "The Wreckage."

Cognitive Disruption Detected

The Plumb Line

If the wall was the site of the accident, the plumb line was the cold, harsh truth. It didn't care for narrative flow or the comfort of a "beginning, middle, and end." It only cared for alignment against the shakes. I spent nights, sweating and cold, adjusting the tension of that string, making sure the reconstruction wouldn't lean. The past is a heavy, dirty material; if you don't build straight, the street eventually swallows the present.

Evidence Tag: 44A

"The table is a map of where I lost it. The wall is a blueprint of how to crawl back. Do not confuse the two."

Psych Eval Note

Use rust-covered pins for fixed traumas. Use cheap tape for fleeting clarity. The architecture is precarious until the withdrawals stop.
