The image is burned into the glass of my mind. A wide shot, almost cinematic, taken from within the belly of the beast—Trump Tower, Chicago. Floor-to-ceiling windows turning the city into a diorama of steel and bruised sky.

I always remembered five of us standing there. A solid wall of intent. Matt, Tom, Dave, Ryan, and myself. It made sense logically; we were the unit. The money had just cleared. The settlement. Fifty thousand dollars sitting in a shared account, heavy as lead.

Factual anomaly detected. Referencing photographic evidence IMG_47.

But the photo only shows three. Three silhouettes against the glass. It’s a jarring correction, a slip in the tape. The emotional truth of the moment demanded all of us be there, absorbing the gravity of what we'd done, what we'd won. The factual correction—that Ryan was absent, that perhaps I was holding the camera—feels thinner than the memory.
