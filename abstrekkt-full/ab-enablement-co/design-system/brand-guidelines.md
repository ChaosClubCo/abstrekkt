# Brand Guidelines

    _Generated: 2026-06-21. Reconstruction confidence is bounded by accessible project memory/context and the current chat session._


## Visual Direction

Dark, sharp, practical, AI-forward, not generic AI blob aesthetics. The colorful robot/lightning identity can be used as a friendly FlashFusion symbol, but business-facing materials should stay credible and restrained.

## Copy Rules

Use practical language: workflows, revenue, bottlenecks, adoption, training, risk, implementation. Avoid unverifiable authority claims.
