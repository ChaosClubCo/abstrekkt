    _Generated: 2026-06-21. Reconstruction confidence is bounded by accessible project memory/context and the current chat session._


## Patterns

- Progressive disclosure for complex audits.
- Inline evidence tags for every generated claim.
- Review status badges: draft, needs evidence, approved, rejected, archived.
- Confidence indicators: supported, inferred, assumed.
- Destructive actions require confirmation and permission check.
