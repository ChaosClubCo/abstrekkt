## PART F: SOURCING METHODOLOGY

### Search Strategy

1. **Primary sources:** NIST, OWASP, MITRE official documentation and publications
2. **Enterprise research:** McKinsey, Deloitte, PwC, Gartner, Stanford AI Index (peer-reviewed)
3. **Industry analysis:** Qualified advisory firms (Nightfall, Securiti, Bright Defense, Comp AI, Schellman)
4. **Product-specific:** Anthropic official Privacy Center, API docs, newsroom
5. **News & analysis:** Reputable tech publications (Dataconomy, CIO Magazine, ISACA)

### Quality Criteria

- **Currency:** All sources published 2023 or later; preference for 2024-2025
- **Credibility:** Government bodies, 401(c)(3) research institutes, Big 3 consulting, AICPA
- **Verifiability:** All URLs valid and accessible as of Dec 11, 2025
- **Relevance:** Sources directly support claims in taxonomy v4.1

### Verification Notes

- **NIST AI RMF:** Official release Jan 26, 2023; generative AI profile July 26, 2024 (NIST-AI-600-1)
- **OWASP Top 10 LLM:** November 2024 release with 5 new vulnerabilities vs. v1.0
- **McKinsey 78% adoption:** Confirmed in multiple 2025 reports (State of AI, Superagency)
- **Stanford 56.4% incident increase:** AI Index Report 2025 (published Q1 2025)
- **IBM $4.45M breach cost:** 2024 Cost of Data Breach Report; AI cases $650K+ higher
- **Anthropic ZDR policy:** Verified via Privacy Center as of Sept 15, 2025 API log reduction
