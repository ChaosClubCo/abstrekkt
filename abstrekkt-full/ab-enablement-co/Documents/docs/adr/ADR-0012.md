# ADR-0012 — Schedule Governance

    _Generated: 2026-06-21. Reconstruction confidence is bounded by accessible project memory/context and the current chat session._


## Status
Accepted for target-state reconstruction.

## Old Direction
Many automations can become noise.

## New Direction
Every recurring schedule must produce concrete action at least twice per month or be paused/reworked.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep all schedules
- Only daily briefings
- Manual reminders

## Tradeoffs
Reduces attention waste; may pause useful long-tail monitoring.

## Risk Analysis
Risk of missing slow-burn opportunities; mitigated by watchlist exceptions.

## Migration Impact
Weekly Business Review includes schedule action-yield audit.

## Recommended Direction
Use action-yield as schedule KPI.
