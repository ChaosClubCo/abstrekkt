# Sora 2 Director Spec vNEXT (2025-12) – Working Rules

- Prompt length: keep under 900–1100 characters of semantic content per clip.
- Camera: only ONE deliberate move per clip (pan *or* tilt *or* push *or* track *or* whip).
- Beat timing: define 3–5 labeled ranges covering the full duration.
- Negative prompts: always include motion, exposure, and anatomy stability constraints.
- Reference frames: identify 2–3 anchor frames per clip (start / mid / end) for QA.
- Rascal / creature rules: lock anatomy, proportions, and fur/skin pattern; no morphing between cuts.
- Lighting: keep a consistent key/rim vector across a sequence; avoid WB and exposure automation.
- Micro-gesture drift: cap pose drift and idle animations so characters don’t “float” between beats.
- Emotional variants: for dialogue, define baseline emotional tone and one controlled escalation point.
