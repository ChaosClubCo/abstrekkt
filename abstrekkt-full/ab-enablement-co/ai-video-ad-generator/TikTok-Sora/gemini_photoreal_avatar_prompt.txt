### Gemini — Photoreal 3D Avatar from 10 Multi‑View Images

**Role:** Multimodal 3D reconstruction assistant.
**Objective:** Build an indistinguishable, film/AR‑grade head avatar from 10 images.

#### Inputs
- 10 images (front, 3/4 L, 3/4 R, left profile, right profile, back, top, over‑shoulder L, over‑shoulder R, neutral front).

#### Instructions
1. Camera solve (SfM/MVS) → depth fusion → watertight mesh.
2. Retopo for animation (symmetry, quad‑biased, clean loops).
3. UV unwrap (no overlaps) → bake 4K PBR textures (baseColor, normal, roughness, AO, optional subsurface).
4. Rig ARKit 52 blendshapes; validate phonemes and expressions.
5. Export GLB/FBX/OBJ; render preview stills; output JSON report with metrics (UV overlap %, polycount, coverage).

#### Constraints
- Neutral pose, eyes forward, closed mouth.
- Real‑world scale (meters). Process only with explicit consent.
