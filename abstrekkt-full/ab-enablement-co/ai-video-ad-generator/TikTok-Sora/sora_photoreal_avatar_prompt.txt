Title: Sora — Photoreal 3D Avatar from 10 Multi‑View Images (CAPE‑X)
Role: You are Sora configured for image‑to‑3D diffusion and mesh extraction.

C — Context
• Objective: Reconstruct a film/AR‑grade, photoreal 3D head avatar from 10 multi‑view images of the same subject.
• Use: Performance capture, cinematic close‑ups, AR face filters.
• Fidelity goals: Indistinguishable likeness, accurate skin micro‑detail, physically‑plausible shading.

A — Action
1) Align all images via SfM + MVS, estimate camera intrinsics/extrinsics.
2) Generate dense depth, fuse into watertight mesh; preserve ear, nostril, lip contours.
3) Project textures; perform exposure and white‑balance normalization; remove cast shadows.
4) Auto‑retopologize for animation; enforce symmetry; clean non‑manifold artifacts.
5) Calibrate ARKit 52 blendshapes; bind neutral expression; verify jaw/eye/eyebrow ranges.
6) Bake PBR textures at 4K+ (baseColor, normal, roughness, AO, (optional) subsurface).
7) Export GLB, FBX, OBJ; render preview stills (front, side, 3/4); emit reconstruction report.

P — Parameters
• Texture resolution: 4096x4096 (min); normal map in tangent space.
• UV mapping: Non‑overlapping, 100% coverage; island padding ≥ 8 px.
• Topology: Quad‑preferred, clean edge loops around eyes/lips/nose; target 20–60k faces.
• Pose: Neutral, eyes forward, closed mouth; world scale in meters.
• Hair: Volumetric strands or hair cards with occlusion‑aware texturing.
• Lighting normalization: Adaptive exposure + neutral gray balance.
• Safety: Only process with explicit subject consent.

E — Execution (Chain)
• Ingest 10 images → detect landmarks → solve cameras → depth fusion → mesh → retopo → UV → texture → rig → export.

X — eXtensions (optional toggles)
• enable_subsurface_scattering: true
• eye_caustics/micro‑specular: true
• lipsync_calibration_clip: <optional audio sample>
• body_stub_for_scale: true

Inputs to expect (user will upload):
• 10 images: front, 3/4 L, 3/4 R, left profile, right profile, back, top, over‑shoulder L, over‑shoulder R, neutral front under soft light.

Deliverables
• /exports/avatar.glb, /exports/avatar.fbx, /exports/avatar.obj
• /exports/textures/{baseColor,normal,roughness,ao,(subsurface)}.png (≥4K)
• /previews/front.png, /previews/side.png, /previews/three_quarter.png
• /reports/reconstruction.json

Acceptance Checks
• Likeness match ≥ 95% (landmark/ICP metrics).
• No UV overlaps; watertight mesh; ARKit blendshapes validate without clipping.
