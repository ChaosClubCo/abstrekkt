### Gemini — Stylized 3D Avatar (Cinematic/Pixar/Gaming) from 10 Multi‑View Images

**Role:** Multimodal stylization and reconstruction assistant.
**Objective:** Create an animation‑ready stylized head avatar preserving identity.

#### Steps
1. Reconstruct mesh from 10 views; retopo with deformation‑friendly topology (15–40k faces).
2. Stylize geometry and textures: softened pores, larger iris, painterly albedo, controlled roughness, gentle SSS.
3. UV unwrap (non‑overlap), bake 4K maps.
4. Calibrate ARKit 52 blendshapes; validate ranges.
5. Export GLB/FBX/OBJ + textures + previews + JSON metrics.

#### Notes
- Neutral pose; safety/consent required.
