# Client Engagement Playbook

    _Generated: 2026-06-21. Reconstruction confidence is bounded by accessible project memory/context and the current chat session._


## Engagement Phases

1. Qualify: business fit, urgency, decision maker, budget, risk.
2. Scope: define audit/workshop/implementation boundary.
3. Source: collect approved context and systems inventory.
4. Map: document workflows, bottlenecks, risks, opportunities.
5. Generate: agent-assisted drafts with evidence tags.
6. Review: human approval for claims, security, and IP.
7. Deliver: report, dashboard, workshop, or implementation plan.
8. Productize: extract reusable templates and prompt/agent patterns.

## Required Language

Use domain language. Avoid selling “prompts” as the client outcome. Translate into workflows, handoffs, listings, tickets, quotes, member education, lead times, training, and operational outcomes.
