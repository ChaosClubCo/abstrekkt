## Business Model

FlashFusion sells AI enablement through productized consulting, paid audits, workshops, implementation packages, and reusable client dashboards. The strategic direction is to build cashflow from services while converting repeated work into software modules and agent libraries.

## Strategic Positioning

FlashFusion is not generic AI hype. It is a practical operating layer for leaders who need to identify where AI belongs, what to automate safely, what to ignore, what to watch, and what to act on.

## Revenue Stages

1. Paid readiness audits.
2. Workshops and department/team education.
3. Implementation retainers.
4. Reusable dashboard subscriptions.
5. Product modules for commerce/studio automation.
