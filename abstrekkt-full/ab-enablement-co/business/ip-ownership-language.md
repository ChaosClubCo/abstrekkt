## Standard Clause

FlashFusion retains ownership of its pre-existing and independently developed intellectual property, including frameworks, curricula, workflows, templates, prompts, agent definitions, software patterns, evaluation methods, and operating playbooks. Client receives a license to use final deliverables created specifically for the engagement within their organization, unless a separate written agreement grants broader rights.

## Plain-English Explanation

The client owns their business data and receives the agreed deliverables. FlashFusion keeps the reusable methods and systems that make delivery possible.
