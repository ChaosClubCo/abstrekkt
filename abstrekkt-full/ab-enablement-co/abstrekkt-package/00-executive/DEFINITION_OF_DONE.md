Done when **all** of the following are true and evidenced:

1. **LLC filed** — Illinois SOS entity search (apps.ilsos.gov/businessentitysearch) returns "FlashFusion LLC" (or chosen name) as active, OR a filing confirmation email exists. Forms-prepared ≠ done. [SOURCED — instructions]
2. **EIN issued** — obtained from irs.gov after filing confirms. [SOURCED — llc kit]
3. **Business bank account open** — dedicated, no commingling. [SOURCED — llc kit]
4. **Employment-IP cleared** — attorney has confirmed the day-job employment agreement does not assign Abstrekkt/FlashFusion work to the employer; written opinion on file. [SOURCED — llc kit]
5. **E&O bound** — Tech E&O with an affirmative-AI rider is *bound* (not quoted), from a provider that does not carry the industry-standard AI exclusion. Budget $2,000–$3,500/yr. [SOURCED — instructions]
6. **MSA ready** — a reviewed master services agreement template exists for client signature. [SOURCED — llc kit]
