# Breathe Through the Bullsh*t™ – Brand Identity Board
## Tagline:
“Because ‘just breathe’ doesn’t f*cking cut it.”
## Brand Overview:
Breathe Through the Bullsh*t™ is a bold, soulful, slightly rebellious personal brand and flagship course. It exists for the emotionally fried, the spiritually spicy, and the trauma-healing misfits who need more than just fluffy advice.
## Sub-Brand:
Chaos Club™ – The merch and micro-membership community for the healing misfits.
## Brand Keywords:
Bold, Soulful, Slightly Rebellious
## Typography:
- Headlines: Rough brush or hand-drawn fonts
- Body: Modern sans-serif for clean readability
- Alternate: Gritty serif or typewriter-style for emotional emphasis
## Color Palette:
- Earth tones: warm browns, clay, deep forest
- Accents: burnt orange, deep teal, blood red, dirty pink
- Optional pops: muted electric purple or yellow for chaos moments
## Visual Style & Imagery:
- Torn paper textures
- Scribbles, underlines, raw notebook doodles
- Expressive faces (grief, laughter, catharsis)
- Icons: brain, storm cloud, heart, peace-sign with edge
Use this board as your north star when designing everything from your website to merch to course visuals.
