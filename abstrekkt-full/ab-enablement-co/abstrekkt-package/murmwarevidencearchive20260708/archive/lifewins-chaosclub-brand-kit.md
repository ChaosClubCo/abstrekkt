Cultural Pillars
• Radical authenticity: Members are encouraged to share the messy, imperfect parts of their journey
• Celebration of small wins: Micro-progress is honored just as much as big breakthroughs
• Peer mentorship: Veteran members organically guide and support newer members
• Consent-first interaction: Trauma-aware community agreements are visible and reinforced
• Branded rituals: Phrases, hashtags, and in-jokes create a unique club identity (ex: “beautiful disaster tribe,” “breathing through the bullsh*t,” “own your chaos”)
Tone & Vibe
• Witty but compassionate
• Bold but inclusive
• Irreverent but trauma-informed
• Growth-focused but never perfectionist
• Fun-first: The community is a sanctuary where people can exhale, not just “self-improve”
