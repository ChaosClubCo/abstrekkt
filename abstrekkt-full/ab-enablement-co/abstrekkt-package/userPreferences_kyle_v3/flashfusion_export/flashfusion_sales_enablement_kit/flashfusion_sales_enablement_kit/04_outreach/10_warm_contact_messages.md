# 10 Warm Contact Outreach Messages

## 1. Realtor / broker

**Why this angle fits:** You already have a warm realtor lead path. Focus on missed follow-up, listing content, buyer/seller intake, CRM hygiene, and team adoption.

**Message:**

Hi [Name], I’m putting together a small fixed-scope AI Workflow Readiness Assessment for real estate teams. The goal is not to add another tool — it is to find one workflow like lead follow-up, listing prep, or client intake where AI can save measurable time without losing human control. If you are curious, I can show you the sample report and get your take.

## 2. Home services contractor

**Why this angle fits:** Lead intake, estimates, dispatch notes, and follow-up are common pain points.

**Message:**

Hi [Name], I’m testing a practical AI assessment for service businesses that have leads, estimates, and follow-ups spread across forms, email, texts, and CRM notes. It maps one workflow, scores readiness, and identifies the safest first automation. I think it could fit the way contractors actually operate. Want me to send the sample?

## 3. Local restaurant / hospitality owner

I’d be interested in showing you the sample and hearing whether it matches real pain on your end.
