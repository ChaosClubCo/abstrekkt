# Follow-Up Sequence

## Follow-up 1 — 3 to 4 days later

Hi [Name], quick follow-up on this. I’m not trying to pitch a giant AI project — the assessment is just a practical way to identify one workflow where AI could save time without creating risk. Happy to send the sample report if useful.

## Follow-up 2 — 7 to 10 days later

Hi [Name], closing the loop here. I’m collecting feedback from local business owners/operators on a fixed-scope AI Workflow Readiness Assessment. If AI is not a priority right now, no worries. If it is, I can share the sample and get your take.

## If they reply with interest

Great — I’ll send the sample. The main thing I’m looking for is whether the deliverable feels useful and whether the first workflow recommendation would be clear enough for a business owner to act on. If it looks relevant, we can also talk through what this would look like for your team.

## If they ask for pricing

The standard assessment is $750 and includes a workflow map, readiness score, opportunity ranking, and 30-day roadmap. If it leads to an implementation sprint within 14 days, I credit $250 of the assessment toward the build.
