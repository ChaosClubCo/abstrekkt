# FlashFusion Final Launch Playbook

## Purpose

This kit turns FlashFusion from a broad AI consulting idea into a sellable, fixed-scope service system. The flagship offer is the **AI Workflow Readiness Assessment**. The supporting offer is the **AI Productivity Sprint**. The landing pages, outreach messages, demo, proposal, discovery script, and case study templates all point back to that sequence.

## Positioning

FlashFusion helps small teams turn AI curiosity into safe, repeatable workflows. The message is intentionally practical: assess the workflow, identify the highest-value use case, build one measurable automation, train the team, and capture results.

Use this line consistently:

> FlashFusion helps small businesses find the one workflow where AI can save real time before they buy another tool or attempt a messy automation project.

## The offer ladder

1. **AI Workflow Readiness Assessment** — fixed-scope entry offer. Best first sale.
2. **AI Productivity Sprint** — one working automation based on the assessment findings.
3. **AI Team Workshop** — practical training for staff and leadership.
4. **Internal Knowledge AI Package** — organize scattered documents into an AI-ready knowledge system.
5. **AI Governance Starter Kit** — policies, approved tools, human review points, and usage guardrails.
6. **Monthly AI Optimization Review** — recurring advisory, measurement, and workflow improvement.

## What to do first

Do not lead with every offer. Lead with the readiness assessment. Use the Productivity Sprint demo only after the prospect understands the problem.

### First 48 hours

1. Open `01_flagship_assessment/AI_Workflow_Readiness_Assessment_One_Pager.md` and convert it into a PDF or page.
2. Open `01_flagship_assessment/Sample_Assessment_Report_Fictional_HVAC.md` and use it as the sample deliverable.
3. Open `02_productivity_sprint_demo/index.html` in a browser and rehearse the talk track.
4. Pick 10 warm contacts and paste them into `04_outreach/send_queue_top_10.csv`.
5. Send five personalized messages on day one and five on day two.
6. Track replies in `04_outreach/outreach_tracker.csv`.
7. If anyone replies positively, use `06_sales_ops/discovery_call_script.md`.
8. After the call, send `06_sales_ops/proposal_template.md` with a fixed price and clear deliverables.

## Sales call flow

1. Start with their workflow pain, not AI tools.
2. Ask where manual work repeats every week.
3. Ask where information gets lost: inboxes, CRMs, spreadsheets, documents, Slack/Teams, texts, or notebooks.
4. Ask what cannot be automated without human approval.
5. Show the Productivity Sprint demo for one minute.
6. Say: “This is not the exact solution. It shows the kind of workflow we can map, simplify, and automate safely.”
7. Offer the readiness assessment as the low-risk next step.

## Pricing guidance

Use simple pricing until proof exists.

- Introductory pilot: $497
- Standard assessment: $750
- Deeper assessment with report and roadmap: $997
- Productivity Sprint: $1,500–$3,500 depending on complexity
- Team workshop: $750–$1,500
- Monthly optimization: $250–$750/month

Do not discount by making the scope vague. If you discount, reduce scope.

## Proof system

Every engagement must produce an anonymized case study. Capture before/after metrics even if they are rough estimates.

Minimum metrics:

- Hours saved per week
- Number of workflow steps removed
- Response time reduction
- Error reduction
- Adoption rate
- Tools consolidated
- Follow-up opportunities identified

Use `05_case_studies/case_study_capture_form.md` immediately after delivery. Do not wait a month to document the result.

## Quality gates

Every client recommendation should pass these checks:

1. The problem is a real workflow issue, not just “add AI.”
2. There is a human approval point for customer-facing, financial, legal, HR, or sensitive actions.
3. The automation can be explained to a nontechnical owner.
4. The client can tell whether it worked within 30 days.
5. The deliverable produces a reusable asset for FlashFusion.

## What not to do

Do not build a SaaS before selling the assessment. Do not promise autonomous AI agents as the first engagement. Do not lead with model names. Do not do open-ended “AI consulting” by the hour. Do not overbuild the demo before using it in sales calls.

## Definition of done

This package is ready when these are complete:

- One flagship offer can be sent to a prospect.
- One demo can be shown in a call.
- One landing page can be published.
- Ten outreach messages can be sent.
- One discovery call can be run.
- One proposal can be sent.
- One completed project can become a case study.
