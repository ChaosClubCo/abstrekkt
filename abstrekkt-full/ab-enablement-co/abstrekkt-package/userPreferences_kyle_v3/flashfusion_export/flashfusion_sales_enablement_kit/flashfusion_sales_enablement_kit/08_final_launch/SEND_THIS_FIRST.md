# Send This First

Use this before anything else. Personalize the bracketed parts and send it to warm contacts who already know you or are one degree away.

## Warm contact message

Hi [Name], I’m starting to offer a focused AI Workflow Readiness Assessment through FlashFusion.

It’s for small businesses that are curious about AI but not sure what to automate first, which tools are worth using, or where AI could actually save time without creating chaos.

The assessment maps one real workflow, identifies the top automation opportunities, flags risks, and gives a practical 30-day roadmap.

I’m looking for a few local businesses to run through the first version. Would it be worth a quick conversation to see if this fits anything you’re dealing with?

## Shorter version

Hi [Name], I’m piloting a FlashFusion AI Workflow Readiness Assessment for small businesses. It helps identify one workflow where AI can realistically save time before buying more tools or building automations. Would you be open to a quick conversation to see if it fits anything on your plate?

## If they ask what it costs

The pilot version is $497. It includes a workflow review, readiness score, top automation opportunities, and a practical 30-day roadmap. If the workflow is clearly not a fit after the intro call, I’ll say that before recommending anything.

## If they ask what kind of business it fits

It fits businesses where work still moves through inboxes, spreadsheets, repeated customer messages, manual follow-ups, inconsistent documentation, or CRM/process gaps. Realtors, contractors, local service businesses, professional services, and small internal teams are good examples.

## If they say “we already use AI”

That’s actually a good reason to do it. A lot of teams use AI informally, but they don’t have consistent workflows, approval points, or measurable outcomes. The assessment helps turn scattered AI use into something repeatable.

## If they say “we are not ready for AI”

That’s exactly what the assessment is for. It does not assume you are ready. It identifies what needs to be cleaned up first: workflow steps, documentation, data, ownership, risk, and where a simple automation could help.
