# FlashFusion AI Workflow Readiness Assessment

## One-line promise

Identify the one workflow where AI can save measurable time, reduce operational drag, and create a realistic 30-day implementation path—before the business buys another tool.

## Best-fit customers

Small and midsize businesses that are interested in AI but are stuck in one or more of these patterns:

1. They have scattered documentation, inconsistent handoffs, or tribal knowledge.
2. They use spreadsheets, inboxes, CRMs, or shared drives as unofficial workflow systems.
3. They have tried ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, or Claude but have not turned usage into repeatable process.
4. They want automation but do not know what should stay human-controlled.
5. They need leadership-level clarity before investing in AI tools, training, or implementation.

## Core deliverables

1. AI readiness score across workflow clarity, documentation, data quality, tool maturity, risk, and implementation feasibility.
2. Current-state workflow map for one high-value process.
3. Bottleneck and rework analysis.
4. Existing tool and AI usage inventory.
5. Human approval map for sensitive steps.
6. Top 5 AI-enabled opportunities ranked by effort, value, risk, and speed-to-impact.
7. 30-day implementation roadmap.
8. Executive summary suitable for the owner, department head, or board.

## Sales qualification questions

1. What work repeats every week that still depends on manual follow-up?
2. Where does information get lost: inbox, CRM, spreadsheet, shared drive, chat, or someone's head?
3. What AI tools are employees already using?
4. What would be risky to automate without human approval?
5. What would make this worth the investment: hours saved, faster response time, fewer errors, better follow-up, or less rework?

## Buyer-safe pitch

Most businesses do not need to automate everything. They need to pick the right first workflow. FlashFusion helps map the current process, expose the bottlenecks, and identify one practical AI-enabled improvement that can be tested within 30 days.

## Delivery timeline

Day 1: Intake and workflow selection.

Day 2–3: Discovery session, artifact review, and workflow mapping.

Day 4–5: Readiness scoring, opportunity ranking, roadmap, and executive summary.

Day 6–7: Readout and recommended next step.

## Success metrics

Use at least one primary metric and one supporting metric.

Primary metrics: hours saved per week, lead response time, customer response time, turnaround time, manual handoff reduction, rework reduction.

Supporting metrics: number of tools consolidated, number of SOPs created, number of approval checkpoints clarified, number of prompts/workflows standardized.
