# AI Workflow Readiness Assessment Delivery Checklist

## Before the call

- Send intake form.
- Ask for screenshots or exports of the workflow tools.
- Ask client to pick one workflow, not five.
- Confirm what AI should never do without approval.

## During discovery

- Map the current workflow from trigger to final outcome.
- Identify every handoff, delay, duplicate entry, and tool switch.
- Mark steps as human-only, AI-assisted, or automatable.
- Capture baseline metrics.
- Confirm one measurable success target.

## After discovery

- Score readiness using the scorecard.
- Rank top 5 opportunities.
- Pick one first implementation.
- Draft 30-day roadmap.
- Write executive summary.
- Prepare readout.

## Quality gate

Do not recommend an automation unless the workflow has:

1. Clear trigger.
2. Clear inputs.
3. Clear owner.
4. Clear output.
5. Human approval boundary.
6. Measurable before/after metric.
