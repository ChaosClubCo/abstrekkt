# FlashFusion AI Productivity Sprint

## One-line promise

Automate one repetitive workflow with human approval, usable templates, and measurable impact—without turning the business into a science project.

## Best first demo workflow

Lead intake → qualification → CRM-ready summary → human-approved follow-up.

This works well in sales calls because almost every business understands missed leads, inconsistent notes, slow follow-up, and CRM friction.

## Sprint deliverables

1. Finalized workflow map.
2. Automation design.
3. AI prompt and output schema.
4. Human approval checkpoint.
5. CRM-ready data structure.
6. Follow-up email/message template.
7. Simple weekly summary report.
8. Staff handoff guide.

## Suggested price

Starter Sprint: $1,500–$2,500

Expanded Sprint: $3,000–$5,000 if integrations, multiple channels, or team training are included.

## Sales-call script

"Instead of trying to automate the whole business, this sprint improves one repetitive process. We start with a workflow that already happens every week, keep sensitive decisions human-approved, and measure whether it saves time or reduces missed follow-ups."
