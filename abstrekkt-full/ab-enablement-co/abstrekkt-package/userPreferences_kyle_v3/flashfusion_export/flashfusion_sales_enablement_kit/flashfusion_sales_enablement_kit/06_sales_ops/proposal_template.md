# FlashFusion Proposal Template

## Project

AI Workflow Readiness Assessment for [Client]

## Objective

Identify the highest-value AI-enabled workflow opportunity, define human approval boundaries, and create a 30-day implementation roadmap.

## Scope

Included:

- Intake review
- 90-minute discovery session
- One workflow map
- AI readiness scorecard
- Top 5 opportunity ranking
- Human approval map
- 30-day roadmap
- Executive summary

Not included:

- Full automation build
- Production integrations
- CRM migration
- Custom software development
- Sending customer-facing AI messages without approval

## Timeline

5–7 business days from completed intake.

## Investment

$750 standard assessment.

$1,250 expanded assessment with deeper documentation review and readout.

## Optional next step

AI Productivity Sprint: $1,500–$5,000 depending on integrations and workflow complexity.

## Acceptance criteria

The engagement is complete when FlashFusion delivers the written report and readout covering score, map, ranked opportunities, and roadmap.
