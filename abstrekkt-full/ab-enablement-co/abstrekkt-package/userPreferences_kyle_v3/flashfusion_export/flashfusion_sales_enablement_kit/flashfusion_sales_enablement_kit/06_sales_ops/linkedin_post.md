# LinkedIn Post Draft

Most AI projects fail before anyone touches AI.

Not because the model is bad.
Not because the team is lazy.
Not because the business picked the wrong chatbot.

They fail because the workflow was already unclear.

The handoff lived in someone's head.
The CRM was half-filled.
The SOP was outdated.
The approval step was implied, not documented.
The data was scattered across inboxes, spreadsheets, drives, and chat threads.

AI does not fix that automatically.
It usually exposes it.

The best first step is not "build an AI agent."
The best first step is:

1. Map one real workflow.
2. Identify the bottlenecks.
3. Decide what stays human-approved.
4. Score readiness.
5. Build one measurable improvement.

That is what I’m packaging with FlashFusion: a fixed-scope AI Workflow Readiness Assessment for small teams that want practical AI without turning their business into a science project.

If you run a small business and want to know where AI could actually save time, I’m opening a few pilot assessment slots.
