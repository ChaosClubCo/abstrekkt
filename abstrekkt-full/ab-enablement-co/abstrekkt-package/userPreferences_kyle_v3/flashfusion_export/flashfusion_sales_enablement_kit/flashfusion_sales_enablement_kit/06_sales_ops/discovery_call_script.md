# AI Workflow Readiness Discovery Call Script

## Opening

"The goal today is not to sell you a giant AI system. It is to understand where work repeats, where information gets lost, and whether there is one workflow where AI could safely help."

## Questions

1. What workflow made you curious about AI in the first place?
2. Where does that workflow start?
3. Where does it end?
4. Who touches it?
5. Which tools are involved?
6. Where do delays or mistakes happen?
7. What information is usually missing?
8. What should never be automated without human approval?
9. What would make this worth fixing?
10. If we improved only one workflow in the next 30 days, which one would matter most?

## Close

"Based on what you shared, I would not recommend starting with a broad AI rollout. I would start with a readiness assessment around [workflow]. That gives you a map, score, ranked opportunities, and a 30-day roadmap before committing to implementation."
