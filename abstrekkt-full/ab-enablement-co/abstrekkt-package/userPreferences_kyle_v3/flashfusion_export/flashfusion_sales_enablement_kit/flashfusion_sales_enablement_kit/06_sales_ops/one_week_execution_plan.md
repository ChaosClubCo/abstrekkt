# One-Week FlashFusion Execution Plan

## Goal

Create 10 conversations, 2 discovery calls, and 1 paid assessment opportunity.

## Day 1

Finalize the assessment one-pager, sample report, and landing page copy.

## Day 2

Open the AI Productivity Sprint demo locally and rehearse the 5-minute talk track.

## Day 3

Send 5 personalized warm-contact messages.

## Day 4

Send 5 more personalized warm-contact messages.

## Day 5

Publish one LinkedIn post: "Most AI projects fail before anyone touches AI."

## Day 6

Follow up with anyone who engaged. Offer to send the sample report.

## Day 7

Review: messages sent, replies, calls booked, objections, and offer clarity. Tighten the copy based on what people actually respond to.
