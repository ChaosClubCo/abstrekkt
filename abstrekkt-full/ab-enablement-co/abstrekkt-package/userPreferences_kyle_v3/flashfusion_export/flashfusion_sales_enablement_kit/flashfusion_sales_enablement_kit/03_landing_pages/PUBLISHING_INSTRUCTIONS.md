# Landing Page Publishing Instructions

## Recommended first page

Publish this page first:

`03_landing_pages/ai-workflow-readiness-assessment.html`

Do not publish all five service pages at once unless your site navigation is already clean. The priority is one clear action: book or request an AI Workflow Readiness Assessment.

## Page order

1. AI Workflow Readiness Assessment
2. AI Productivity Sprint
3. AI Team Workshop
4. Internal Knowledge AI Package
5. AI Governance Starter Kit

## CTA rule

Every page should use one primary CTA:

> Request the AI Workflow Readiness Assessment

The Productivity Sprint and other offers should point back to the assessment unless the prospect is already qualified.

## Minimum edits before publishing

- Replace generic contact links with your booking link or email.
- Add your preferred service area, such as Lake County, Chicago metro, or remote SMB teams.
- Add “sample report available” if you are comfortable sharing the fictional HVAC report.
- Add one sentence making clear that implementation projects are scoped after the assessment.

## Simple site structure

Recommended navigation:

- Home
- AI Workflow Readiness Assessment
- Productivity Sprint
- Workshops
- Case Studies
- Contact

## Tracking

Track these manually at first:

- Page views
- CTA clicks or form submissions
- Discovery calls booked
- Assessments sold
- Implementation projects proposed
- Case studies created

Manual tracking is enough until you have real traffic.
