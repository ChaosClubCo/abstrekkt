# Sample Case Study — Fictional Example

# How a Local Service Business Turned Missed Follow-Ups into a Human-Approved AI Workflow

## Snapshot

Client type: Local home services business

Team size: 6–15 employees

Service delivered: AI Workflow Readiness Assessment + AI Productivity Sprint

Workflow: Lead intake and follow-up

Timeline: 3 weeks

Primary result: Same-day lead response became consistent and visible to the owner.

## The problem

The business received leads from web forms, phone calls, email, and social messages. Some leads were entered into the CRM, some stayed in the inbox, and some were followed up from memory. The owner knew opportunities were slipping but did not have a clear view of where or why.

## What FlashFusion found

The workflow was not broken because the team lacked effort. It was broken because intake rules, qualification notes, and follow-up steps were not standardized. AI could help only after the process was mapped and human approval boundaries were defined.

## What changed

FlashFusion designed a workflow where new leads are summarized, scored, structured into CRM-ready fields, and paired with a human-approved follow-up draft. The office manager still approves customer-facing messages and scheduling decisions.

## Results

- Faster response to high-priority leads.
- More consistent CRM notes.
- Clearer owner visibility into lead volume and status.
- Less repeated manual writing for follow-up messages.

## Why it worked

The project started with workflow readiness, not AI novelty. The team improved one process, measured the result, and kept sensitive decisions human-controlled.

## Reusable lesson

The best first AI workflow is usually the one the team already repeats every week.
