# Blindspot Register

Areas the project may be underestimating. Not necessarily surfaced in the KB — that's the point.

## BS-1 · Uninsured free engagements carry the same liability as paid ones — HIGH
Art and Ryan are free, but "free" does not remove professional-liability or IP exposure. An AI recommendation that harms a client's business is a claim risk whether or not money changed hands. The E&O gate is being treated as a *paid-contract* gate; it arguably should gate *any* delivery. [GENERATED — legal-adjacent reasoning; confirm with attorney, not a legal opinion]

## BS-2 · Employer IP claim is the real lane-separation threat — HIGH
Lane separation is framed culturally (don't name the firm, don't touch its clients), but the binding threat is *legal*: a broad IP-assignment clause in the employment agreement. This is the one place the day-job can reach into Abstrekkt. B-4 addresses it; until cleared, it's an unpriced risk. [SOURCED — llc kit] [GENERATED — elevation]

## BS-3 · Client-delivered AI agents = new duty of care — HIGH
The moment a client depends on an autonomous agent (HITL, approval queues, always-on), the org inherits monitoring, prompt-injection, model-fallback, and abuse-vector obligations it has no ops muscle for yet (no on-call, no alerting). The champion model's "exit the dependency" partly mitigates this — but only if the workflows are truly handed off, not hosted by Kyle. [GENERATED — from PRR + HITL skill posture]

## BS-4 · Case-study anonymity vs proof tension — MEDIUM
Art must stay anonymous; Ryan is the only named case study. A pipeline that needs "3 case studies to warm referrals" while most clients require anonymity may starve the proof engine. Plan for anonymized-but-specific case studies, or secure naming consent up front. [SOURCED — instructions, flashfusion-arc] [GENERATED — tension]

## BS-5 · Pricing floor vs first-client psychology — MEDIUM
Holding $4,500 "without flinching" on the *first ever* paid audit, with only one case study, is behaviorally hard. The risk is a quiet exception that breaks the model. The mitigation is the Ryan case study as proof + a scripted floor-hold, both of which exist. [SOURCED — flashfusion-arc]
