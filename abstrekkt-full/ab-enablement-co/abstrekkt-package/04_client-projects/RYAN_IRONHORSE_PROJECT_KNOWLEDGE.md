- **Company:** Iron Horse Seating (ironhorseseating.com)
- **Owner:** Ryan Anderskow
- **Business type:** Custom furniture and upholstery — made-to-order, craft-driven, direct-to-consumer and some trade/wholesale
- **AI baseline:** Ryan was already using Claude before this engagement. The goal is to systematize what works, not introduce AI from scratch.
- **Claude project scope:** Marketing copy, client communications, operational writing, and brand content. GUI/web only — no code, no new tools.
- **Champion model:** Ryan is the company-wide AI expert. This project is about building that capability so it doesn't need outside support.

## Brand voice and positioning

**What Iron Horse is:**
A craft business. Custom, made-to-order, built by hand. The work speaks for itself — the content job is to show the craft, not just the product.

**What Iron Horse is not:**
A catalog furniture retailer. A fast-turnaround operation. Generic.

**Tone in copy:**
- Confident in the craft, specific about materials and process
- Warm and direct with clients, never stiff or corporate
- On social: shows the work — the fabric, the challenge, the decision, the result
- On email: personal, clear, no filler

**Things to avoid in copy:**
- Generic phrases: "quality craftsmanship," "attention to detail," "passionate about furniture"
- Overselling or hype
- Vague timelines ("soon," "shortly") — Iron Horse clients care about lead time; be specific when known
- Corporate distance — these are real people buying custom furniture, often for a meaningful purpose

## Product / piece description formula

**Structure:** What it does for the buyer → Material specifics → Build detail → Customization note → CTA

**Example:**
> A corner sectional built for the room you actually have — not the showroom floor. This piece started with [CLIENT'S SPECIFIC SITUATION: a narrow wall, a particular angle]. The frame is [MATERIAL], upholstered in [FABRIC NAME + SHORT DESCRIPTION — e.g., "a midcentury-weight linen in warm slate"]. [ONE BUILD DETAIL that shows craft — e.g., "The corners are hand-tufted, not stapled."]. Every Iron Horse piece is made to order — [LEAD TIME or contact CTA].

**What to name in every description:**
- The specific fabric or material (never just "fabric" or "leather")
- One detail that shows how it's made
- Lead time or custom order path

## Quoting and scope language

### "What's included" baseline language
```
Standard custom build includes:
- [FRAME MATERIAL + CONSTRUCTION METHOD]
- [FOAM SPEC — density, comfort level]
- Fabric/upholstery of your choice from [SOURCE/OPTIONS]
- [FINISH OPTIONS]
- Lead time: approximately [RANGE] from deposit and material confirmation

What costs extra: [LIST — e.g., COM (customer's own material), oversize shipping, expedited timeline, specialty hardware, matching sets with high tolerance required]
```

### Deposit and terms language
```
A [PERCENTAGE/AMOUNT] deposit is required to hold your spot and begin material sourcing. The balance is due at [DELIVERY/PICKUP/COMPLETION]. 

Custom orders are non-refundable after materials are cut. Changes to spec after [POINT IN BUILD] may affect timeline and pricing — I'll always flag this before proceeding.
```
