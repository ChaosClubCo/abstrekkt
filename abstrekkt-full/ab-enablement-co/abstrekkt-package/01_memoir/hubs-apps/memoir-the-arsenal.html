The distinction the chapter must make

The tools are prosthetics. Every single one addresses a specific cognitive gap created by the injury. They are not optimization for a brain that works fine — they are compensation for a brain that doesn't. The chapter needs to make this distinction concrete: not "I use Notion to stay organized" but "I use Notion because if I don't, that information doesn't exist for me."

The tools aren't a productivity flex. They're a workaround for a system that can't be restored.
