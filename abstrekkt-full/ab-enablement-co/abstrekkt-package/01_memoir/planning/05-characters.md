KYLE (Narrator / Author)
Approaching 40. Self-taught engineer, no CS degree. Path: bartending at O'Toole's, quality control, self-taught programming, engineering, founder. Father to Kinsley (Friday custody). Based in Mundelein, Illinois.

Defining trait: The diagnostic instinct — reads rooms, processes, and systems with the same pattern-recognition skill. This instinct predates the injury, survives it, and becomes the foundation for reconstruction. At the bar: knowing which customer was about to start trouble before he moved. In code: diagnosing system failures before the error log catches up.

Internal contradiction: The systems-builder identity both enables and protects him. The tools and documentation are genuine survival infrastructure — but they also create distance from feeling. The book has to hold both truths simultaneously.

Memory: Genuinely unreliable. Can't pin down whether dependency started at 19 or 21. Some periods are inaccessible. The book acknowledges this rather than smoothing it over. Writing sometimes functions as a retrieval mechanism — material surfaces through the act of transcription.

Voice: Direct, metaphor-heavy, technically precise about emotions, strategically profane. Self-aware about the irony of systematizing everything, including self-examination. No performance of either vulnerability or toughness.


KINSLEY (Daughter)
The audience. The book is a letter to her — documenting reconstruction as evidence of commitment. She is the grounding constraint and motivation. What she sees now vs. what she'll understand when she's older is a recurring question.

Protected: Details about Kinsley stay on Kyle's terms. She exists as motivation and frame, not as a character to be developed or examined beyond what Kyle chooses to share.


THE COUCH FRIEND (Unnamed)
The person who said "You don't have to" when offering marijuana for the first time. Not peer pressure — permission. The psychological mechanism matters: Kyle doesn't break rules, he finds frameworks that allow him to. The first drug experience wasn't transgressive; it was permitted. This person may be composite or may be a single real individual. Memory is unclear. The scene is load-bearing regardless.


PRE-INJURY KYLE
A ghost in the book. The person who existed before cognitive disruption — who had organic memory, whose strategies worked, who didn't need external scaffolding. Not idealized. The question "What would pre-injury Kyle think of constructed Kyle?" runs through the text.


THE DIAGNOSTIC INSTINCT (Personified Thread)
Not a character but functions like one — the skill that survives every version of Kyle. Appears first at O'Toole's reading the room. Resurfaces in QC work. Adapts to engineering. The thread connecting all phases. The book's spine.
