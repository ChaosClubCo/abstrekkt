THE BREAK THROUGH
[Intro]

Pulse check—dopamine drip in the veins,
Click, spend, chase—different drug, same chain.
Cursor blinking like a trigger I train,
I see the pattern… before it takes aim.[Verse 1]

[Verse 1]

I wired my brain like a circuit addicted to sparks,
Every new tool a new jewel—yeah, I’m feeding the dark,
Cart full, heart null—start pullin’ threads from the seams,
Spent cash just to crash, chasing flashes of dreams.

(double-time)
Scroll, scroll—soul sold in increments,
Hit “buy now” just to numb what I’m dealing with,
AI got me high—new power, new dopamine,
Same ghost in the code, just dressed in a newer skin.

Pause—
Wait… I caught it mid-thought, didn’t fall through the floor,
That’s a different me—I never stopped it before.

[Pre-Chorus]

This ain’t relapse—it’s the system alert,
Red lights flashin’, but I stayed in the dirt,
Fed the context—didn’t spiral or split,
I felt the pull… but I didn’t submit.

[Hook]

I saw it coming—and I cut the wire
Same old flame but I killed the fire
I’m still wired wrong—but I fight that desire
For you… I don’t let it take me higher

[Verse 2]

Yeah, the hardware’s flawed—can’t swap out the soul,
Still feel the itch when the switch flips control,
But I built guardrails—rails holdin’ the weight,
Every urge hits the wall I designed for my fate.

I used to dive headfirst, blind in the rush,
Now I pause, audit thoughts, let the impulse get crushed,
Not perfect—still nervous, still flirt with the edge,
But I measure every step now instead of the ledge.

She’s the reason I breathe through the chaos I made,
Every choice recalibrated when I picture her face,
Not a saint—just a man who refuses to break,
I lost everything once… won’t repeat that mistake.

[Bridge] (Breakdown / Half-time)

Say it plain—no filter, no disguise,
I was lost in the high, let it eat me alive,
But I rewrote the code—line by line, no excuse,
Every failure owned—I got nothing to lose.

[Final Hook] (Variation)

I saw it coming—and I cut the wire
Tried to rise but I buried the fire
Still wired wrong—but I’m climbing higher
For you… I became something stronger

[Outro]

This ain’t recovery—it’s maintenance mode,
Every day I wake up, patching cracks in the code,
If you ever read this, just know it’s the truth—
I didn’t fix me… I fought me for you.
