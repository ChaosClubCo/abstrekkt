## The Problem

Your organization is adopting AI agents. Fast. But every new agent is a new risk surface:

- **Who authorized this agent to access customer data?**

Most AI platforms were built for productivity — not compliance. They give you capability with no audit trail.

**vs. doing nothing:** Every un-audited AI agent you deploy is a liability. Regulatory scrutiny of AI is accelerating. The question isn't whether to add controls — it's whether you add them before or after a finding.

## How It Works

**Step 1 — Configure:** Use the Builder to define your agent: what tools it can access, what it can't, what persona it presents, what guardrails apply.

**Step 2 — Deploy:** The Runtime executes every agent action inside an auditable envelope. Every call logged. Every decision traceable.

**Step 3 — Prove:** When your auditor asks, ComplianceReport generates a formatted, policy-mapped artifact in minutes. No manual documentation sprint.
