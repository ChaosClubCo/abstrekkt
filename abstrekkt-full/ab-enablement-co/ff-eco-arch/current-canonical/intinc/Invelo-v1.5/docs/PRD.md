# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - INT Inc. Website

## 1. Executive Summary
INT Inc. is a premier AI services company specializing in pragmatic, enterprise-grade AI solutions. The website serves as a professional digital presence to showcase their expertise, services, and track record (InVelo service line).

## 2. Target Audience
- Enterprise Decision Makers (CTOs, CISOs, VPs of Operations)
- IT Managers and Engineering Leads
- Potential Strategic Partners

## 3. Key Features & Functionality

### 3.1. Homepage
- **Hero Section**: High-impact visual (Quantum Scene) and clear value proposition.
- **Problem/Solution Narrative**: Clearly articulates the gap in current AI consulting and how INT Inc. fills it.
- **AI Services Overview**: Grid layout of core service offerings with icons and descriptions.
- **Catalyst Builds**: Showcasing specific, time-boxed products (SOC 2 Sprint, Onboarding Accelerator, etc.).
- **Social Proof**: Client testimonials from enterprise leaders.
- **Contact/Lead Gen**: A robust form for project inquiries with service-specific dropdowns.

### 3.2. About Page
- **Company Heritage**: Detailing 25 years of managed excellence.
- **Mission & Vision**: Focus on "Pragmatic AI" and demystifying technology.
- **Detailed AI Services**: Expanded view of service offerings.
- **Case Studies**: Interactive carousel showcasing real-world impact (Problem, Solution, Outcome).
- **Why INT Inc.**: Highlighting Heritage, Stability, and Exit Strategy.

## 4. User Experience (UX) Goals
- **Professionalism**: Clean, high-contrast design using a sophisticated palette (Navy, Gold, Teal).
- **Clarity**: Minimal jargon, focusing on business outcomes and "Mission Complete" status.
- **Responsiveness**: Seamless experience across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- **Performance**: Fast load times and smooth animations (Framer Motion).

## 5. Technical Requirements
- **Framework**: React 19 with TypeScript.
- **Routing**: Client-side routing via `react-router-dom`.
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS for utility-first design.
- **Icons**: Lucide-React for consistent iconography.
- **Animations**: Framer Motion for interactive elements (Carousels, fades).
- **3D Elements**: React Three Fiber for the "Quantum Scene" hero visuals.

## 6. Success Metrics
- Increased lead generation through the contact form.
- High engagement (time on site) on the About page case studies.
- Positive feedback on brand perception and professional clarity.
