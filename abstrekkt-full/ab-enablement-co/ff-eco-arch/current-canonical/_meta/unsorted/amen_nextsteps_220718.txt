Phase 2 and general brain health: 
- ORGANIZATION: Please work on structuring your days (ESPECIALLY unstructured time) like we 
discussed. Structure is KEY. SET MULTIPLE ALARMS/REMINDERS FOR: a fixed wake up time, fixed 
meal times, fixed exercise times, fixed times for medications/ supplements, with leaving other slots 
for chores, appointments, social things.  
 
- NUTRITION: FIXED MEAL TIMES: Set alarms.  
AT THE VERY LEAST: High protein, greens. Minimize carbs, and sugar and processed foods. Choose 
grass-fed meat.  
Please ensure adequate fluid intake, at least eight 8-ounce glasses, which equals about 2 liters, or half 
a gallon daily. This is called the 8 X 8 rule and is very easy to remember. 
 
- MEDITATION: Set an alarm. Fixed time (morning or winding-down before bed). 
Daily meditation for as little as 15 minutes a day, can show measurable increases in grey-matter in 
the frontal lobes and hippocampus, and reduced grey-matter in the amygdala (which correlates with 
less stress).  
The Headspace app also has 'SleepCasts' which are stories that help you drift into sleep. The 'Calm' 
app, the "10% Happier" app, and the 'breathe to relax' app are some of my favorites. See what you

- SLEEP: Let's work on getting 7.5 hours a night. (5 sleep cycles, each cycle is 90 minutes). 
7.30am: FIXED wake up time. Set an alarm AND GET OUT OF BED. If you tend to feel groggy in the 
morning, get some sunlight for 20 mins, have a glass of warm water, do deep breathing (30 full, slow, 
deep breaths) and jumping jacks or stretches. 
For DINNER, minimize fast carbs/ sugars. Sweet potatoes, carrots (slow carbs) and kombucha are ok.  
11pm: (1 hour before bedtime): Wind-down before bed (DIM LIGHTS, wear sunglasses indoors, 
scheduled worry time, scheduled to-do lists, nightly treasure hunt, meditation, boring reading, warm 
shower, sauna, hyperbaric oxygen, etc.)  
12am: OR LATER: Only get to bed when sleepy, if possible, sleep on your NON-DOMINANT SIDE.  
- When you are working late (till 3am), wake up at 7.30am the next day, and schedule a 3 hour nap 
that afternoon (instead of sleeping in). 
 
- EXERCISE: FIXED exercise TIME: Set an alarm. 
MODERATE intensity exercise for 30 mins, 5-7 times per week as a baseline. The simplest way, is to 
use a stationary bicycle or elliptical or treadmill, or even brisk walking, as if you are running late.  
 
- SUBSTANCE USE: need to be completely avoided/ minimized.
