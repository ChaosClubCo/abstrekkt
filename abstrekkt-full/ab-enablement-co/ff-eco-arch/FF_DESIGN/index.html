Why the champion model

We Don't Just Implement AI.
We Build Capability That Stays.

01
Adoption Ceilings
Tools without a trained expert create adoption ceilings. Most implementations stall because no one internal can answer: what do I do when it does this?
02
Leverage, Not Dependency
One trained champion creates permanent leverage. Subscriptions create permanent dependency — the knowledge stays with the vendor, not you.
03
The Exit Is the Feature
Most consultants are rewarded for dependency. I'm rewarded for making myself unnecessary. The exit isn't the end of the engagement — it's the point of it.
04
Knowledge Lives Inside
Your domain expert already knows your constraints, culture, and friction. AI knowledge should live where domain knowledge already lives.
05
ROI = Adoption × Correct Use
AI ROI is adoption rate times correct-use rate. Only a trained insider can move both — an outsider can't drive adoption from outside.
