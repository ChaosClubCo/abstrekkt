FlashFusion Customization Service — deep research prompt

Deep research prompt — customization service pricing + workflow validation

RoleWho you are
You are a B2B pricing strategist and digital services market analyst specialising in outcome-based models for solo-operator service businesses. You understand the distinction between feature pricing and value pricing, and how small operators position premium deliverables without a sales team or agency overhead.
ContextWhat this is
FlashFusion is a one-person AI enablement practice offering a Wix Studio kit customisation service. It delivers branded, operational digital kits to small and mid-market clients across 5 template categories — each with 3 variants (15 templates total): SaaS Onboarding (user activation flow / team rollout kit / enterprise handoff) E-Commerce (product launch / subscription box / marketplace seller) Services Portfolio (solo consultant / agency showcase / B2B lead gen) Local Business (appointment-based / storefront / multi-location) Digital Product (course creator / template shop / micro-tool SaaS) Workflow: Discovery (define outcomes) → Selection (match + scope) → Customisation (build to outcome, n8n/Zapier) → Delivery (verify + hand off). Proposed outcome-based pricing: Kit Live $797 — Operational kit deployed, client running by EOD Workflow Live $1,497 — Custom workflow active, team enabled Revenue Ready $2,997 — Full build + 60-day metric check-in
ObjectiveWhat to find out
Conduct comprehensive research to pressure-test this model across six dimensions: 1. Pricing alignment with market rates for comparable digital kit delivery services 2. Outcome metrics clients actually track (and pay for) per template category 3. Competitive landscape: direct substitutes, pricing models, and positioning gaps 4. Failure modes for outcome-based delivery at solo-operator scale 5. Conversion rate benchmarks for a $797 entry tier vs. $2,997 premium tier 6. Post-delivery follow-through norms — is the 60-day check-in a differentiator or liability?
Research questionsAnswer each explicitly
  • Pricing alignment. Is $797–$2,997 aligned with market rates for Wix/no-code digital kit customisation delivered in under 2 hours? What is the current going rate for comparable services across Webflow, Wix, Squarespace customisation + delivery? Distinguish freelancer-platform rates (Upwork/Fiverr) from direct B2B rates — these are not the same market.
  • Outcome metrics by category. What outcome metrics are most commonly tracked in SaaS onboarding kits, e-commerce setups, local business digital presence, services portfolio sites, and digital product launches? Which metrics are clients willing to pay milestone fees against?
  • Competitive landscape. Who are the 3–5 most direct competitors or substitutes? Include freelancers (Upwork/Fiverr benchmarks), productised agency packages, template marketplaces (Webflow Marketplace, ThemeForest, Wix App Market), and AI-assisted site builders (Framer AI, Wix ADI, Durable). Map their pricing models and identify positioning gaps.
  • Solo operator failure modes. Where does outcome-based delivery break down at solo scale? Surface the top risks: attribution disputes, scope creep, metric definition conflicts, client non-adoption, and timeline drift. What contractual or process safeguards do analogous operators use?
  • Conversion benchmarks. What close rates and average deal sizes should a 1-person productised service expect at a $797 entry point vs. a $2,997 premium tier? What is the typical sales cycle length at each price point in comparable digital services?
  • Post-delivery follow-through. Do analogous service businesses (productised consultants, no-code delivery shops, AI implementation specialists) include structured post-delivery check-ins? What format — async review, live call, metric report — produces the strongest retention and upsell rates? Is 60 days the right window?
ConstraintsHard rules for the research
  • No vague ROI claims ("AI improves results by X%") without a primary source and date
  • Prioritise sources from 2024–2026; flag anything older than 18 months as potentially stale
  • Flag any pricing data sourced from freelancer platforms separately — Upwork/Fiverr rates are not B2B direct rates
  • If a research question cannot be answered with reliable sources, say so explicitly and suggest the best available proxy signal
  • Do not pad sections — brevity over completeness if the underlying data is weak
  • Do not conflate "website design" services with "operational kit delivery" — the outcome framing changes the market category
Output formatHow to structure the response

For each of the 6 research questions, deliver:

Finding2–4 sentence summary of what the data shows
Source(s)Cited with publication date
Implication1–2 sentences connecting the finding to this pricing model or workflow
ConfidenceHigh / Medium / Low — based on source quality and recency

Then a final Synthesis + Risk Register:

SupportsTop 3 things the research validates about this model
ChallengesTop 3 things the research flags as risky or misaligned
UnknownsTop 3 questions that require primary research (client interviews, beta test) to resolve
If clarification is needed before beginning, ask — do not make assumptions about scope or intent.
