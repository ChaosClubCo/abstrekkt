Origin

The diagnostic instinct survives everything.

"I built all of this the same way I survived: alone, quietly, one thing at a time, not sure if any of it was good enough."

It started at O'Toole's — reading the room. Who's about to cause trouble. Who needs a drink before they ask. What the actual problem is underneath the stated one. That instinct didn't come from engineering school. It came from a bar at 2am.

It carried through quality control, into systems work, into AppSec and AI infrastructure. The thing that survived cognitive disruption was the ability to diagnose. The tools changed. The instinct didn't.

The 240+ tools and MCP configurations aren't a productivity flex. They're prosthetics. External cognition for a brain that was rebuilt from scratch and needed scaffolding to function.
