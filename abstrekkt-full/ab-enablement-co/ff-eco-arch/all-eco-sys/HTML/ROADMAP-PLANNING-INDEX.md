## **🚦 Decision Framework**

### **Monthly Review Checklist**

**Metrics to Track:**
- [ ] User growth vs. baseline (+/- 30% triggers scenario shift)
- [ ] MRR growth
- [ ] Burn rate & runway (min 12 months)
- [ ] NPS score (target >60)
- [ ] Agent success rate (target 85%+)
- [ ] LLM cost per PR (target <$2.50)

**Scenario Triggers:**

| If... | Then Switch To... |
|---|---|
| Q1 users > 150 (vs. 100 baseline) | High Growth Scenario |
| Q1 users < 75 (vs. 100 baseline) | Slow Growth Scenario |
| Major competitor launches | Competitive Disruption Plan |
| Runway < 6 months | Crisis Mode (reduce burn immediately) |

**Quarterly Reviews:**
1. **Financial:** CFO reviews burn rate, runway, ARR growth
2. **Technical:** CTO reviews tech debt score, infrastructure costs
3. **Product:** PM reviews NPS, activation rate, churn
4. **Strategic:** CEO reviews market position, competitive landscape
