## Cost Optimization

1. **Use Flash models** (10x cheaper than Pro)
2. **Enable caching** (60% savings on repeated prompts)
3. **Batch processing** (50% discount)
4. **Right-size outputs** (set max_output_tokens)
5. **Choose regions** (some regions cheaper)
