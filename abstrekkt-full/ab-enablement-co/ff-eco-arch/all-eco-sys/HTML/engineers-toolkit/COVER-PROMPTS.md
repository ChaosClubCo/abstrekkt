## For Midjourney / DALL-E / Ideogram:

"Minimalist tech product cover, dark background with subtle gradient
from deep navy to charcoal. A glowing geometric circuit pattern forms
the shape of a toolkit or toolbox. Clean sans-serif typography reads

Aspect ratio 3:2, high contrast."

## For Canva (simpler approach):

Use template: "Digital Product Mockup" or "Ebook Cover"
- Background: Dark navy/charcoal gradient (#0F172A to #1E293B)
- Accent color: Electric blue (#3B82F6) or teal (#14B8A6)
- Font: Inter or Space Grotesk (bold for title, regular for subtitle)
- Title: "The Engineer's Prompt Toolkit"
- Subtitle: "7 Prompts That Fix How You Use AI"
- Optional icon: Terminal cursor, circuit node, or minimal gear

## For 1:1 Thumbnail (Gumroad square):

Same design, tighter crop. Title should be readable at 200x200px.
Drop the subtitle for the square version — title only.
