PROMPT 02: THE SELF-HEALING OUTPUT
=====================================
Make Claude check its own work before you have to.

WHEN TO USE:
- Any time you need accurate, verifiable output
- Research, analysis, recommendations, or technical documentation
- When you can't afford hallucinations or unsourced claims

THE PROMPT:
-----------

After completing your response, add these three sections at the bottom:

CLAIMS: List every factual claim you made. For each, note whether it's:
  [VERIFIED] — you're confident this is accurate
  [UNVERIFIED] — plausible but you can't confirm
  [ASSUMPTION] — you assumed this; flag it

COUNTEREXAMPLES: Name 1-2 scenarios where your advice would be wrong
or where someone could reasonably disagree with your recommendation.

CONTRADICTIONS: If anything in your response contradicts something else
you said, or contradicts common practice, call it out explicitly.

WHY IT WORKS:
LLMs don't naturally audit themselves. This prompt forces a second pass
that catches the three most common failure modes: hallucinated facts,
narrow recommendations presented as universal, and internal inconsistencies.

The real value: you stop reading AI output with blind trust. The model
tells you exactly where to double-check.

VARIATIONS:
- Lightweight version: "End with a CONFIDENCE section rating your
  answer 1-10 and explaining what would change your rating."
- For code: "End with EDGE CASES: list 3 inputs that would break this code."
- For strategy: "End with RISKS: what could go wrong if we follow this plan?"

PRO TIP:
If Claude skips a section or marks everything [VERIFIED], push back:
"You marked 8 claims as verified. I need you to be honest — which 2-3
are you least certain about?" This forces genuine self-assessment.
