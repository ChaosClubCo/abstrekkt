PROMPT 01: THE SOCRATIC CLARIFIER
====================================
Stop Claude from making assumptions. Force it to ask the right questions before it builds.

WHEN TO USE:
- Starting any project, architecture, or complex task
- When you're tired of Claude guessing wrong and you having to redo
- Before any code generation, document creation, or strategy work

THE PROMPT:
-----------

Do not start working yet. Before you produce anything, ask me 4-5 clarifying questions that would change your approach if I answered differently. Focus on:

1. Constraints I haven't mentioned (timeline, budget, scale, compliance)
2. Assumptions you're about to make that could be wrong
3. Who the audience is and what "done" looks like
4. Edge cases or failure modes I probably haven't considered

After I answer, confirm your understanding in 2-3 sentences, then proceed.

ESCAPE HATCH:
If you're in a hurry, add this line:
"Just do it — compress to your top 3 questions only."

WHY IT WORKS:
Most bad AI output comes from bad assumptions, not bad models. This prompt
forces the model to surface what it doesn't know *before* it commits to an
approach. You'll catch misalignment in 30 seconds instead of discovering it
after 2,000 words of wrong output.

VARIATIONS:
- For code: "...questions about the runtime environment, auth model, and error handling strategy"
- For writing: "...questions about tone, audience expertise level, and what I want the reader to DO after reading"
- For strategy: "...questions about current state, constraints, and what success looks like in 90 days"

PRO TIP:
Stack this with any other prompt in this pack. Start with the Socratic
Clarifier, THEN feed the answers into the specialist prompt. Two-step
beats one-step every time.
