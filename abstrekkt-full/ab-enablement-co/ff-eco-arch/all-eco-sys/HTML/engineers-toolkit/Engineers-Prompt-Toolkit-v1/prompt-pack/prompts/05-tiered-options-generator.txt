PROMPT 05: THE TIERED OPTIONS GENERATOR
==========================================
Stop getting one-size-fits-all answers. Get options matched to your actual constraints.

WHEN TO USE:
- When you need a solution but aren't sure how deep to go
- Evaluating build vs. buy, quick fix vs. proper fix
- Any decision where time, budget, and quality trade off

THE PROMPT:
-----------

Give me three approaches to this problem, tiered by investment level:

OPTION A — QUICK (ships in hours, minimal effort):
What's the fastest path to a working solution? What do I sacrifice?
What breaks first? When would I outgrow this?

OPTION B — STANDARD (ships in days, solid foundation):
What's the balanced approach? What does "good enough for production"
look like? What's included vs. deferred?

OPTION C — COMPLETE (ships in weeks, built to last):
What's the full solution? What would a team with time and budget build?
What does this unlock that A and B don't?

For each option, include:
- Estimated time to implement
- Key tradeoffs (what you gain, what you lose)
- When to upgrade to the next tier
- One specific risk unique to this approach

End with: "Given [restate my constraints], I recommend Option [X] because [reason]."

WHY IT WORKS:
Most AI answers assume unlimited time and resources. This prompt forces
the model to think in constraints — the same way engineering managers,
consultants, and founders actually make decisions.

The real insight is usually in Option A vs. B. If the Quick option solves
80% of the problem in 10% of the time, you probably don't need Option C yet.

VARIATIONS:
- For hiring: "Option A: Contractor. Option B: Part-time hire. Option C: Full-time + process."
- For content: "Option A: Social post. Option B: Blog article. Option C: Comprehensive guide."
- For learning: "Option A: Watch one tutorial. Option B: Build a small project. Option C: Take a course + ship something real."

PRO TIP:
After picking an option, feed it back: "I'm going with Option B. Now give
me a step-by-step implementation plan for Option B only. Include the first
3 concrete actions I should take today."
