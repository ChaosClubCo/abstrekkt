# TOP 3 UNKNOWNS (BEFORE FIRST PITCH)

## 🚨 1. LEGAL REVIEW: Intinc Boundaries

### The Risk
Non-competes + IP agreements vary by company. Publishing "I'm a Staff Engineer at a 50-200 person tech company" might violate your employment contract depending on Intinc's specific wording.

### What You Need to Do
Get a tech employment lawyer (1 hour consultation, ~$200-300) to review:
1. **Can I publish about cognitive disruption + systems thinking while employed?**
2. **What product/incident details are completely off-limits?**
3. **Do you recommend legal review of the final draft before submission?**

### Cost of Skipping
- Cease-and-desist letter from Intinc legal
- Forced unpublication from all outlets
- Potential employment action

### Cost of Doing It
- $200-300 legal review
- Peace of mind
- Safe publication

## 🚨 2. RIGHTS NEGOTIATION STRATEGY: What's Your Endgame?

### The Question
Is this essay:
- **(a)** A one-off standalone publication?
- **(b)** The opening chapter of your book proposal?
- **(c)** An evergreen essay you republish everywhere possible?
- **(d)** A stepping stone to something else?

### Why It Matters
Different publications ask for different rights:
- **Atlantic:** First NA rights (standard)
- **The Sun:** Allows simultaneous submissions
- **Guernica:** First NA rights (standard)
- **Longreads:** Can republish with attribution
- **Substack:** Own the content entirely

### The Decision You Need to Make
**Option A (Recommended):** "This essay republishes everywhere possible" = maximizes reach + placement chance. Higher volume = higher likelihood someone picks it up.

**Option B:** "Exclusive first-publication to tier-1, then opens to tier-2/3 after publication" = creates scarcity, better for agents/publishers, lower placement volume.

**Option C:** "Customize essay per outlet" = different angle for literary vs. tech vs. systems outlets. High effort, highest relevance per placement.

### Why This Matters NOW
If you go with Option A and Atlantic rejects, you can immediately pitch Longreads with the same essay. If you go with Option B and Atlantic rejects, you're locked into waiting. Different strategy = different submission timeline.

**Recommendation:** Clarify this BEFORE first submission so you're not caught off-guard by exclusivity clauses.

---

## 🚨 3. PITCH TEMPLATE VARIATIONS: Different Angles for Different Outlets

### The Problem
The Atlantic wants "construction vs. recovery" framed philosophically.
MIT Technology Review wants "systems thinking for cognitive disruption" framed technically.
WIRED wants "unconventional engineer" framed culturally.
Axioms wants "AI + work" framed as business problem.

**Same essay. Different pitches.**

### What You Need to Create
**Master essay** (your current work) + **5 pitch variations:**

1. **Literary angle:** Recovery reframed as agency + construction. Philosophy + systems thinking. (Atlantic, Guernica, Sun)
2. **Tech culture angle:** Self-taught + diagnostic instinct + unconventional path. (WIRED, Slate, The Verge)
3. **Tech systems angle:** 240+ tools as cognitive architecture. Engineering problem-solving. (MIT TR, IEEE Spectrum, Protocol)
4. **Business/work angle:** AI + augmentation in knowledge work. How tools enable despite cognitive gaps. (Axios, TechCrunch, Protocol)
5. **AI/future angle:** What happens when human cognition meets systematic tool orchestration. (Futurism, Singularity Hub, Aeon)

### Why This Matters
- **Without it:** Generic pitches = low open rates. Editors see "another personal essay."
- **With it:** Each outlet sees why essay is perfect *specifically for them*. Opens = 20-30% higher (industry standard).

# FINAL STRATEGY SUMMARY

**Goal:** Saturate the market. 1 acceptance = success. Many acceptances = better.

**Week 1-2:** Hit all tier-1 simultaneously. Different submission routes = no conflict.

**Week 3+:** Blast tier-3. Volume plays a numbers game. 15 submissions = higher statistical chance of acceptances.

**Success metric:** *Minimum* 1 publication. *Target* 3-5 publications across tiers (gives you multiple credible bylines + reach).
