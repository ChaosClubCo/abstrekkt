    The Common Pattern

    Every system here resolves to one of three operating modes: augmentation (AI assists discrete tasks), automation (AI runs defined workflows), or orchestration (specialist agents coordinate end-to-end). 2026 has pulled the median from augmentation toward orchestration. The framing — Delegate, Review, Own — comes from CIO's analysis of how engineering teams actually scale agentic work without losing accountability.

    How to Read the Bars

    The amber depth bars show how much of the system's value depends on the AI layer. One bar = the framework works without AI; the agent is a force multiplier. Two bars = AI does meaningful work the human used to do. Three bars = the system collapses without the agents — they are the system.
