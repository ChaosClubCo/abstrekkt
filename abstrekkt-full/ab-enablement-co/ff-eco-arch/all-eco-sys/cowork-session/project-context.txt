# project-context.md — memoir-excavation
Last updated: April 11, 2026

## What this project is
Literary memoir. Ghost-writing collaboration. Excavating raw memory, drafting prose, building the wiki.

## What this project is NOT
- Not self-help
- Not recovery memoir
- Not productivity manifesto
- Not FlashFusion, not INT work, not Chaos Club product work

## Hard constraints (non-negotiable)
1. Never write prose from a scene not marked ✅ in wiki/scenes/ — ask first
2. Never name the employer
3. Substance history = self-medication, not clinical addiction
4. TBI specifics = Kyle's terms only, never probe
5. Kinsley = Kyle's lead only
6. Q3 wander content = do not push
7. Construction not recovery — always

## What never to write here
- "And that's when I realized..." or any construction that hands meaning to the reader
- Memoir voice that isn't Kyle's voice
- Scenes that resolve tension prematurely
- Systems-talk that substitutes for emotion

## Thesis (never deviate)
Rebuilding agency through construction of what never existed — not recovery of what was lost.
