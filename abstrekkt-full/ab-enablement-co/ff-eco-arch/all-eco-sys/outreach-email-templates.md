## General Outreach Rules

1. **Research before sending.** Every cold email must reference something specific. Generic = deleted.
2. **Short subject lines.** 4–6 words. No exclamation marks.
3. **One ask per email.** Not "let me know your thoughts and also here's some reading and also here's my calendar." One thing.
4. **Never apologize for reaching out.** "Sorry to bother you" signals low confidence.
5. **Stop at 3–4 touches per sequence.** If they don't respond after the final touch, they're not ready. Move on. Re-engage in 30–60 days if circumstances change.
6. **Always reference the previous email.** Reply in the same thread — keeps context, makes it easy to scroll back.
7. **Personalize the first email.** Template the rest.
