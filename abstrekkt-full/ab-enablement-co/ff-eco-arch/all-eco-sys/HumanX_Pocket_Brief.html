🏗️ 5 Core Architecture Validation Questions
▾

Ask every relevant vendor. Record answers for architecture decision doc.

  • Multi-Tenant Isolation: How do you enforce data boundaries between tenants? (Compare: AWS RLS/IAM vs. partition-based RBAC vs. dedicated vault)
  • Encryption Model: Customer-managed keys? Client-side or server-side? (Compare: envelope encryption vs. E2E)
  • AI Model Exposure: Does client data leave the vault to reach the model, or does the model come to the data?
  • Compliance Automation: Built-in audit logging, HIPAA/SOC2/CMMC readiness, compliance-by-design?
  • Integration Surface: Open standards (Parquet, Iceberg, REST APIs) vs. proprietary connectors?
