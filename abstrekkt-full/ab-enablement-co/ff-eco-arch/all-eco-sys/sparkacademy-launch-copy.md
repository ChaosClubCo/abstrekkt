- ❌ "Revolutionary" / "Game-changing" / "Transform" — every AI launch uses these; immediate slop signal
- ❌ "240+ tools" / inflated tool counts — violates content integrity rule
- ❌ "Staff Engineer" / inflated title — Kyle is L1 Service Desk + FlashFusion founder
- ❌ "AI is the future" or any tautological hype
- ❌ "Don't get left behind" — fear-based positioning, off-brand

## Risks Block

- **Premature launch:** Posting before flashfusion.co/spark is live results in dead links and lost momentum. Hard gate.
- **Over-claiming completion:** The launch announcement should not claim metrics that don't exist (e.g., "thousands of students"). Be honest about scale.
- **LinkedIn algorithm:** Posts with external links get throttled. Consider linking to a flashfusion.co landing page in the comments rather than the main post body.
- **Comment management:** Expect 2-5 "can you DM me details" responses. Have a templated reply ready that routes them to the catalog without becoming sales work.
- **Existing client conflicts:** The email to existing FF clients should never go to current Sprint/Audit prospects in negotiation. Send it AFTER any active deal closes, not during.
