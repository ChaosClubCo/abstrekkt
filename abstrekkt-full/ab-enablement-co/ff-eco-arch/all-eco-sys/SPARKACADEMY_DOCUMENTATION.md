## Platform Overview

SparkAcademy is FlashFusion's AI education platform for business owners, operators, team leads, and non-technical professionals. All content is practical, non-technical, and built around real business problems.

**Target audience:** Business owners, solo operators, service professionals, managers, compliance officers, IT staff, L&D leaders. NOT developers (except AI-Native System Architecture).

**Instructor:** Kyle Rosebrook — AI enablement practitioner, FlashFusion founder.
