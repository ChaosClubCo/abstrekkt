**The most profitable path for INT is to lead with Microsoft-native AI solutions — six of the ten recommended offerings run inside the M365/Azure stack clients already own — then layer in best-of-breed platforms (Vanta, Zendesk, PagerDuty, Writer, Flexera) where Microsoft has gaps.** This strategy minimizes client procurement friction, maximizes recurring CSP license revenue, and lets mid-level consultants deliver in 6–8 weeks without AI PhDs. The mid-market AI services opportunity is enormous: worldwide AI spending hits **$1.5 trillion in 2025** and **$2.52 trillion in 2026**, yet AI sits squarely in Gartner's "Trough of Disillusionment," meaning CIOs are abandoning science experiments and paying for guided, practical implementation — exactly what an MSP packages.
