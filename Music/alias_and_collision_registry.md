- `Twisted Words` and `One Percent of the Temporary` contain the same retrieved lyric body; keep both source-document titles.
- `Dear Dad`, `Please Dad I Need You`, and `Please Daddy I Need You` are duplicate source documents in the dad-future-letter lineage.
- `Lockdown / Memory Maze` was a recovery label; canonical retrieved filename is `LOCKDOWN MAZE`.
- `Congratulations (Everyone's Here)` is a specific partial version inside the broader `Congratulations / Front Row Seat` lineage.
- `Glasslungs & Haloes` is an alternate-version hypothesis related to `Little Blue Halos`, not a confirmed canonical rename.
