ChaosClubCo · Portfolio · v1

Content + software, under one org.

ChaosClubCo is the parent organization for content, media, and software products. No flagship product yet. No community layer. The org holds the work; each product stands on its own.

Pivot note (May 2026): ChaosClubCo was previously framed as a peer-support / mental-health platform. That direction was dropped. The brand voice carried forward; the therapy-adjacent positioning did not. All onboarding flows, crisis-routing infrastructure, premium-audio gating, and Firestore peer-support schema from prior iterations are deprecated.

What it is, what it isn't

What ChaosClubCo is

  • A parent organization holding multiple products
  • A GitHub org (50+ repositories)
  • A content + media + software brand
  • A vehicle for distribution decisions on built work
  • A distinct brand from FlashFusion and kylerosebrook.com

What it isn't

  • A peer-support platform
  • A mental-health or therapy-adjacent product
  • A community or membership product
  • A lyrics, audio, or music platform
  • A flagship product (none designated yet)

Brand voice

ChaosClubCo carries 14 brand pillars (pillar #13, the mental-health-specific one, was dropped in the May 2026 pivot). The voice survives the pivot; the framing doesn't. Pillars #6 ("Shout/whisper/feel"), #7 ("Some cycles meant broken"), and #14 ("No shame in chaos/climb") are flagged for review — they may read as mental-health-adjacent voice in a content+tools brand context and could need rewording.

The "Reading Room at Midnight" aesthetic is Kyle's content style, not a ChaosClubCo product description. Keep the two distinct.

What's next

  • Distribution decision for claude-code-plugins-plus. The work is built. Options: marketplace listing, blog launch, direct outreach to Claude Code users, partnership with Anthropic. Decision should be a separate artifact, not bundled with the org positioning.
  • Scope clarification for Ample and FissionPrompt. Both have repos and active code. Neither has a one-line description that explains who it's for and what it does. Without that, neither can be marketed.
  • Flagship decision (or explicit decision to stay flagship-less). Parent orgs without a flagship are fine for a portfolio. Parent orgs without a flagship are not fine when they need to explain themselves to the market. Decide which one this is.
  • Pillar review (specifically #6, #7, #14). Audit whether the residual mental-health-coded pillar language still fits the content+tools positioning.
  • Open ambiguity: Chaos Collective vs. ChaosClubCo. Memory flags an open question about whether "Chaos Collective" is a rebrand of ChaosClubCo or a separate publishing surface. Needs resolution before any external launches use either name.
