## 1. What this is

A **free** AI audit + profile build for Ryan / Iron Horse, provided as a **case-study trade** —
not a discount, not a favor, a defined exchange. Ryan gets the work at no cash cost; FlashFusion gets
a documented, referenceable case study and a warm path to paid services. This is **documented
exception #2** against the $4,500 audit floor. The floor still holds for everyone else.

## 2. What the trade includes (scope edges — hard)

- **Full-business AI audit**: a scan across Iron Horse's operations, sales, communications, and data
  workflows to identify where AI creates leverage. This is a *diagnostic* scan, not implementation.
- **Profile build**, three artifacts:
  1. AI **capability map** of the business.
  2. Prioritized **opportunity shortlist** (ranked, with rationale).
  3. **One GUI/web mockup** of the single top opportunity.
- Delivery format: web/GUI artifacts (consistent with FF hard rules).

## 3. What the trade does NOT include (exclusions — the part that protects the floor)

- No ongoing retainer, no implementation build, no support/maintenance beyond delivery.
- **No more than one (1) consolidated revision round** on the delivered profile.
  _(Recommended default — tightens the boundary given the wide audit scope. Anything beyond converts to paid.)_
- Only the **one** mockup in §2 is included — additional mockups, or building the mockup into a
  working system, is out of scope by default.
- Nothing that isn't named in §2 is in scope by default — silence means excluded.

## 4. What the case-study trade costs Ryan (the non-cash consideration)

- **Named case study**: FlashFusion may reference Iron Horse **by name, with logo, and with a short
  testimonial quote from Ryan.** _(Recommended default — full attribution is the fair return for a
  full-business audit done at no cash cost. Downgrade to named-no-quote, or anonymized, only if Ryan
  can't be named publicly.)_
- Reasonable access + timely responses so the audit can complete within the sprint window.

## 5. Conversion gate — what "this became paid" looks like

The trade converts to a paid engagement when **any** of these is true:
- Ryan requests work beyond §2 scope, or a revision past the §3 cap.
- Implementation/build is requested — including building the §2 mockup into a working system
  (audit → build is a new engagement at the floor).
- The relationship moves to ongoing/retained work.

At conversion, standard FlashFusion terms apply: **$4,500 floor** for audits, per-project for builds.
The free trade does not roll forward as a discount — it ends, and paid terms begin at full rate.
