Build for Asymmetry

Leverage tiny advantages to earn outsized outcomes.

Apr 30, 2025·8 min read·Systems

The Shape of Upside

Most outcomes are not symmetric. A small action can produce a disproportionate result, in either direction. The work is not to predict the future but to position yourself where being right pays far more than being wrong costs. Find the bets shaped like that, and the odds matter less than the structure.

Small Bets, Large Payoffs

The cheapest experiments often hold the largest hidden upside: a letter sent, a question asked, a thing made in public. Each costs little and most return nothing. But the few that hit return more than the rest combined. You don't need a high hit rate. You need enough attempts and a payoff that dwarfs the stake.

Cap the downside. Leave the upside uncapped. Then repeat until something compounds.

Protect the Downside

Asymmetry is destroyed by ruin. One catastrophic loss erases a hundred small wins, so the first rule is to survive. Size bets so that no single failure can take you out of the game. Boring on the downside is what buys you the freedom to be ambitious on the upside.

Designing for Luck

You cannot manufacture luck, but you can build a life with more surface area for it. Show your work. Keep optionality open. Stay in rooms where good things can happen. Over a long enough horizon, the person taking many cheap, survivable shots beats the one waiting for certainty.

Risk a little, often. Never risk the ability to keep playing.
