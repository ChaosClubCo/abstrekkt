## Workshop scope (current format)

**Format:** Free, non-credit, 3-session series — 60 minutes each
**Audience:** CLC students and staff, mixed AI experience levels
**Delivery:** In-person or hybrid
**Ask to CLC:** Room, willing students, scheduling support — no budget required for the initial run
**What the facilitator brings:** Curriculum, facilitation, all materials
**CLC benefit:** Co-branded community event, documented student outcomes, faculty upskilling, repeatable format with no internal overhead

**Session arc (working structure):**
1. Session 1 — What AI actually is and isn't (demystify, dispel anxiety, first prompt)
2. Session 2 — AI in your field (applied workflows for the jobs students are heading into)
3. Session 3 — Making it a habit (building a personal AI practice, evaluating AI claims critically)

---

## Audience considerations

**Mixed AI experience:** Some participants will have used ChatGPT. Some will never have run a prompt. Design for the skeptic and the curious, not the power user.

**Workforce framing:** These are students heading into careers. Frame everything around: "When you're at your job, here's how this applies." Not: "Here's a cool AI thing."

**Common fears to address directly:**
- "AI will take my job" → Address first, not last. The members who don't learn are the ones at risk.
- "I'm not technical" → You don't need to be. Demonstrate this immediately.
- "I'll do something wrong" → There's no wrong prompt. Everything is editable.
