SEED 2 — "Relief is the most addictive feeling of all"
Source: Emerged during Storyteller Pro couch scene draft — first use chapter.

Note: "Counting streetlights" = earliest data point in the book if real —
the moment the architecture was already under strain.
SEED 4 — The Couch Permission Scene
Source: "You don't have to." That was the permission I needed. Not pressure —
permission.

like permission rather than pressure?
Status options: [GROUNDED IN ACTUAL MEMORY] / [EMOTIONAL TRUTH, CONSTRUCTED SCENE] / [DIFFERENT — HERE'S WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED]
Note: The mechanism is what matters — not transgression or rebellion, but
permission. Maps to a broader pattern: Kyle doesn't break rules, he finds
frameworks that allow him to.
SEED 5 — The Monitor Reflection
Source: First day at Intinc — seeing your reflection in a dark screen before
