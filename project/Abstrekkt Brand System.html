§ 05, VOICE & LAYOUT

12-column grid, 40px desktop margins, 8px base unit. Strong left-aligned grids with right-aligned telemetry. Avoid centered layouts, archive data is rarely centered.

DO, EMBRACE ASYMMETRY

Left-align text; pin metadata to unconventional corners to break the grid.

DO, HIGH CONTRAST

Let display headers dwarf body text for an editorial, high-end feel.

DON'T, ROUND CORNERS

No radius on brutalist surfaces. It destroys the industrial weight.

DON'T, VIBRANT COLOR

Nothing outside the palette. For "success," reach for a desaturated metallic, never neon.
