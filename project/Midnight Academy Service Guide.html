Service Guide

The standard operating procedure for running a Midnight Academy engagement.

v3.2 · Current
Section 01

Operating Principles

Every engagement runs on a single conviction: tools absorb cognitive load so people can operate at the top of their judgment. The work is not to automate decisions, it is to clear the noise around them. Enablement succeeds where it expands capacity, never where it merely cuts headcount.

The Diagnostic InstinctBefore recommending an intervention, name the friction it removes and the judgment it protects. If you cannot state both in one sentence, you have not diagnosed the problem yet.
  • Augmentation over replacement, in every framing.
  • Demonstrated competence over recalled procedure.
  • Sustained gains over launch-day theater.
Section 02

Engagement Cadence

An engagement moves through five stages — Diagnose, Onboard, Enable, Certify, Sustain — tracked in the Enablement Flow. Stages may overlap, but none is skipped. The cohort owner is accountable for forward motion; the Accountability board records every commitment made along the way.

Weekly rhythm

  • Monday — pathway review; clear blocked steps before they compound.
  • Midweek — live pairing and shadow rotations during the Enable stage.
  • Friday — accountability check-in; log outcomes to the tracker.
Section 03

Directive Standards

System directives are authored in the Prompt Builder and must declare four components: Role, Objective, Constraints, and Output. A directive that omits constraints is not production-ready, no matter how capable the model. Treat the constraint block as the safety perimeter, not an afterthought.

Minimum barNo directive ships to a Tier 1 workflow without an explicit uncertainty clause and a named human checkpoint.
Section 04

Escalation & Human-in-the-Loop

Tier 1 critical processes require a Human-in-the-Loop checkpoint at every decision boundary. The model drafts; the human decides. When confidence is low or the source is ambiguous, the directive must surface that explicitly rather than resolve it silently. Escalate novel objections through the Objection Handler before improvising a response in front of a client.

Section 05

Closeout & Handoff

An engagement is not complete at certification — it is complete when the Sustain cadence is running without you. Closeout requires a documented refresh cycle, a named internal owner, and a scheduled quarterly re-score. Anything less is a launch, not an enablement.

  • Refresh cycle scheduled and assigned.
  • Internal owner briefed and accountable.
  • Quarterly capability re-score on the calendar.
