The Rain

2012 - A cleansing rhythm.

The drops hit the glass with a heavy, deliberate cadence. Not the frantic splatter of a sudden storm, but the steady, enduring wash that settles into the bones of the city. Outwardly, it washed the streets clean, erasing the dust of a long, dry summer. Inwardly, it demanded stillness.
