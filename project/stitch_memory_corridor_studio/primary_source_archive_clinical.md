Along with the decreased tracer activity in the occipital lobe and internal cerebellum, these findings may suggest a history of traumatic brain injury and an encephalopathic process. > This finding occurs when the brain, on the outside surface view, appears scalloped or irregular and not smooth. The 2020 blood finding of morphine is inconsistent with both of those self-reports. **This is a material discrepancy.** Three possible readings:

## 11. How to Use This Archive

1. **Verification layer.** Any line drafted for the memoir should be checkable against this archive. If a claim is not here and is not in `primary-source-archive.md`, it is inference and needs to be labeled as such.
2. **Contradiction ledger.** Section 8 is the primary resolution queue. Every contradiction needs a decision: which version is in the book, and how is the other version handled (named, flagged, omitted).
3. **Memoir voice rule.** The raw clinical language here (verbatim in 3.2, 3.4, 4.1, 5.1, 5.4) is often the highest-value material.
