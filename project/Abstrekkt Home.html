The Manifesto →
01

No masks.

You don't have to translate your situation into a cleaner version of itself to participate. Plain language. Real circumstances. The version of you that's actually here.

02

No baseline required.

Recovery assumes there's an earlier self to return to. For a lot of us, there isn't. Abstrekkt is for people building something instead of going back.

03

Construction, not recovery.

Honest tools. A community that gets it. Practical systems for the version of you that's actually here, made from non-standard materials.
