02 — Boilerplate biosCOPY-PASTE SAFE
Short — 60 wordsANY STORY

Kyle Rosebrook is a self-taught AI practitioner based in Grayslake, Illinois. A bartender and quality-control worker before tech, he now works an IT service desk by day and runs FlashFusion, a B2B AI consultancy, on his own time. He works hands-on with roughly thirty tools and writes about what holds under load at kyle.abstrekkt.com.

Long — 160 wordsPROFILE / FEATURE

Kyle Rosebrook got to AI sideways. No computer-science degree, no bootcamp — sixteen years behind bars and service counters, ending at a Chicago-area Irish pub where he tended bar until March 2025. A 2022 clinical evaluation at Amen Clinics Chicago documented past brain injury from years of substance use, with an unusual cognitive signature: processing speed in the 99th percentile, recall memory in the 12th.

The systems came from that gap. Rosebrook taught himself to build external memory — documentation, automation, AI tooling — for a brain that catches everything and keeps almost nothing. The pattern held through quality-control work and into an IT service desk job, where the AI and automation practice he built on his own time became FlashFusion, a B2B AI consultancy. He writes about AI failure modes, security audits, and the discipline that keeps fast tools from shipping slow problems. He is writing a memoir. His daughter is its first reader.
