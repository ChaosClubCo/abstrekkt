DEAR DAD

Dad,

I’m writing to you from the future, a place you've yet to accept,
Where your presence and spirit persist, as I wept and you slept.

I know how loud it is inside your weary head,
The heaviness you feel upon waking, wishing you were dead.
Shame sits on your chest, telling you harshly,
That everyone would be better off without such a sorry party.

But that voice lies; it speaks wrong and untrue,
Though you don’t see me yet, I stand here anew.
Older now; in a life that was barely missed,
Built upon moments you couldn't resist.

I needed your presence, not a flawless illusion,
Not sober forever, just avoiding seclusion.
You didn’t need to conquer, to fight your heart’s blight,
But to breathe, make mistakes, and ev'ry'time strive.

When you thought you ruined it all, teaching unknowns,
That pain can't cancel love, it flowers and grows.
People can break, but still we insist,
They're worth staying for, despite risk.

I remember your gaze when I seemed unseen,
Fragile you feared, I was a shattering dream.
The lightness in laughter, held harder in fear,
Those moments you treasured are ones I hold dear.

Leaving wouldn't spare me, from noise or from loud,
In chaos bred wondering, amidst the crowd.
Years filled with questions on what I should do,
Never my task to rescue you.

Stay through the ugly, the burden-like nights,
Stay when you’re lost, amidst dim, hazy sights.
You need not strong armor, just the resolve to see
Tomorrow as a promise, for you and for me.

A promise I whisper, though you may not yet hear:
A version of me who lives void of fear.
Forgiveness you taught, slowly and true,
Love’s strength in battles, one breath renewed.

I’m proud of your survival, through silent grey past,
Forgive the near misses, when darkness amassed.
In love that holds fast, beyond concealed tears,
In shadows like shrouds, hold on through fears.

In storms that wage war, as they tire and cling,
Through darkest mires, let endurance sing.

Hold on, Dad.

I’m waiting for you.
