It started at O'Toole's — reading the room. Who's about to cause trouble. What the actual problem is underneath the stated one. That instinct didn't come from engineering school. It came from a bar at 2am with no fallback.

It carried through quality control, into systems work, into AppSec and AI infrastructure. The thing that survived cognitive disruption was the ability to diagnose. The tools changed. The domain changed. The instinct didn't.

The MCP configurations and tool stacks aren't a productivity flex. They're prosthetics. External cognition for a brain that was rebuilt from scratch and needed scaffolding to function.

Writing a book about what that reconstruction actually looks like — for Kinsley, and for anyone who ever had to build instead of recover.

Developers

You've pasted code into ChatGPT and gotten garbage back. The difference isn't the model — it's the workflow. How you frame problems, which tools handle which tasks, how AI slots into your actual process without breaking it.

Gap: context architecture · MCP integration · debugging patterns

What changes for you

  • Context architecture that cuts hallucinations significantly
  • MCP servers connecting Claude directly to GitHub, Jira, your DB
  • Custom Claude Code skills that trigger on your own phrases
  • Debugging patterns for when the model confidently lies to you
Did you know?
MCP is the unlock
Model Context Protocol (Anthropic's open standard) lets Claude talk directly to your GitHub repos, Jira boards, Slack channels, and databases — no copy-paste, no context switching. Most devs don't know it exists. It's been live since late 2024.
Reduce hallucination
Asking Claude to respond in structured XML or JSON — with explicit schema — reduces confident wrong answers by a significant margin. Unstructured output prompts are where most engineering AI failures live. Structure the output, structure the thinking.
Custom skills
Claude Code supports installable SKILL.md files — reusable workflows that activate on specific trigger phrases. "/fix-tests", "/audit-security", "/decompose-task" — custom commands that know your codebase, your stack, and your conventions. Built once, used forever.
Context window use
Most devs dump their entire codebase into context and wonder why quality degrades. The move is surgical context — relevant files only, with a system prompt that explains the architecture. Smaller, targeted context outperforms massive dumps every time.
Gemini Code Assist
If your team is on Google Cloud, Gemini Code Assist has native repo indexing — it understands your entire codebase semantically, not just what you paste. It's the sleeper hit of the Google AI stack for engineering teams already in GCP.

Ops. Sales. Founders.

If your job involves thinking, writing, or deciding — AI can multiply your output right now, not someday. The setup is the difference between a fancy search engine and a system that runs parts of your week without you.

Gap: role profile · tool integrations · automation layer

What changes for you

  • Role-specific system prompt that frames every session automatically
  • Integration layer connecting AI to your actual tools, not hypothetical ones
  • Automated outputs on the repetitive work eating your calendar
  • One clear next action from every meeting, email thread, or decision
Did you know?
Second brain
n8n + Claude can build a "second brain" that ingests your meeting notes, CRM updates, and Slack messages — and answers questions about your pipeline in plain English. Not a chatbot. A queryable memory layer that knows everything you've worked on this quarter.
Sales acceleration
A properly structured system prompt for sales cuts proposal writing from 3 hours to under 25 minutes. The key isn't asking AI to "write a proposal" — it's pre-loading your ICP, past wins, objection library, and pricing logic so every output is already contextualized.
Gemini + Workspace
If your company runs on Google Workspace, Gemini reads your Docs, Sheets, Gmail, and Calendar live — no uploading, no pasting. It knows what's in your drive. For ops and founders already in the Google ecosystem, this is the fastest time-to-value AI setup available.
Reporting automation
Weekly status reports, pipeline summaries, board updates — these can be auto-generated from your existing data sources in under 4 hours of setup time. n8n pulls the data, Claude structures and writes it, you review and send. That's 2–3 hours back every week, forever.
Copilot for M365
If your team is on Microsoft 365, Copilot is already embedded in Word, Excel, Teams, and Outlook — most people have it and don't know it's on. The unlock is a custom organizational profile that tells Copilot who you are, what you're working on, and how you communicate.
