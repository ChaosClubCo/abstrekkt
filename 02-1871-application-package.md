## APPLICATION: COMPANY OVERVIEW

**Company name:** FlashFusion (under Abstrekkt)
**Website:** flashfusion.co
**Location:** Grayslake, IL (Chicago metro — north suburbs)
**Stage:** Early Stage (validated problem, paying clients, pre-scale)
**Category:** AI / Enterprise Software / Professional Services

**One-sentence description:**
```
FlashFusion trains one person inside an organization to become their AI expert, 
then exits — so the business owns the capability instead of renting it.
```

**Problem (100 words):**
```
Most small and mid-size businesses have bought AI tools they can't fully use. 
They've either hired consultants who installed something and disappeared, or watched 
internal experiments stall because no one owns the outcome. The result is wasted spend 
and growing anxiety about falling behind competitors who figured it out.

The real gap isn't tools — it's a lack of one person inside the organization who 
knows what to build, when to use it, and how to teach colleagues. That person rarely 
exists, and most businesses can't hire a full-time AI lead at this stage of their 
growth.
```

**Solution (100 words):**
```
FlashFusion identifies the best candidate inside a client's organization — typically 
a domain expert who is already trusted internally — and builds them into a functional 
AI champion over a 6–12 week engagement.

The champion learns to apply AI to their specific workflows, train colleagues, 
evaluate new tools, and maintain what's built. FlashFusion exits when they're capable, 
not when a contract expires.

The positioning: we don't install AI in your business — we install an AI expert in 
your team. Clients pay for the exit, not the dependency. That inversion is the 
product.
```

**Traction:**

• Active client: Real estate business owner + AI coach training other agents

  and structured learning paths for client organizations.
```

**Market:**
```
Target: SMBs and mid-market organizations (10–500 employees) in traditional verticals 
— real estate, manufacturing, hospitality, skilled trades, professional associations.

These organizations are universally behind on AI adoption, have domain expertise worth 
automating, and lack internal technical staff to lead the effort.

The AI consulting market is estimated at $15B+ and growing. FlashFusion targets the 
underserved segment: businesses too small for McKinsey, too complex for a Fiverr 
automation contractor.

## APPLICATION: AI INNOVATION LAB PITCH

*This section is for the AI Innovation Lab specifically — some membership tiers have a supplemental pitch form or interview.*

**What problem are you solving with AI? (200 words):**
```
The AI skills gap inside traditional businesses isn't a technology problem — it's 
a knowledge transfer problem. Tools exist. Training programs exist. What doesn't 
exist is a person inside the organization who can translate between "what AI can 
do" and "what this business actually needs."

FlashFusion solves this by embedding a trained AI champion inside each client 
organization. We select an existing domain expert, build their AI capability over 
6–12 weeks, and exit when they can operate independently.

The AI component of this work is sophisticated: we're deploying Claude API-based 
agents, n8n workflow orchestration, multi-step automation pipelines, and custom 
evaluation frameworks tailored to each client's vertical. A real estate champion 
needs different tools than a manufacturing operations champion.

What makes this an AI-first business: our delivery platform (SparkAcademy) is AI-
native, our audit tooling uses AI assessment scoring, and the champions we train 
use AI as their primary productivity layer. We're not teaching people to use a 
specific app — we're teaching them to think and build with AI as an operating system.
```

**What's your technical differentiation?**
```
Stack: Claude API (Anthropic), n8n, Supabase, React, Vercel, Cloudflare Workers, 
Base44 (rapid platform prototyping), PostHog (usage telemetry).

Two specific differentiators:

1. Champion model exit structure: unlike typical consulting that creates dependency, 
   FlashFusion's contracts are designed around the client achieving independence. 
   This requires the delivery framework to be teachable, not just executable.

2. Vertical-specific deployment: we don't deploy generic "AI for business" training. 
   We map AI capabilities to the actual workflows of the client's industry — real 
   estate CMA prep, manufacturing QC documentation, union member onboarding, 
   restaurant reservation and inventory management. The tech is general; the 
   application is surgical.
```
