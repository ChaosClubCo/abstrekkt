BLACKLIGHT FRACTURE

[Intro — low, controlled]

Blacklight truth…
Some lies only show when you burn them

[Verse 1 — dense, internal rhyme chains]

I was gaslightin myself, match-strike mentality
flashin past facts, maskin fractures in me

Stackin narratives, parrots of panic I paraded
till the static I crafted became what I created

Thread-count flex but the thread was tension
stitchin fiction, addiction to edited intention

Sewin illusions like fashion—fit for confusion
truth in the lining but cut for exclusion

I made you the villain, convenient position
so I could avoid my own part in the system

Pause

That’s on me

Pills in my mouth like I muted the proof
lines in my nose rewrote what was truth

[Pre-Chorus — slower, cracked delivery]

I bent what I saw just to match what I felt
turned mirrors to windows to blame someone else

[Hook — simple, repeatable]

I did it to myself — blacklight truth
burn every lie till it show the proof

I did it to myself — no excuse
now I live what I said when I face that truth

[Verse 2 — cadence switches, punchlines]

(Double-time)
Synapse snappin, adaptin to patterns of panic
stackin the habits, erratic, reactive, I had it

till habits attacked me and dragged me backwards

(Slow switch)
You said it happened
I said it didn’t

Not ‘cause you lied
I just wouldn’t admit it

I edited memory—mentally weaponized doubt
cut out the truth till the lie made sense in my mouth

(Lil Wayne-style flips)
Brain got a loom, I be weavin excuses
then wear ‘em in public like “look how the truth fit”

Spokes on a cycle, I spun till it stuck
kept runnin the loop till the loop fucked me up

[Bridge — tension build]

Say it exact
don’t remix it

[Breakdown — heavy, aggressive]

I HEARD WHAT YOU SAID — I JUST CHOSE TO TWIST IT
I BUILT MY OWN HELL — THEN SAID YOU ASSISTED

I LIED TO YOU
BUT WORSE — I LIED TO ME

[Verse 3 — accountability, stripped]

I avoided your eyes like they mirrored the worst
of the version of me I rehearsed in reverse

I stole from my future to fund my escape
then blamed the reflection for cracks in the frame

I said I loved you — I meant it in pieces
the sober parts drowned in reactive releases

You held up the truth like a light in the room
I broke every bulb just to stay in the gloom

That’s on me

[Final Hook — full release]

I did it to myself — blacklight truth
burn every lie till it show the proof
