# Historical Comparison Window

## Frozen rule

All five primary artifacts must come from the same pre-injury comparison window:

**January 1, 2015 through December 31, 2019, inclusive.**

The window uses the last five complete calendar years preceding the earliest plausible injury year so eligibility does not depend on resolving disputed injury chronology first.

## Inclusion

An artifact is eligible only when its best-supported creation/occurrence date falls inside the window, provenance authenticates ownership/participation, it is not a later reconstruction, and it was not selected for narrative usefulness.

If a supported date range crosses outside the window, the artifact is ineligible until independent evidence narrows it inside the window. Copy/upload/repository timestamps establish custody, not historical creation, unless independently shown to be original timestamps.

If a domain lacks an admissible artifact inside the window, record insufficient evidence. Do not independently widen that domain.
