# Canonical Artifact Precedence

Use the highest authenticated representation available before considering narrative usefulness:

1. Native original with contemporaneous metadata.
2. Bit-faithful original/provider export preserving source metadata.
3. Provider-generated archival representation preserving headers/timestamps/identity/content.
4. Contemporaneous capture with provenance.
5. Later faithful copy with established fidelity.
6. Verifiable transcription.
7. Authenticated excerpt/partial representation.
8. Reconstruction/recollection. This cannot govern blind scoring without explicit protocol amendment.

## Same-tier tie breakers

1. stronger source-native metadata;
2. stronger chain of custody;
3. earlier independently verifiable provenance event;
4. more complete preservation.

If unresolved: **PRECEDENCE TIE — NO CANONICAL VERSION SELECTED**.

Do not merge conflicting versions into a synthetic source. Preserve lower-tier/conflicting representations separately. Narrative usefulness, emotional intensity, thesis consistency, or expected STRANGER/CONTINUOUS result are forbidden tie-breakers.

Transformations such as OCR, redaction, transcription, normalization, enhancement, metadata extraction, or conversion remain derivatives and never replace the governing source.
